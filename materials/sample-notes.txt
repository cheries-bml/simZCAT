Subject-Verb Agreement — Cheat Sheet

1. She / He / It -> verb + s/es
   She goes / He likes / It works

2. I / You / We / They -> base verb
   I go / They like / We work

3. Team / group is usually singular in this course:
   The team, which WAS responsible...

4. Having + past participle:
   Having BEEN warned about the risks...

Tip: Find the real subject before choosing the verb.
