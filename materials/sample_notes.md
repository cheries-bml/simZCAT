# Grammar & Tense Reference

## Simple Present Tense
- **Formula:** `Subject (He/She/It) + Verb (base form + s/es)`
- **Example:** She **goes** to the gym every morning.

## Subject-Verb Agreement
- Collective nouns such as *team* or *committee* take a singular verb when acting as a single unit:
  - Correct: *The team, which **was** responsible, proceeded cautiously.*
