# Simple Present Tense — Quick Notes

## Formula
**Subject (He / She / It) + Verb + s / es**

## When to use it
- Habits and routines
- Facts and general truths

## Examples
- She **goes** to the gym every morning.
- He **goes** to school every day.
- She **likes** to read books.

## Common mistake
- She go ❌ → She **goes** ✅
