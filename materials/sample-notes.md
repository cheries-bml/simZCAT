# Simple Present Tense — Quick Notes

Use the simple present for habits and facts.

- I / You / We / They + base verb → *I go*
- He / She / It + verb + s/es → *She goes*

## Examples
- She **goes** to the gym every morning.
- He **likes** to read books.

## Watch out
- Don't use *going* for a habit without *am/is/are*.
- Third person singular needs *s/es*.
