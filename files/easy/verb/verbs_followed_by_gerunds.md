# 📘 Self-Study Guide: English Verbs Followed by Gerunds

**Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Kata Kerja Bahasa Inggris yang Harus Diikuti Gerund (Verb-ing)**

Welcome to your self-study guide on **English Verbs Followed by Gerunds**! 🌟 In English, when two verbs appear side by side, the second verb cannot always stay in its base form (*Verb 1*) or use *to + Verb 1*. Certain verbs strictly require the second verb to be a **Gerund (`Verb-ing`)**, which functions like a noun! Mastering these verb patterns will make your English sound fluent, natural, and grammatically accurate.

> 💡 **Core Idea (Ide Utama):** A **Gerund** is a verb ending in **-ing** (`swimming, reading, eating`) that acts like a **noun** (an activity or thing). Some English verbs **must** be followed directly by a **Gerund (`Verb-ing`)**, NOT an infinitive (`to + Verb 1`).
> *(**Gerund** adalah kata kerja berakhiran **-ing** (`swimming, reading, eating`) yang berfungsi seperti **kata benda** (suatu aktivitas atau hal). Beberapa kata kerja dalam bahasa Inggris **harus** diikuti langsung oleh **Gerund (`Verb-ing`)**, BUKAN infinitive (`to + Verb 1`).)*

---

## 🧠 Quick Rules & Tips Before You Memorize (Aturan & Tips Singkat)

1. **The Basic Pattern 🏗️** *(Pola Dasar)*
   * **Formula:** `Main Verb + Verb-ing (Gerund)`
   * **EN:** *I **enjoy reading** mystery novels.* ✅ *(NOT: I enjoy to read ❌)*
   * **ID:** *Saya **menikmati membaca** novel misteri.*

2. **Making the Gerund Negative 🚫** *(Membuat Gerund Menjadi Negatif)*
   * Put **not** right before the **Verb-ing**.
   * **Formula:** `Main Verb + not + Verb-ing`
   * **EN:** *She **regrets not studying** harder for the exam.*
   * **ID:** *Dia **menyesal tidak belajar** lebih giat untuk ujian itu.*

3. **All Tenses Apply to the First Verb ⏰** *(Semua Tense Berlaku pada Kata Kerja Pertama)*
   * The first verb changes according to the tense (*past, present, future*), while the **Gerund (`Verb-ing`)** always stays the same!
   * **Present:** *He **avoids eating** junk food.* ➡️ *(Dia menghindari makan makanan cepat saji.)*
   * **Past:** *He **avoided eating** junk food yesterday.* ➡️ *(Dia menghindari makan makanan cepat saji kemarin.)*
   * **Future:** *He **will avoid eating** junk food tomorrow.* ➡️ *(Dia akan menghindari makan makanan cepat saji besok.)*

## ⚠️ Watch Out! Verbs That Change Meaning (Hati-Hati! Kata Kerja yang Berubah Arti)

A few special verbs can take **either** a Gerund (`V-ing`) **or** an Infinitive (`to + V1`), **but their meanings change**! 🔍

* **Stop + `V-ing`** ➡️ berhenti melakukan suatu kebiasaan/aktivitas (*He stopped **smoking**.* = Dia berhenti merokok selamanya.)
  **Stop + `to V1`** ➡️ berhenti berjalan/melakukan sesuatu *untuk* melakukan hal lain (*He stopped **to smoke**.* = Dia berhenti berjalan untuk merokok.)
* **Remember / Forget + `V-ing`** ➡️ ingat/lupa tentang kejadian yang **sudah dilakukan di masa lalu** (*I remember **meeting** you in Manado.* = Saya ingat pernah bertemu kamu di Manado.)
  **Remember / Forget + `to V1`** ➡️ ingat/lupa untuk melakukan **tugas yang harus dikerjakan** (*Remember **to lock** the door!* = Ingat untuk mengunci pintu!)

### Part A: Multiple Choice (Pilihan Ganda)

   a) eating ➡️ b) to eat ➡️ c) eat ➡️ d) ate

6. He couldn't help _______ (smile) when he read the congratulatory message.
7. The heavy rain necessitated _______ (postpone) the football match.
8. She regrets not _______ (learn) how to swim when she was younger.

### Part C: Translate & Complete (Terjemahkan & Lengkapi)

### ✅ Answer Key & Explanations (Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan)

1. **c) deleting** ➡️ **Admit** must be followed by a gerund (`Verb-ing`). *(Dia mengakui menghapus file rahasia itu secara tidak sengaja.)*
2. **b) turning down** ➡️ **Mind** is always followed by `Verb-ing`. *(Apakah kamu keberatan mengecilkan AC-nya? Udara mulai dingin.)*
3. **a) eating** ➡️ Phrasal verb **give up** is followed by `Verb-ing`. *(Saudara laki-laki saya memutuskan berhenti makan makanan cepat saji agar tetap sehat.)*
4. **c) being** ➡️ **Deny** + `Verb-ing` (here using `being` as the gerund of *be*). *(Tersangka menyangkal berada di dekat bank itu malam tersebut.)*
5. **buying** ➡️ `Consider + buying`. *(Kami sedang mempertimbangkan membeli rumah kecil di dekat pantai.)*
6. **smiling** ➡️ `Couldn't help + smiling`. *(Dia tidak bisa menahan senyum saat membaca pesan ucapan selamat itu.)*
7. **postponing** ➡️ `Necessitate + postponing`. *(Hujan lebat itu mengharuskan penundaan pertandingan sepak bola tersebut.)*
8. **learning** ➡️ Negative gerund: `not + learning`. *(Dia menyesal tidak belajar cara berenang ketika masih muda.)*
9. **EN:** *I really enjoy reading books in the morning.* (`enjoy + reading`)
10. **EN:** *She avoids talking about that problem.* (`avoid + talking`)
11. **EN:** *Please keep practicing writing in English!* (`keep + practicing + writing`)
12. **ID:** *Saksi tersebut mengingat kembali melihat sebuah mobil biru terparkir di luar gedung.*
