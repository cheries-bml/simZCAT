# 📘 Self-Study Guide: English Verbs and Their Prepositions

**Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Kata Kerja Bahasa Inggris dan Preposisinya (Dependent Prepositions)**

Welcome to your self-study guide on **English Verbs and Their Prepositions**! 🌟 In English, many verbs need a specific preposition (*in, on, at, for, to, with, about, from, of*) to connect naturally to an object. Learning a verb together with its preposition as a single "chunk" will make your English sound much more natural and accurate!

> 💡 **Core Idea (Ide Utama):** Certain English verbs always pair with specific prepositions (**dependent prepositions**). Changing the preposition can make the sentence grammatically incorrect or completely change its meaning!
> *(Kata kerja tertentu dalam bahasa Inggris selalu berpasangan dengan preposisi khusus (**dependent prepositions**). Mengubah preposisinya dapat membuat kalimat menjadi salah secara tata bahasa atau mengubah artinya sepenuhnya!)*

---

## 🧠 Quick Tips Before You Memorize (Tips Singkat Sebelum Menghafal)

1. **Preposition + Verb-ing 🔄** *(Preposisi diikuti Kata Kerja -ing)*
   * Whenever a verb comes directly after a preposition, it **must** be in the **-ing form (Gerund)**.
   * **EN:** *She apologized **for arriving** late.* (Not *for arrive* ❌)
   * **ID:** *Dia meminta maaf **karena datang** terlambat.*

2. **Same Verb, Different Preposition ➡️ Different Meaning! 🔀** *(Kata kerja sama, preposisi berbeda ➡️ arti berbeda!)*
   * **Agree with** *(someone)* ➡️ setuju dengan seseorang (*I agree **with** you.*)
   * **Agree on** *(a plan/topic)* ➡️ sepakat mengenai suatu hal (*We agreed **on** the price.*)
   * **Look at** ➡️ melihat ke arah (*Look **at** that bird!*)
   * **Look for** ➡️ mencari (*I am looking **for** my keys.*)
   * **Look after** ➡️ merawat / menjaga (*She looks **after** her little brother.*)

### ✅ Answer Key & Explanations (Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan)

1. **b) on** ➡️ **Rely on** means *mengandalkan*.
2. **c) for** ➡️ **Apologize for** + Verb-ing means *meminta maaf karena melakukan sesuatu*.
3. **d) to** ➡️ **Belong to** means *milik / kepunyaan*.
4. **a) on** ➡️ **Concentrate on** means *berkonsentrasi pada*.
5. **c) in** ➡️ **Succeed in** + Verb-ing means *berhasil dalam melakukan sesuatu*.
6. **with** ➡️ **Comply with** means *mematuhi / tunduk pada aturan*.
7. **on** ➡️ **Elaborate on** means *menjelaskan lebih rinci tentang*.
8. **from** ➡️ **Deter (someone) from** + Verb-ing means *menghalangi seseorang untuk melakukan sesuatu*.
9. **to** ➡️ **Attribute (X) to (Y)** means *menganggap X disebabkan oleh Y*.
10. **from** ➡️ **Refrain from** + Verb-ing means *menahan diri dari / menghindari*.
11. **EN:** *Don't worry, I will **pay for** our lunch today.* (`pay for` = membayar untuk).
12. **EN:** *The meal **consists of** rice, grilled fish, and fresh vegetables.* (`consist of` = terdiri dari).
