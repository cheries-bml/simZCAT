# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Present Perfect Continuous Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Present Perfect Continuous Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to say *"I **have been studying** English for two hours"* or *"Look at the wet ground—it **has been raining**"*? That is the **Present Perfect Continuous Tense** (also known as the *Present Perfect Progressive*)!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin mengatakan "I **have been studying** English for two hours" atau "Look at the wet ground—it **has been raining**"? Itulah **Present Perfect Continuous Tense**—juga dikenal sebagai *Present Perfect Progressive*!)*

This tense acts as a **bridge between the past and the present**. It focuses on an **ongoing action or activity** that started in the past and either is still happening right now or just stopped a moment ago with visible results today.

*(Tense ini berfungsi sebagai **jembatan antara masa lalu dan masa kini**. Fokusnya adalah pada **tindakan atau aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung** yang dimulai di masa lalu dan masih terjadi hingga sekarang, atau baru saja berhenti dengan hasil yang masih terlihat saat ini.)*

---

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Present Perfect Continuous

**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Present Perfect Continuous)**

There are **two main situations** where native speakers use this tense:

*(Ada **dua situasi utama** ketika penutur asli menggunakan tense ini:)*

1. **Action Started in the Past ➡️ Still Continuing Now (Fokus pada Durasi / Lamanya Proses):**
   * *Example:* She **has been working** at this company **since 2022**. *(She started in 2022 and still works here today.)*
   * *(Dia **sudah bekerja** di perusahaan ini sejak 2022—dan sampai sekarang masih bekerja di sana.)*

2. **Action Recently Stopped ➡️ Visible Evidence / Result Right Now (Aksi Baru Saja Berhenti dengan Bukti Sekarang):**
   * *Example:* You look exhausted! **Have you been running**? *(You stopped running just now, and you are sweating/panting right now.)*
   * *(Kamu terlihat sangat kelelahan! **Apakah kamu habis berlari**?)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Am I emphasizing **how long** the activity has been going on, or the **temporary ongoing effort** itself?"* If yes, use **`have/has been + V-ing`**!
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah saya sedang menekankan **berapa lama** aktivitas ini berlangsung, atau **proses aktivitasnya** itu sendiri?" Jika ya, gunakan **`have/has been + V-ing`**!)*

## 2. Sentence Formulas & Conjugation Rules

**(Rumus Kalimat & Aturan Perubahan Kata Kerja)**

Remember the subject rule for **`have`** vs. **`has`**:
* Use **`have`** for: **I, You, We, They**, and plural nouns (*the students*).
* Use **`has`** for: **He, She, It**, and singular/uncountable nouns (*Rina, the rain*).

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + have / has + been + V-ing` | *I **have been reading** this novel all morning.* *(Saya sudah membaca novel ini sepanjang pagi.)* |
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + have / has + not + been + V-ing` *(haven't / hasn't)* | *He **hasn't been feeling** well lately.* *(Dia merasa kurang enak badan akhir-akhir ini.)* |
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Have / Has + Subject + been + V-ing?` | ***Have** you **been waiting** long?* *(Apakah kamu sudah menunggu lama?)* |
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + have / has + Subject + been + V-ing?` | *What **have** you **been doing** since noon?* *(Apa saja yang sudah kamu lakukan sejak siang tadi?)* |

## 3. Signal Words: `For`, `Since`, `Lately`, `Recently`, `All Day`

**(Kata Penanda Waktu yang Sering Muncul)**

### A. `For` vs. `Since` (Sangat Penting!)
* **`For` + Duration of Time (Durasi / Lamanya Waktu):** *for ten minutes, for two hours, for three weeks, for ages*
  * ➡️ We **have been practicing** for two hours. *(Kita sudah berlatih selama dua jam.)*
* **`Since` + Starting Point in Time (Titik Awal Waktu):** *since 8:00 AM, since Monday, since last year, since I moved here*
  * ➡️ She **has been learning** Japanese since January. *(Dia sudah belajar bahasa Jepang sejak Januari.)*

### B. Other Common Time Markers (Penanda Waktu Lainnya)
* **`Lately` / `Recently` (Akhir-akhir ini / Belakangan ini):**
  * ➡️ They **have been exercising** a lot recently. *(Mereka sering berolahraga belakangan ini.)*
* **`All day` / `All morning` / `All week` (Sepanjang hari / pagi / minggu):**
  * ➡️ It **has been snowing** all morning. *(Salju sudah turun sepanjang pagi.)*
* **`How long...?` (Sudah berapa lama...?):**
  * ➡️ **How long have** you **been living** in Jakarta? *(Sudah berapa lama kamu tinggal di Jakarta?)*

## 4. Present Perfect Continuous vs. Present Perfect Simple

**(Perbandingan: Present Perfect Continuous vs. Present Perfect Simple)**

Learners often ask: *"Should I say `I have read` or `I have been reading`?"* Use this simple comparison:

*(Pembelajar sering bertanya: "Sebaiknya saya pakai `I have read` atau `I have been reading`?" Gunakan perbandingan sederhana ini:)*

| Feature (Aspek) | Present Perfect Continuous (`have/has been + V-ing`) | Present Perfect Simple (`have/has + V3`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Focus (Fokus Utama)** | **The ongoing activity / duration** *(Proses & durasi aktivitas)* | **The completed result / quantity** *(Hasil akhir yang selesai / jumlah)* |
| **Example 1 (Reading)** | *I **have been reading** this book for two hours.* *(Still reading; focus on time spent)* | *I **have read** 50 pages so far.* *(Focus on how much is completed)* |
| **Example 2 (Painting)** | *She **has been painting** the bedroom.* *(Her hands have paint on them; maybe not finished yet)* | *She **has painted** the bedroom.* *(The job is completely finished!)* |
| **Stative Verbs (Kata Kerja Keadaan)** | ❌ **Do NOT use** (*know, like, own, believe*) | ✅ **Always use** (*I **have known** him for years.*) |

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context

**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Continuing Action (`for`):**
   ➡️ My brother **has been playing** video games **for three hours straight**.
   *(Adik laki-laki saya sudah bermain gim video selama tiga jam berturut-turut.)*

2. **Continuing Action (`since`):**
   ➡️ I **have been working** on this presentation **since 9:00 AM**.
   *(Saya sudah mengerjakan presentasi ini sejak pukul 9 pagi.)*

3. **Visible Result in the Present (Bukti Terlihat Sekarang):**
   ➡️ Why are your clothes covered in flour? — **I've been baking** cookies!
   *(Mengapa bajumu penuh tepung? — Saya habis memanggang kue kering!)*

4. **Temporary Recent Habit (`lately`):**
   ➡️ She **has been drinking** more water **lately** to stay hydrated.
   *(Dia lebih banyak minum air putih akhir-akhir ini agar tetap terhidrasi.)*

5. **Negative Form (`haven't / hasn't been`):**
   ➡️ We **haven't been sleeping** well because of the street noise this week.
   *(Kami tidak tidur nyenyak minggu ini karena kebisingan jalan.)*

6. **Question Form (`How long...`):**
   ➡️ **How long has** your friend **been waiting** at the station?
   *(Sudah berapa lama temanmu menunggu di stasiun?)*

## 6. Crucial Traps & Spelling Rules to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting & Aturan Ejaan yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using Stative Verbs in Continuous Form.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai kata kerja keadaan/perasaan dalam bentuk `-ing`.)*
  Verbs like **`know`, `like`, `love`, `hate`, `want`, `need`, `own`, `understand`, `believe`** describe states, not physical actions. Use **Present Perfect Simple** instead!
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I **have been knowing** Sarah since high school.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I **have known** Sarah since high school. *(Saya sudah mengenal Sarah sejak SMA.)*

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Forgetting `been` (`have + V-ing` ❌).**
  *(Jebakan 2: Lupa menyertakan kata `been`.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She **has studying** for two hours.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She **has been studying** for two hours.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `for` and `since`.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Tertukar antara `for` dan `since`.)*
  * Use **`for`** + number/duration (*for 5 days, for a long time*).
  * Use **`since`** + specific clock time, day, date, or past event (*since Friday, since 2020, since we met*).

* 📝 **Quick `V-ing` Spelling Reminders:**
  * Drop silent **`-e`**: *write* ➡️ **writing**, *make* ➡️ **making**
  * Double final consonant (CVC short vowel): *run* ➡️ **running**, *sit* ➡️ **sitting**, *plan* ➡️ **planning**
  * Change **`-ie`** to **`-y`**: *lie* ➡️ **lying**, *die* ➡️ **dying**

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. Look at the puddles on the street! It ______________ (rain / has rained / has been raining) all night.
   *(Lihat genangan air di jalan! Hujan sudah turun sepanjang malam.)*

2. They ______________ (have been building / have built / are building) that new bridge **since** last year, and it still isn't finished.
   *(Mereka sudah membangun jembatan baru itu sejak tahun lalu, dan sampai sekarang belum selesai.)*

3. How long ______________ (have you been learning / do you learn / have you learned) to play the guitar?
   *(Sudah berapa lama kamu belajar bermain gitar?)*

4. My eyes are tired because I ______________ (have been staring / stared / am staring) at my laptop screen **for** six hours.
   *(Mata saya lelah karena saya sudah menatap layar laptop selama enam jam.)*

5. We have been living in this neighborhood ______________ (for / since / during) 2019.
   *(Kami sudah tinggal di lingkungan ini sejak tahun 2019.)*

6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*
   * A) I have been owning this bicycle for three years.
   * B) I have owned this bicycle for three years.
   * C) I have been own this bicycle for three years.

7. She ______________ (hasn't been feeling / hasn't felt / isn't feeling) very energetic lately, so she decided to rest this weekend.
   *(Dia merasa kurang berenergi akhir-akhir ini, jadi dia memutuskan untuk beristirahat akhir pekan ini.)*

8. "Why are you out of breath?" — "I ______________ (have been jogging / jogged / have jogged) in the park!"
   *("Mengapa napasmu terengah-engah?" — "Saya baru saja joging di taman!")*

---

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **has been raining**
   ➡️ Aktivitas hujan berlangsung sepanjang malam (`all night`) dan bukti nyatanya terlihat sekarang (genangan air di jalan).
2. **have been building**
   ➡️ Dimulai sejak tahun lalu (`since last year`) dan **masih berlangsung** sampai sekarang (belum selesai).
3. **have you been learning**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan durasi menggunakan `How long + have + subjek + been + V-ing`.
4. **have been staring**
   ➡️ Fokus pada durasi (`for six hours`) dan efek yang terasa sekarang (mata lelah).
5. **since**
   ➡️ `2019` adalah titik awal waktu spesifik, sehingga menggunakan **`since`** (bukan `for`).
6. **B) I have owned this bicycle for three years.**
   ➡️ `Own` adalah *stative verb* (kepemilikan), sehingga tidak dipakai dalam bentuk continuous (`have been owning` ❌); gunakan Present Perfect Simple (`have owned`).
7. **hasn't been feeling**
   ➡️ Menjelaskan kondisi sementara yang berlangsung akhir-akhir ini (`lately`).
8. **have been jogging**
   ➡️ Aktivitas yang baru saja berhenti dengan bukti langsung saat ini (napas terengah-engah).
