# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Future Continuous Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Future Continuous Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to describe what you will be in the middle of doing at a specific time tomorrow—like *"This time tomorrow, I **will be flying** to Jakarta"* or *"Don't call at 8:00 PM because I **will be studying** for my exam"*? That is the **Future Continuous Tense** (also called the *Future Progressive Tense*)!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin menggambarkan apa yang akan sedang kamu lakukan pada waktu tertentu besok—seperti "This time tomorrow, I **will be flying** to Jakarta" atau "Don't call at 8:00 PM because I **will be studying** for my exam"? Itulah **Future Continuous Tense**—juga disebut *Future Progressive Tense*!)*

Think of the Future Continuous as **projecting yourself into a future moment** and showing an activity **in progress** at that time—not just a quick action that starts and finishes right on the dot.

*(Anggap saja Future Continuous seperti **memproyeksikan dirimu ke sebuah momen di masa depan** dan menunjukkan aktivitas yang **sedang berlangsung** pada saat itu—bukan sekadar tindakan singkat yang mulai dan selesai tepat di detik tersebut.)*

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Future Continuous

**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Future Continuous)**

Use the Future Continuous Tense in **four main situations**:

*(Gunakan Future Continuous Tense dalam **empat situasi utama**:)*

1. **An Action in Progress at a Specific Time in the Future (Aksi yang Sedang Berlangsung pada Waktu Tertentu di Masa Depan):**

   * *Example:* At 9:00 AM tomorrow, she **will be sitting** in a job interview.

   * *(Besok pukul 09.00 pagi, dia **akan sedang duduk** di sesi wawancara kerja.)*

2. **A Longer Future Action Interrupted by a Shorter Future Action (Aksi Masa Depan yang Lebih Panjang Disela Aksi Singkat):**

   * *Example:* I **will be sleeping** **when** you arrive home tonight.

   * *(Saya **akan sedang tidur** **ketika** kamu tiba di rumah malam ini.)*

3. **Asking Politely About Someone's Plans Without Pressure (Menanyakan Rencana Seseorang dengan Sopan Tanpa Memaksa):**

   * *Example:* **Will** you **be using** your laptop later this evening? *(Polite way to ask if you can borrow it!)*

   * *(**Apakah** kamu **akan sedang memakai** laptopmu nanti malam? — Cara sopan menanyakan apakah boleh meminjamnya!)*

4. **Expected / Natural Future Events (Kejadian Masa Depan yang Wajar atau Sudah Terjadi Secara Alami):**

   * *Example:* Don't worry about Budi—he **will be arriving** soon, just like every Friday.

   * *(Jangan khawatir soal Budi—dia **akan segera tiba**, seperti setiap hari Jumat.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"At that future time, will the action **already be underway (in the middle of happening)**?"* If yes, use **`will be + V-ing`** (or **`am/is/are going to be + V-ing`**)!
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Pada waktu di masa depan tersebut, apakah tindakannya **sudah berjalan / sedang di tengah proses**?" Jika ya, gunakan **`will be + V-ing`** atau **`am/is/are going to be + V-ing`**!)*

## 2. Sentence Formulas & Conjugation Rules

**(Rumus Kalimat & Aturan Perubahan Kata Kerja)**

Good news for self-learners: **`will be + V-ing`** is the **same for all subjects** (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`)!

*(Kabar baik bagi pembelajar mandiri: bentuk **`will be + V-ing`** **sama untuk semua subjek**—I, You, We, They, He, She, It!)*

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + will be + V-ing` | *We **will be traveling** across Bali next week.* *(Kita akan sedang berkeliling Bali minggu depan.)* | 
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + will not (won't) be + V-ing` | *He **won't be working** at 6:00 PM; his shift ends at five.* *(Dia tidak akan sedang bekerja pukul 6 sore; sifnya berakhir pukul lima.)* | 
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Will + Subject + be + V-ing?` | ***Will** you **be waiting** for me at the exit gate?* *(Apakah kamu akan sedang menunggu saya di pintu keluar?)* | 
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + will + Subject + be + V-ing?` | *What **will** you **be doing** this time tomorrow?* *(Apa yang akan sedang kamu lakukan jam segini besok?)* | 

> 📌 **Alternative Form (`Going to be + V-ing`):**
> You can also use **`am / is / are + going to be + V-ing`** when talking about planned or intended future processes:
> ➡️ *She **is going to be presenting** her research at 2:00 PM.* *(Dia akan sedang mempresentasikan risetnya pukul 14.00.)*

## 3. Signal Words & Time Markers

**(Kata Penanda Waktu yang Sering Muncul)**

When you see time markers pointing to a **specific future moment or ongoing future period**, Future Continuous is often a great fit:

*(Ketika kamu melihat penanda waktu yang menunjuk pada **momen masa depan tertentu atau periode masa depan yang berlangsung**, Future Continuous sering kali sangat tepat:)*

* **This time tomorrow / next week / next month** ➡️ *jam segini besok / minggu depan / bulan depan*

* **At + specific future time** ➡️ *at 8:00 AM tomorrow, at noon on Sunday, at midnight*

* **All day / all morning / all evening tomorrow** ➡️ *sepanjang hari / pagi / malam besok*

* **When + Simple Present** ➡️ *when you call, when the class starts, when I get home*

## 4. Future Continuous vs. Simple Future (`Will + V1`)

**(Perbandingan: Future Continuous vs. Simple Future)**

| Feature (Aspek) | Future Continuous (`will be + V-ing`) | Simple Future (`will + V1`) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Main Focus (Fokus Utama)** | **Action in progress** at a future time *(Sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu)* | **Single action, decision, promise, or prediction** *(Tindakan tunggal, keputusan spontan, janji, atau prediksi)* | 
| **Example 1 (Dinner)** | *At 7:00 PM, we **will be eating** dinner.* *(In the middle of eating at 7:00)* | *I **will cook** dinner tonight.* *(Decision / statement of future action)* | 
| **Example 2 (Phone Call)** | *Don't ring at 9:00; I **will be teaching**.* *(Busy in class at 9:00)* | *I **will call** you at 9:00.* *(Promise to make a call at 9:00)* | 
| **Stative Verbs (Kata Kerja Keadaan)** | ❌ **Do NOT use** (*know, like, love, want, need, own*) | ✅ **Use Simple Future** (*I **will know** the results next week.*) | 

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context

**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Specific Time Tomorrow:**
   ➡️ At 10:30 AM tomorrow, I **will be taking** my driving test.
   *(Besok pukul 10.30 pagi, saya **akan sedang menjalani** ujian praktik mengemudi.)*

2. **Interrupted Future Action (`when` + Simple Present):**
   ➡️ **When** you wake up tomorrow morning, I **will already be making** breakfast.
   *(**Ketika** kamu bangun besok pagi, saya **sudah akan sedang membuat** sarapan.)*

3. **Polite Inquiry About Plans:**
   ➡️ **Will** you **be passing** by the post office later? Could you mail this letter for me?
   *(**Apakah** kamu **akan sedang melewati** kantor pos nanti? Bisakah kamu mengirimkan surat ini untuk saya?)*

4. **Ongoing Future Period (`all afternoon`):**
   ➡️ The students **will be practicing** for the school play **all afternoon** on Thursday.
   *(Para siswa **akan sedang berlatih** untuk drama sekolah **sepanjang sore** pada hari Kamis.)*

5. **Negative Sentence (`won't be + V-ing`):**
   ➡️ We **won't be using** the meeting room at 1:00 PM, so your team can take it.
   *(Kita **tidak akan sedang memakai** ruang rapat pada pukul 13.00, jadi timmu bisa memakainya.)*

## 6. Crucial Traps & Spelling Rules to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting & Aturan Ejaan yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using Simple Future (`will + V1`) inside a `when` time clause.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai Simple Future `will` di dalam klausa waktu yang diawali `when`.)*
  In time clauses starting with **`when`**, **`before`**, **`after`**, or **`as soon as`**, use **Simple Present**, not `will`!

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I will be sleeping when you **will arrive**.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I will be sleeping when you **arrive**.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using Stative Verbs in Future Continuous.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai kata kerja keadaan/perasaan dalam bentuk `will be + V-ing`.)*
  Verbs like **`know`, `like`, `love`, `hate`, `want`, `need`, `understand`, `believe`, `own`** do not normally take `-ing`.

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I **will be knowing** the answer by tomorrow afternoon.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I **will know** the answer by tomorrow afternoon.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Forgetting `be` (`will + V-ing` ❌).**
  *(Jebakan 3: Lupa menyertakan kata `be` setelah `will`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She **will working** at 4:00 PM.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She **will be working** at 4:00 PM.

* 📝 **Quick `V-ing` Spelling Reminders:**

  * Drop silent **`-e`**: *write* ➡️ **writing**, *make* ➡️ **making**

  * Double final consonant (CVC short vowel): *sit* ➡️ **sitting**, *plan* ➡️ **planning**, *travel* ➡️ **traveling** *(US)* / **travelling** *(UK)*

  * Change **`-ie`** to **`-y`**: *lie* ➡️ **lying**

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. This time tomorrow, we \_____________\_ (will fly / will be flying / are fly) over the clouds on our way to Tokyo.
   *(Jam segini besok, kita akan sedang terbang di atas awan dalam perjalanan menuju Tokyo.)*

2. Please don't call me at 7:30 PM because I \_____________\_ (will have / will be having / have) dinner with my family.
   *(Tolong jangan hubungi saya pukul 19.30 karena saya akan sedang makan malam bersama keluarga.)*

3. \_____________\_ (Will you be using / Will you use / Are you use) the car tomorrow afternoon, or can I borrow it?
   *(Apakah kamu akan sedang menggunakan mobilnya besok sore, atau boleh saya pinjam?)*

4. When the train \_____________\_ (arrives / will arrive / is arriving) at 8:00 AM, everyone will be waiting on the platform.
   *(Ketika keretanya tiba pukul 08.00 pagi, semua orang akan sedang menunggu di peron.)*

5. He \_____________\_ (won't be sleeping / won't sleep / isn't sleep) at midnight; he is staying up to finish a project.
   *(Dia tidak akan sedang tidur pada tengah malam; dia begadang untuk menyelesaikan proyek.)*

6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*

   * A) I will be needing your help tomorrow morning.

   * B) I will need your help tomorrow morning.

   * C) I will be need your help tomorrow morning.

7. What \_____________\_ (will she be doing / she will be doing / does she be doing) at 3:00 PM this Friday?
   *(Apa yang akan sedang dia lakukan pada pukul 15.00 hari Jumat ini?)*

8. The technicians \_____________\_ (will be testing / will test / are test) the new sound system all morning tomorrow.
   *(Para teknisi akan sedang menguji sistem suara yang baru sepanjang pagi besok.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **will be flying**
   ➡️ Menunjukkan aktivitas yang akan **sedang berlangsung** pada waktu tertentu besok (`This time tomorrow`).

2. **will be having**
   ➡️ Menunjukkan bahwa pada pukul 19.30 pembicara akan berada di tengah proses makan malam (`will be + V-ing`).

3. **Will you be using**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan sopan mengenai rencana/kegiatan masa depan seseorang tanpa memberi tekanan (`Will + subjek + be + V-ing?`).

4. **arrives**
   ➡️ Di dalam klausa waktu yang diawali `when`, gunakan **Simple Present** (`arrives`), bukan `will arrive`.

5. **won't be sleeping**
   ➡️ Bentuk negatif Future Continuous untuk menyatakan aktivitas yang tidak akan sedang berlangsung pada tengah malam (`won't be + V-ing`).

6. **B) I will need your help tomorrow morning.**
   ➡️ `Need` adalah *stative verb* (kebutuhan), sehingga tidak memakai bentuk continuous (`will be needing` ❌).

7. **will she be doing**
   ➡️ Susunan kalimat tanya `Wh-` Future Continuous adalah `Wh-word + will + subjek + be + V-ing`.

8. **will be testing**
   ➡️ Menggambarkan proses pengujian yang berlangsung terus selama periode waktu tertentu di masa depan (`all morning tomorrow`).
