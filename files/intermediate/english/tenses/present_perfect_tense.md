# 📘 Self-Study Guide: Present Perfect Tense
**Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Present Perfect Tense**

Welcome to your self-study guide on the **Present Perfect Tense**! 🌟 Many learners find this tense tricky at first because it acts like a bridge between **the past** and **the present**. Take your time reading each section, study the examples in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and test yourself with the quiz at the end.

> 💡 **Core Idea (Ide Utama):** Present Perfect Tense is used when a past action or state has a **connection, effect, or relevance right now (in the present)**, OR when the exact time in the past is **not mentioned / not important**.
> *(Present Perfect Tense digunakan ketika aksi atau keadaan di masa lalu memiliki **kaitan, dampak, atau relevansi di saat ini**, ATAU ketika waktu pastinya di masa lalu **tidak disebutkan / tidak penting**.)*

---

## 🎯 1. Usage Situations: When to Use Present Perfect Tense (Situasi Penggunaan)

Use the Present Perfect Tense in these **five main situations**:

1. **Unfinished Actions That Started in the Past and Continue Now ➡️** *(Aksi yang dimulai di masa lalu dan masih berlanjut sampai sekarang)*
   * **EN:** *I have lived in Bitung for five years.* (I started living here five years ago, and I still live here now.)
   * **ID:** *Saya telah tinggal di Bitung selama lima tahun.*

2. **Life Experiences (Exact Time Not Mentioned) 🧭** *(Pengalaman hidup — waktu pastinya tidak disebutkan)*
   * **EN:** *She has visited Bunaken twice.* (Focus is on the experience, not *when* she went.)
   * **ID:** *Dia telah mengunjungi Bunaken dua kali.*

3. **Recent Actions with Visible Results Now 🔍** *(Aksi yang baru saja terjadi dengan hasil yang terlihat sekarang)*
   * **EN:** *Someone has broken the glass!* (Look at the broken glass right now.)
   * **ID:** *Seseorang telah memecahkan gelas itu!*

4. **Completed Actions in an Unfinished Time Period ⏳** *(Aksi yang selesai dalam periode waktu yang belum berakhir)*
   * **EN:** *We have taken three quizzes this week.* (The week is not over yet.)
   * **ID:** *Kami telah mengikuti tiga kuis minggu ini.*

5. **Changes Over Time 📈** *(Perubahan seiring berjalannya waktu)*
   * **EN:** *Your English has improved a lot.* (Compared to before, your skill is better now.)
   * **ID:** *Bahasa Inggris kamu telah meningkat pesat.*

> ⚠️ **Quick Contrast (Perbandingan Cepat):**
> * **Simple Past:** *I lost my keys yesterday.* ➡️ Focus on **yesterday** (finished past time). *(Saya kehilangan kunci saya kemarin.)*
> * **Present Perfect:** *I have lost my keys.* ➡️ Focus on **now** (I still cannot find them right now). *(Saya kehilangan kunci saya [dan sampai sekarang belum ketemu].)*

## 🧩 2. Formulas: Positive, Negative, Interrogative (Verbal & Nominal)

Before forming sentences, remember how **have** and **has** pair with subjects:
* **Have** ➡️ used with **I, You, We, They** (and plural subjects like *the students*).
* **Has** ➡️ used with **He, She, It** (and singular subjects like *Rina, my brother*).

### A. Verbal Type (Kalimat Verbal — Uses Action Verbs / Kata Kerja Aksi)
Verbal sentences use **Verb 3 (Past Participle)** ➡️ *e.g., gone, eaten, studied, finished*.

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | **Subject + have/has + Verb 3 + Object/Complement** | **EN:** *They **have finished** their project.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka **telah menyelesaikan** proyek mereka.* |
| **Negative (-)** | **Subject + have/has + not + Verb 3 + Object/Complement** | **EN:** *He **has not (hasn't) watched** that movie.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **belum menonton** film itu.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | **Have/Has + Subject + Verb 3 + Object/Complement?** | **EN:** ***Has** she **sent** the report?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** dia **sudah mengirim** laporan itu?* |

---

### B. Nominal Type (Kalimat Nominal — No Action Verb / Tanpa Kata Kerja Aksi)
Nominal sentences have **no action verb** (they use adjectives, nouns, or adverbial phrases of place) and use **been** as the Verb 3 of *to be* (`am/is/are` ➡️ `been`).

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | **Subject + have/has + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place** | **EN:** *I **have been** sick since Monday.*<br>**ID:** *Saya **sudah sakit** sejak hari Senin.* |
| **Negative (-)** | **Subject + have/has + not + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place** | **EN:** *We **have not (haven't) been** to that café.*<br>**ID:** *Kami **belum pernah ke** kafe itu.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | **Have/Has + Subject + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place?** | **EN:** ***Have** you **been** a member here long?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** kamu **sudah lama menjadi** anggota di sini?* |

> 📌 **Helpful Note on "Been" vs. "Gone":**
> * **Has/Have been to** ➡️ went and came back *(sudah pergi dan sudah kembali)*. Example: *I have been to Manado.* (I went there and returned.)
> * **Has/Have gone to** ➡️ went and is still there / hasn't come back *(sudah pergi dan masih di sana / belum kembali)*. Example: *Budi has gone to the market.* (He is at the market right now.)

## ⏰ 3. Important Time Signals (Penanda Waktu Penting)

Time signals are helpful clues that frequently appear in **Present Perfect Tense**:

| Time Signal | Meaning in Bahasa Indonesia (Arti) | Typical Position (Posisi Umum) | Example (EN & ID) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Already** | sudah | Between `have/has` and `Verb 3` (or at the end) | **EN:** *I have **already** eaten.*<br>**ID:** *Saya **sudah** makan.* |
| **Yet** | belum *(negative)* / sudahkah? *(question)* | Usually at the **end** of the sentence | **EN:** *Haven't you finished **yet**?*<br>**ID:** *Apakah kamu **belum** selesai?* |
| **Just** | baru saja | Between `have/has` and `Verb 3` | **EN:** *She has **just** arrived.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **baru saja** tiba.* |
| **Ever** | pernah *(mostly in questions)* | Between subject and `Verb 3` | **EN:** *Have you **ever** tried sushi?*<br>**ID:** *Apakah kamu **pernah** mencoba sushi?* |
| **Never** | tidak pernah | Between `have/has` and `Verb 3` | **EN:** *He has **never** ridden a horse.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **tidak pernah** menunggang kuda.* |
| **Since** | sejak *(point in time / titik waktu)* | Before a specific time, date, or event | **EN:** *We have studied here **since** 2023.*<br>**ID:** *Kami telah belajar di sini **sejak** 2023.* |
| **For** | selama *(duration / durasi waktu)* | Before a length of time | **EN:** *They have worked together **for** three years.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka telah bekerja sama **selama** tiga tahun.* |
| **So far / Recently / Lately** | sejauh ini / baru-baru ini / akhir-akhir ini | Beginning or end of the sentence | **EN:** *It has rained a lot **lately**.*<br>**ID:** *Akhir-akhir ini sering turun hujan.* |

## 📝 4. Self-Study Quiz (Latihan Mandiri)

Try answering all **10 questions** first before checking the answer key below! ✍️

### Part A: Multiple Choice (Pilihan Ganda)
1. My brother _______ his phone. He can't find it anywhere.
   a) loses ➡️ b) has lost ➡️ c) lost ➡️ d) is losing
2. _______ you ever _______ to Raja Ampat?
   a) Did / visit ➡️ b) Do / visited ➡️ c) Have / visited ➡️ d) Has / visited
3. We haven't received the package _______.
   a) already ➡️ b) just ➡️ c) since ➡️ d) yet
4. Dika and Tari _______ married for six years. *(Nominal)*
   a) has been ➡️ b) have been ➡️ c) are ➡️ d) were

### Part B: Fill in the Blanks (Isilah Titik-Titik)
5. She _______ (just / walk) out of the meeting room. *(Verbal, Positive)*
6. I _______ (not / be) to Surabaya this year. *(Nominal, Negative)*
7. _______ your teacher _______ (check) your essay yet? *(Verbal, Interrogative)*

### Part C: Translate & Identify Type (Terjemahkan & Tentukan Jenis Kalimat)
8. **EN:** *They have not lived in this town for a long time.* ➡️ Translate to Bahasa Indonesia & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
9. **ID:** *Dia (perempuan) telah menjadi dokter sejak tahun lalu.* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
10. **ID:** *Apakah kamu baru saja minum kopi?* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.

---

### ✅ Answer Key & Explanations (Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan)

1. **b) has lost** ➡️ Singular subject (*My brother*) + `has` + `lost` (Verb 3). *(Saudara laki-laki saya kehilangan HP-nya.)*
2. **c) Have / visited** ➡️ Subject *you* takes `have` + `ever` + `visited` (Verb 3). *(Apakah kamu pernah mengunjungi Raja Ampat?)*
3. **d) yet** ➡️ Negative sentence with "haven't" commonly ends with *yet*. *(Kami belum menerima paket itu.)*
4. **b) have been** ➡️ Plural subject (*Dika and Tari*) + `have` + `been` + adjective (*married*). *(Dika dan Tari sudah menikah selama enam tahun.)*
5. **has just walked** ➡️ Singular subject (*She*) + `has` + `just` + `walked` (Verb 3). *(Dia baru saja berjalan keluar dari ruang rapat.)*
6. **have not (haven't) been** ➡️ Subject *I* + `have not` + `been` + adverb of place (*to Surabaya*). *(Saya belum ke Surabaya tahun ini.)*
7. **Has / checked** ➡️ Singular subject (*your teacher*) starts with `Has` + subject + `checked` (Verb 3). *(Apakah gurumu sudah memeriksa esaimu?)*
8. **Verbal** ➡️ **ID:** *Mereka belum lama tinggal di kota ini.* *(atau: Mereka tidak telah tinggal di kota ini untuk waktu yang lama.)*
9. **Nominal** ➡️ **EN:** *She has been a doctor since last year.* *(No action verb; uses "been" + noun "a doctor".)*
10. **Verbal** ➡️ **EN:** *Have you just drunk coffee?* *(Verb 3 of drink is "drunk".)*
