# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to talk about how long an activity **would have been going on by a specific future point from a past point of view**—like *"Yesterday he told me that by 6:00 PM tonight, he **would have been working** for ten hours"* or *"By last Friday, we thought they **would have been living** in Jakarta for five years"*? That is the **Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense** (also called **Future Perfect Continuous in the Past**) ⏳!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin membicarakan sudah berapa lama suatu aktivitas **akan sudah berlangsung pada titik waktu tertentu dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu**—seperti "Yesterday he told me that by 6:00 PM tonight, he **would have been working** for ten hours" atau "By last Friday, we thought they **would have been living** in Jakarta for five years"? Itulah **Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense**—juga disebut **Future Perfect Continuous in the Past** ⏳!)*

Think of it like this: **at a past moment**, someone looked forward to a later deadline and pictured an ongoing process **already running for a duration** up to that point ➡️!

*(Anggap saja begini: **pada suatu momen di masa lalu**, seseorang melihat ke depan menuju suatu batas waktu berikutnya dan membayangkan sebuah proses yang **sudah berjalan selama durasi tertentu** hingga titik waktu tersebut ➡️!)*

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Past Future Perfect Continuous

**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Past Future Perfect Continuous)**

Use the **Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense** in **three main situations**:

*(Gunakan **Past Future Perfect Continuous Tense** dalam **tiga situasi utama**:)*

1. **Reported Duration Up to a Future Deadline from a Past Viewpoint (Melaporkan Durasi Hingga Batas Waktu dari Sudut Pandang Masa Lalu):**

   * *Direct thought:* "By 8:00 PM tonight, I **will have been studying** for three hours."

   * ➡️ *Past Future Perfect Continuous:* She said that by 8:00 PM tonight, she **would have been studying** for three hours.

   * *(Dia berkata bahwa menjelang pukul 20.00 malam ini, dia **sudah akan belajar** selama tiga jam.)*

2. **Past Predictions About an Ongoing Process Before Another Event (Prediksi Masa Lalu tentang Proses Berkelanjutan Sebelum Kejadian Lain):**

   * *Example:* We thought the team **would have been testing** the software for two weeks before the launch date.

   * *(Kami mengira tim tersebut **sudah akan menguji** perangkat lunak itu selama dua minggu sebelum tanggal peluncuran.)*

3. **Emphasizing Continuous Effort / Process Rather Than Just Completion (Menekankan Proses atau Durasi Berkelanjutan Dibandingkan Sekadar Selesai):**

   * *Example:* He hoped that by last December, he **would have been running** his own café for two years.

   * *(Dia berharap bahwa menjelang Desember tahun lalu, dia **sudah akan menjalankan** kafe miliknya sendiri selama dua tahun.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Is this a **past viewpoint** (`said`, `thought`, `knew`, `hoped`, `expected`) looking ahead to a **duration (`for ...`)** of an **ongoing action (`V-ing`)**?"* If yes (and the verb is an action verb, not a stative verb), use **`would have been + V-ing`**! ✅
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah ini **sudut pandang masa lalu** yang melihat ke depan pada **durasi (`for ...`)** dari suatu aktivitas **yang sedang berlangsung (`V-ing`)**?" Jika ya—dan kata kerjanya adalah kata kerja aksi, bukan stative verb—gunakan **`would have been + V-ing`**! ✅)*

## 2. Two Main Forms: `Would Have Been + V-ing` vs. `Was/Were Going To Have Been + V-ing`

**(Dua Bentuk Utama: `Would Have Been + V-ing` vs. `Was/Were Going To Have Been + V-ing`)**

* **`Would have been + V-ing`:** The most common and natural form in reported speech (*said, told, thought, knew, hoped, expected*) and storytelling. Same form for **all subjects** (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`).
  *(Bentuk yang paling umum dan alami dalam kalimat tidak langsung seperti said, told, thought, knew, hoped, atau expected serta cerita. Bentuknya sama untuk **semua subjek**.)*

* **`Was / Were going to have been + V-ing`:** Used occasionally for past intentions or expectations about an ongoing duration, though `would have been + V-ing` is much more common in everyday and formal English.
  *(Kadang digunakan untuk niat atau ekspektasi masa lalu tentang durasi yang berlangsung, meskipun `would have been + V-ing` jauh lebih umum digunakan.)*

  * Use **`was`** for: `I, He, She, It`, singular nouns (*Dina*).

  * Use **`were`** for: `You, We, They`, plural nouns (*the students*).

### Sentence Formula Table (Tabel Rumus Kalimat)

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with `Would Have Been` | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Positive (+)** | `S + would have been + V-ing` | *He said by noon he **would have been driving** for six hours.* *(Dia bilang menjelang siang dia sudah akan menyetir selama enam jam.)* | 
| **Negative (-)** | `S + would not (wouldn't) have been + V-ing` | *She thought she **wouldn't have been waiting** long when we arrived.* *(Dia mengira dia tidak akan menunggu lama saat kami tiba.)* | 
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Would + S + have been + V-ing?` | ***Would** they **have been practicing** for long by the time the show started?* *(Apakah mereka sudah akan berlatih cukup lama pada saat pertunjukan dimulai?)* | 
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + would + S + have been + V-ing?` | *How long **would** she **have been teaching** by last year?* *(Sudah berapa lama dia akan mengajar menjelang tahun lalu?)* | 

## 3. Signal Words & Time Contexts

**(Penanda Waktu & Konteks Kalimat yang Sering Muncul)**

Past Future Perfect Continuous usually combines a **past reporting/mental verb** with a **future deadline (`by`, `by the time`)** and a **duration (`for ...`)**:

*(Past Future Perfect Continuous biasanya menggabungkan **kata kerja pelapor/pikiran masa lalu** dengan **batas waktu (`by`, `by the time`)** dan **durasi (`for ...`)**:)*

* **Past Reporting / Mental Verbs:** *said, told, thought, knew, believed, hoped, expected, promised* *(berkata, memberi tahu, berpikir, tahu, percaya, berharap, mengharapkan, berjanji)*

* **Deadline Markers:** *by 5:00 PM, by tonight, by the time he arrived, by last June* *(menjelang pukul 17.00, menjelang malam ini, pada saat dia tiba, menjelang Juni tahun lalu)*

* **Duration Markers:** *for two hours, for three days, for months, for ten years* *(selama dua jam, selama tiga hari, selama berbulan-bulan, selama sepuluh tahun)*

## 4. Comparing Related Past Future Tenses

**(Perbandingan Tense Terkait)**

| Tense (Jenis) | Formula (Rumus) | Focus (Fokus) | Example from a Past Viewpoint | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Past Future Simple** | `would + V1` | Future action/decision/prediction from the past *(Tindakan/keputusan/prediksi dari masa lalu)* | *He said he **would call** later.* *(Dia bilang dia akan menelepon nanti.)* | 
| **Past Future Continuous** | `would be + V-ing` | Action **in progress** at that future time *(Sedang berlangsung pada waktu tersebut)* | *He said at 8:00 PM he **would be sleeping**.* *(Dia bilang jam 8 malam dia akan sedang tidur.)* | 
| **Past Future Perfect** | `would have + V3` | Action **completed** before that future time *(Sudah selesai sebelum waktu tersebut)* | *He said by 8:00 PM he **would have finished**.* *(Dia bilang menjelang jam 8 malam dia sudah akan selesai.)* | 
| **Past Future Perfect Continuous** | `would have been + V-ing` | **Duration / ongoing process** up to that future time *(Durasi atau proses yang masih berjalan hingga waktu tersebut)* | *He said by 8:00 PM he **would have been working** for four hours.* *(Dia bilang menjelang jam 8 malam dia sudah akan bekerja selama empat jam.)* | 

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context

**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Reported Duration Before a Time Limit:**
   ➡️ Rina told me that by 9:00 PM tonight, she **would have been studying** for her exam **for** four hours.
   *(Rina memberi tahu saya bahwa menjelang pukul 21.00 malam ini, dia **sudah akan belajar** untuk ujiannya **selama** empat jam.)*

2. **Past Expectation About a Long Process:**
   ➡️ We expected that by last month, the company **would have been developing** the new app **for** six months.
   *(Kami memperkirakan bahwa menjelang bulan lalu, perusahaan tersebut **sudah akan mengembangkan** aplikasi baru itu **selama** enam bulan.)*

3. **Before Another Event in the Narrative:**
   ➡️ He knew that by the time the guests arrived, they **would have been preparing** food **for** most of the afternoon.
   *(Dia tahu bahwa pada saat para tamu tiba, mereka **sudah akan menyiapkan** makanan **selama** sebagian besar waktu sore.)*

4. **Negative Form (`wouldn't have been + V-ing`):**
   ➡️ She said she **wouldn't have been living** in Bandung for very long by the time we visited.
   *(Dia berkata bahwa dia **belum akan lama tinggal** di Bandung pada saat kami berkunjung.)*

5. **Question Form (`How long would ... have been ...`):**
   ➡️ **How long would** they **have been traveling** by the time they reached the coast?
   *(**Sudah berapa lama** mereka **akan melakukan perjalanan** pada saat mereka mencapai pesisir?)*

## 6. Crucial Traps to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using Stative Verbs in Past Future Perfect Continuous.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai kata kerja keadaan/perasaan dalam bentuk Past Future Perfect Continuous.)*
  Verbs like **`know`, `like`, `love`, `hate`, `want`, `need`, `understand`, `believe`, `own`** normally use **Past Future Perfect (`would have + V3`)**, not `would have been + V-ing`.

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* By last year, he said he **would have been knowing** her for ten years.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* By last year, he said he **would have known** her for ten years.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Missing `have` or `been` or using `V3` instead of `V-ing`.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Menghilangkan `have` atau `been`, atau memakai `V3` alih-alih `V-ing`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She said she **would been working** / **would have been worked** for three hours.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She said she **would have been working** for three hours.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Using `would` inside a `by the time` or `when` time clause.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Memakai `would` di dalam klausa waktu `by the time` atau `when`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* By the time you **would arrive**, I would have been waiting for an hour.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* By the time you **arrived**, I would have been waiting for an hour.

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below! ✍️

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah! ✍️)*

1. Yesterday Budi said that by 5:00 PM today, he \_____________\_ (would have been working / would be work / will have been working) on the project for six hours.
   *(Kemarin Budi berkata bahwa menjelang pukul 17.00 hari ini, dia sudah akan mengerjakan proyek tersebut selama enam jam.)*

2. She hoped that by last July, she \_____________\_ (would have been playing / would play / had play) the violin for eight years.
   *(Dia berharap bahwa menjelang Juli tahun lalu, dia sudah akan bermain biola selama delapan tahun.)*

3. By the time the train \_____________\_ (arrived / would arrive / arrives), they would have been waiting for nearly two hours.
   *(Pada saat keretanya tiba, mereka sudah akan menunggu selama hampir dua jam.)*

4. He said he \_____________\_ (wouldn't have been driving / wouldn't driving / didn't have been driving) for very long when he stopped for coffee.
   *(Dia berkata bahwa dia belum akan lama menyetir ketika dia berhenti untuk minum kopi.)*

5. \_____________\_ (How long would you have been / How long you would have been / How long did you have been) training by the time the match began?
   *(Sudah berapa lama kamu akan berlatih pada saat pertandingan dimulai?)*

6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*

   * A) By last month, he said he would have been owning the shop for five years.

   * B) By last month, he said he would have owned the shop for five years.

   * C) By last month, he said he would have been own the shop for five years.

7. We thought the workers \_____________\_ (would have been building / would build / have been building) that bridge for over a year by then.
   *(Kami mengira para pekerja sudah akan membangun jembatan itu selama lebih dari setahun menjelang waktu tersebut.)*

8. \_____________\_ (Would she have been / Did she would have been / Was she have been) living there for long before she moved?
   *(Apakah dia sudah akan tinggal di sana cukup lama sebelum dia pindah?)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **would have been working** ✅
   ➡️ Sudut pandang masa lalu (`Yesterday Budi said...`) melihat ke depan pada durasi (`for six hours`) menjelang pukul 17.00 hari ini.

2. **would have been playing** ✅
   ➡️ Harapan dari masa lalu (`She hoped...`) mengenai proses berkelanjutan selama delapan tahun hingga Juli tahun lalu.

3. **arrived** ✅
   ➡️ Klausa waktu `By the time` menggunakan bentuk lampau sederhana (`arrived`), sementara klausa utama memakai `would have been waiting`.

4. **wouldn't have been driving** ✅
   ➡️ Bentuk negatif Past Future Perfect Continuous (`wouldn't have been + V-ing`) untuk menyatakan durasi singkat sebelum berhenti.

5. **How long would you have been** ✅
   ➡️ Urutan tanya `Wh-` yang benar adalah `How long + would + subjek (you) + have been + V-ing`.

6. **B) By last month, he said he would have owned the shop for five years.** ✅
   ➡️ `Own` adalah *stative verb* (kepemilikan), sehingga tidak memakai bentuk continuous (`would have been owning` ❌).

7. **would have been building** ✅
   ➡️ Menunjukkan proses pembangunan yang berlangsung selama lebih dari setahun hingga titik waktu yang dimaksud dari sudut pandang masa lalu (`We thought...`).

8. **Would she have been** ✅
   ➡️ Kalimat tanya Yes/No Past Future Perfect Continuous menggunakan urutan `Would + subjek (she) + have been + V-ing`.
