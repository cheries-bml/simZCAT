# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Past Continuous Tense
**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Past Continuous Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to describe what you were right in the middle of doing at a specific moment yesterday—like *"I **was cooking** dinner at 7:00 PM"* or *"She **was reading** a book when the phone rang"*? That is the **Past Continuous Tense** (also called the *Past Progressive Tense*)!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin menggambarkan apa yang sedang kamu lakukan di tengah-tengah suatu waktu kemarin—seperti "I **was cooking** dinner at 7:00 PM" atau "She **was reading** a book when the phone rang"? Itulah **Past Continuous Tense**—juga disebut *Past Progressive Tense*!)*

Think of the Past Continuous as a **video camera** recording an action in progress in the past, whereas the Simple Past is like a **snapshot** of a completed event.

*(Anggap saja Past Continuous seperti **kamera video** yang merekam aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu, sedangkan Simple Past seperti **foto jepretan** kejadian yang sudah selesai.)*

---

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Past Continuous
**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Past Continuous)**

Use the Past Continuous Tense in **four main situations**:

*(Gunakan Past Continuous Tense dalam **empat situasi utama**:)*

1. **An Action in Progress at a Specific Past Time (Aksi yang Sedang Berlangsung pada Waktu Tertentu di Masa Lalu):**
   * *Example:* At 8:30 PM last night, I **was studying** for my exam.
   * *(Pada pukul 20.30 tadi malam, saya **sedang belajar** untuk ujian saya.)*
2. **An Ongoing Background Action Interrupted by a Shorter Event (Aksi Latar Belakang yang Disela Kejadian Singkat):**
   * *Example:* We **were having** dinner **when** the power went out.
   * *(Kami **sedang makan** malam **ketika** listrik padam.)*
3. **Two Actions Happening at the Same Time in the Past (Dua Aksi yang Terjadi Bersamaan di Masa Lalu):**
   * *Example:* **While** Budi **was washing** the dishes, Rina **was drying** them.
   * *(**Sementara** Budi **sedang mencuci** piring, Rina **sedang mengeringkannya**.)*
4. **Setting the Scene in a Story (Membangun Suasana / Latar Cerita):**
   * *Example:* The sun **was shining**, birds **were singing**, and people **were walking** through the park.
   * *(Matahari **sedang bersinar**, burung-burung **sedang berkicau**, dan orang-orang **sedang berjalan** di taman.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Was this action **unfinished and in progress** at that exact past moment?"* If yes, use **`was / were + V-ing`**!
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah tindakan ini **belum selesai dan sedang berlangsung** pada momen masa lalu tersebut?" Jika ya, gunakan **`was / were + V-ing`**!)*

## 2. Sentence Formulas & Subject–`Was / Were` Rules
**(Rumus Kalimat & Aturan Subjek–`Was / Were`)**

Every Past Continuous sentence needs **two parts**: the past form of `to be` (**`was`** or **`were`**) + **`V-ing` (present participle)**.

*(Setiap kalimat Past Continuous membutuhkan **dua bagian**: bentuk lampau `to be` (**`was`** atau **`were`**) + **`V-ing` (present participle)**.)*

### Subject + `Was / Were` Matching (Pasangan Subjek & `Was / Were`)
* **`I, He, She, It`**, singular nouns (*Dina, the rain*) ➡️ **`was`** *(She was sleeping)*
* **`You, We, They`**, plural nouns (*the students*) ➡️ **`were`** *(They were talking)*

### Sentence Structure Table (Tabel Rumus Kalimat)

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + was / were + V-ing` | *He **was driving** home at 6:00 PM.* *(Dia sedang menyetir pulang pukul 6 sore.)* |
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + was / were + not + V-ing` <br> *(wasn't / weren't)* | *They **weren't paying** attention during the briefing.* *(Mereka tidak sedang memperhatikan saat pengarahan.)* |
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Was / Were + Subject + V-ing?` | ***Were** you **sleeping** when I called?* *(Apakah kamu sedang tidur ketika saya menelepon?)* |
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + was / were + Subject + V-ing?` | *What **were** they **doing** at noon yesterday?* *(Apa yang sedang mereka lakukan kemarin siang?)* |

## 3. Signal Words: `When` vs. `While` & Time Markers
**(Kata Penanda Waktu: Perbedaan `When` vs. `While`)**

One of the most common grammar tests involves combining **Past Continuous** and **Simple Past** using **`when`** and **`while`**:

*(Salah satu soal tata bahasa yang paling sering muncul adalah menggabungkan **Past Continuous** dan **Simple Past** menggunakan **`when`** dan **`while`**:)*

* **`While` (Sementara / Saat) + Past Continuous (`was/were + V-ing`):**
  * Introduces the **longer, ongoing background action** (or two simultaneous actions).
  * *(Mengawali **aksi latar belakang yang lebih panjang dan sedang berlangsung**, atau dua aksi bersamaan.)*
  * ➡️ **While** I **was walking** to the station, it started to rain.
  * *(Sementara saya sedang berjalan ke stasiun, hujan mulai turun.)*
* **`When` (Ketika) + Simple Past (`V2`):**
  * Introduces the **shorter, sudden interrupting action**.
  * *(Mengawali **tindakan singkat dan tiba-tiba yang menyela**.)*
  * ➡️ I was walking to the station **when** it **started** to rain.
  * *(Saya sedang berjalan ke stasiun ketika hujan mulai turun.)*

### Other Common Time Markers (Penanda Waktu Lainnya)
* *at 7:00 PM last night / at this time yesterday* *(pada pukul 7 malam tadi / pada jam segini kemarin)*
* *all morning / all afternoon / all day yesterday* *(sepanjang pagi / sore / hari kemarin)*
* *as* *(ketika / sementara)* ➡️ *As she **was leaving** the house, she remembered her keys.*

## 4. Past Continuous vs. Simple Past
**(Perbandingan: Past Continuous vs. Simple Past)**

| Feature (Aspek) | Past Continuous (`was/were + V-ing`) | Simple Past (`V2`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Focus (Fokus Utama)** | **Action in progress** at a past moment *(Sedang berlangsung di masa lalu)* | **Completed action** in the past *(Tindakan yang sudah selesai)* |
| **Timeline Role (Peran Waktu)** | Longer background activity *(Aktivitas latar belakang yang panjang)* | Shorter interrupting event or sequence *(Kejadian singkat penyela / urutan)* |
| **Example Comparison (Perbandingan)** | *I **was reading** a book at 9:00 PM.* *(Started before 9:00, still reading at 9:00)* | *I **read** a book last night.* *(Finished reading the book)* |
| **Stative Verbs (Kata Kerja Keadaan)** | ❌ **Do NOT use** (*knew, liked, wanted, owned*) | ✅ **Always use** (*I **knew** the answer.*) |

## 5. Crucial Traps & Spelling Rules to Avoid
**(Jebakan Penting & Aturan Ejaan yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using Stative Verbs in Past Continuous.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai kata kerja keadaan/perasaan dalam bentuk Past Continuous.)*
  Verbs of thought, feeling, possession, or senses—such as **`know`, `like`, `love`, `hate`, `want`, `need`, `understand`, `believe`, `own`**—use **Simple Past (`V2`)**, not `was/were + V-ing`.
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* We **were knowing** each other very well back then.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* We **knew** each other very well back then. *(Kami saling mengenal dengan sangat baik waktu itu.)*
* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using Past Continuous for BOTH clauses when one short action interrupts a long action.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai Past Continuous di KEDUA bagian kalimat saat satu aksi singkat menyela aksi panjang.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I **was taking** a shower when the doorbell **was ringing**.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I **was taking** a shower when the doorbell **rang**. *(Saya sedang mandi ketika bel pintu berbunyi.)*
* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `was` and `were` with `you` or plural subjects.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Tertukar memakai `was` untuk subjek `you` atau subjek jamak.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* What **was** you doing yesterday? / The dogs **was** barking.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* What **were** you doing yesterday? / The dogs **were** barking.
* 📝 **Quick `V-ing` Spelling Reminders:**
  * Drop silent **`-e`**: *drive* ➡️ **driving**, *have* ➡️ **having**
  * Double final consonant (CVC short vowel): *run* ➡️ **running**, *chat* ➡️ **chatting**, *swim* ➡️ **swimming**
  * Change **`-ie`** to **`-y`**: *lie* ➡️ **lying**, *tie* ➡️ **tying**

## 6. Self-Study Practice Quiz
**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. At 10:00 PM last night, my sister ______________ (was studying / studied / is studying) for her biology test.
   *(Pada pukul 10 malam tadi, kakak perempuan saya sedang belajar untuk ujian biologinya.)*
2. We ______________ (were watching / watched / are watching) a movie **when** the internet suddenly disconnected.
   *(Kami sedang menonton film ketika internet tiba-tiba terputus.)*
3. **While** I ______________ (was cooking / cooked / cook) dinner, my brother was setting the table.
   *(Sementara saya sedang memasak makan malam, saudara laki-laki saya sedang menata meja.)*
4. Where ______________ (were you going / was you going / did you going) when I saw you at the station this morning?
   *(Ke mana kamu sedang pergi ketika saya melihatmu di stasiun pagi ini?)*
5. They ______________ (weren't playing / wasn't playing / didn't playing) video games; they were doing their group homework.
   *(Mereka tidak sedang bermain gim video; mereka sedang mengerjakan PR kelompok mereka.)*
6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*
   * A) She was wanting a glass of cold water after the run.
   * B) She wanted a glass of cold water after the run.
   * C) She was want a glass of cold water after the run.
7. As the plane ______________ (was landing / landed / lands), the flight attendants checked everyone's seatbelts.
   *(Saat pesawat sedang mendarat, para pramugari memeriksa sabuk pengaman semua penumpang.)*
8. I dropped my keys while I ______________ (was running / ran / am running) to catch the bus.
   *(Kunci saya jatuh ketika saya sedang berlari mengejar bus.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations
**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **was studying**
   ➡️ Aktivitas sedang berlangsung pada jam tertentu di masa lalu (`At 10:00 PM last night`) dengan subjek tunggal `my sister`.
2. **were watching**
   ➡️ Aksi latar belakang yang sedang berlangsung (`were watching`) ketika disela kejadian singkat (`when the internet suddenly disconnected`).
3. **was cooking**
   ➡️ Dua aktivitas yang berlangsung bersamaan di masa lalu menggunakan `While + Past Continuous` di kedua bagian.
4. **were you going**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan `Wh-` masa lalu untuk subjek `you` menggunakan **`were`** + `you` + `V-ing`.
5. **weren't playing**
   ➡️ Bentuk negatif untuk subjek jamak `They` menggunakan **`weren't` + `V-ing`**.
6. **B) She wanted a glass of cold water after the run.**
   ➡️ `Want` adalah *stative verb* (keinginan), sehingga tidak dipakai dalam bentuk `-ing` (`was wanting` ❌); gunakan Simple Past (`wanted`).
7. **was landing**
   ➡️ `As` berfungsi mirip dengan `while` untuk menggambarkan proses yang sedang berlangsung saat tindakan lain terjadi.
8. **was running**
   ➡️ Setelah `while`, gunakan Past Continuous (`was running`) untuk menunjukkan aksi panjang yang sedang berlangsung saat kunci jatuh (`dropped`).
