# 📘 Self-Study Guide: Simple Future Tense

**Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Simple Future Tense**

Welcome to your self-study guide on the **Simple Future Tense**! 🚀 This tense helps you talk about things that have not happened yet—whether you are making a spontaneous decision right now, sharing a plan you already arranged, making a prediction, or promising to help someone. Take your time reading each section, study the examples in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and test yourself with the quiz at the end.

> 💡 **Core Idea (Ide Utama):** Simple Future Tense is used to express actions, events, or states that **will happen in the future**, typically using **will + base verb (Verb 1)** or **be (am/is/are) going to + base verb (Verb 1)**.
> *(Simple Future Tense digunakan untuk menyatakan aksi, kejadian, atau keadaan yang **akan terjadi di masa depan**, umumnya menggunakan **will + kata kerja dasar (Verb 1)** atau **be (am/is/are) going to + kata kerja dasar (Verb 1)**.)*

---

## 🎯 1. Usage Situations: When to Use Simple Future Tense (Situasi Penggunaan)

In English, learners often see two main forms for the future: **will** and **be going to**. Here are the **six main usage situations** so you know when to use each one:

1. **Spontaneous / Instant Decisions Made at the Moment of Speaking ⚡** *(Keputusan spontan yang dibuat saat sedang berbicara)*
   * **EN:** *The phone is ringing. I **will answer** it!* (Decided right now ➡️ uses `will`.)
   * **ID:** *Teleponnya berdering. Saya **akan mengangkatnya**!*

2. **Prior Plans or Intentions Already Decided Before Speaking 🗓️** *(Rencana atau niat yang sudah diputuskan sebelum berbicara)*
   * **EN:** *We **are going to visit** Manado next month.* (Tickets or plans were already thought of ➡️ uses `be going to`.)
   * **ID:** *Kami **akan mengunjungi** Manado bulan depan.*

3. **Predictions Based on Opinion or Belief 🔮** *(Prediksi berdasarkan opini, perkiraan, atau keyakinan pribadi)*
   * **EN:** *I think our team **will win** the match.*
   * **ID:** *Saya rasa tim kita **akan memenangkan** pertandingan itu.*

4. **Predictions Based on Present Evidence 👀** *(Prediksi berdasarkan bukti atau tanda yang terlihat sekarang)*
   * **EN:** *Look at those dark clouds! It **is going to rain** soon.* (Evidence: dark clouds right now ➡️ uses `be going to`.)
   * **ID:** *Lihat awan gelap itu! Hari **akan segera hujan**.*

5. **Promises, Offers, and Requests 🤝** *(Janji, tawaran bantuan, dan permintaan)*
   * **EN:** *Don't worry, I **will help** you with your assignment.*
   * **ID:** *Jangan khawatir, saya **akan membantu** kamu mengerjakan tugasmu.*

6. **Future Facts That We Cannot Control 🌍** *(Fakta masa depan yang tidak bisa kita kendalikan)*
   * **EN:** *The sun **will rise** at 5:35 AM tomorrow.*
   * **ID:** *Matahari **akan terbit** pukul 05.35 besok pagi.*

> ⚠️ **Quick Tip on "Will" vs. "Be Going To" (Tips Cepat: "Will" vs. "Be Going To"):**
> * **Will** ➡️ instant decision, promise, offer, or general opinion. *(Keputusan dadakan, janji, tawaran, atau pendapat umum.)*
> * **Be going to** ➡️ pre-planned intention or strong visual evidence right now. *(Rencana yang sudah ada sebelumnya atau bukti nyata yang terlihat sekarang.)*

## 🧩 2. Formulas: Positive, Negative, Interrogative (Verbal & Nominal)

Good news: **will** stays the same for **all subjects** (*I, you, we, they, he, she, it*), while **be going to** changes **be** according to the subject:
* **I** ➡️ `am going to` (`I'm going to`)
* **He, She, It** (singular) ➡️ `is going to` (`he's / she's / it's going to`)
* **You, We, They** (plural) ➡️ `are going to` (`you're / we're / they're going to`)

### A. Verbal Type (Kalimat Verbal — Uses Action Verbs / Kata Kerja Aksi)
Verbal sentences use **Verb 1 (Base Form / Kata Kerja Dasar)** ➡️ *e.g., go, eat, study, write*.

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with "Will" | Formula with "Be Going To" | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + will + Verb 1 + Object/Complement` | `Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb 1 + Object/Complement` | **EN:** *They **will arrive** tomorrow morning.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka **akan tiba** besok pagi.* |
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + will not (won't) + Verb 1 + Object/Complement` | `Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + Verb 1 + Object/Complement` | **EN:** *She **will not (won't) join** the meeting.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **tidak akan ikut** rapat itu.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | `Will + Subject + Verb 1 + Object/Complement?` | `Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + Verb 1 + Object/Complement?` | **EN:** ***Will** you **call** me tonight?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** kamu **akan menelepon** saya malam ini?* |

---

### B. Nominal Type (Kalimat Nominal — No Action Verb / Tanpa Kata Kerja Aksi)
When there is **no action verb** (using adjectives, nouns, or adverbial phrases of place), use **be** right after `will` or `going to`.

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with "Will" | Formula with "Be Going To" | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + will be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place` | `Subject + am/is/are + going to be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place` | **EN:** *He **will be** a doctor next year.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **akan menjadi** seorang dokter tahun depan.* |
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + will not (won't) be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place` | `Subject + am/is/are + not + going to be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place` | **EN:** *We **won't be** at home this evening.*<br>**ID:** *Kami **tidak akan berada** di rumah sore ini.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | `Will + Subject + be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place?` | `Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to be + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place?` | **EN:** ***Are** you **going to be** ready by 7:00?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** kamu **akan siap** pukul 07.00?* |

> 📌 **Helpful Reminder (Catatan Penting):**
> Never put **Verb-ing** or **Verb 3** right after `will` or `be going to`! Always use **Verb 1** (or **be** for nominal sentences).
> * ❌ *I will going to school.* ➡️ ✅ *I **will go** to school.* *(Saya akan pergi ke sekolah.)*
> * ❌ *She will happy.* ➡️ ✅ *She **will be** happy.* *(Dia akan senang.)*

## ⏰ 3. Important Time Signals (Penanda Waktu Penting)

Time signals often appear at the beginning or end of a sentence to show future time:

| Time Signal | Meaning in Indonesian (Arti) | Example (EN & ID) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tomorrow** | besok | **EN:** *I **will visit** my grandmother **tomorrow**.*<br>**ID:** *Saya **akan mengunjungi** nenek saya **besok**.* |
| **Tonight** | malam ini | **EN:** *We **are going to watch** a movie **tonight**.*<br>**ID:** *Kami **akan menonton** film **malam ini**.* |
| **Next week / month / year** | minggu / bulan / tahun depan | **EN:** *They **will launch** the new app **next month**.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka **akan meluncurkan** aplikasi baru **bulan depan**.* |
| **Soon** | segera / sebentar lagi | **EN:** *The train **will arrive soon**.*<br>**ID:** *Kereta itu **akan segera tiba**.* |
| **Later** | nanti / kemudian | **EN:** *I **will send** you the file **later**.*<br>**ID:** *Saya **akan mengirimkan** filenya **nanti**.* |
| **In + period of time** *(e.g., in two days, in 2028)* | dalam + jangka waktu *(dalam dua hari, pada tahun 2028)* | **EN:** *The results **will be** announced **in three days**.*<br>**ID:** *Hasilnya **akan** diumumkan **dalam tiga hari**.* |
| **This afternoon / weekend** | sore / akhir pekan ini | **EN:** *Rina **is going to bake** a cake **this weekend**.*<br>**ID:** *Rina **akan memanggang** kue **akhir pekan ini**.* |

## 📝 4. Self-Study Quiz (Latihan Mandiri)

Complete all **10 questions** before checking the answer key below! ✍️

### Part A: Multiple Choice (Pilihan Ganda)
1. "I forgot my umbrella!" ➡️ "Don't worry, I _______ you mine."
   a) lend ➡️ b) am lending ➡️ c) will lend ➡️ d) lent
2. Look at that boy riding his bicycle way too fast! He _______ fall.
   a) is going to ➡️ b) will be ➡️ c) going to ➡️ d) was
3. _______ your parents _______ at the ceremony tomorrow? *(Nominal)*
   a) Will / be ➡️ b) Are / going ➡️ c) Will / are ➡️ d) Do / be
4. My cousin _______ graduate from university next year.
   a) going to ➡️ b) will ➡️ c) is ➡️ d) has

### Part B: Fill in the Blanks (Isilah Titik-Titik)
5. We _______ (not / stay) at the hotel tonight because we changed our plan. *(Verbal, Negative)*
6. _______ you _______ (be) free this Sunday afternoon? *(Nominal, Interrogative)*
7. I promise I _______ (never / tell) your secret to anyone. *(Verbal, Positive)*

### Part C: Translate & Identify Type (Terjemahkan & Tentukan Jenis Kalimat)
8. **EN:** *They will not be late for the morning flight.* ➡️ Translate to Indonesian & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
9. **ID:** *Apakah dia (laki-laki) akan membeli buku baru besok?* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
10. **ID:** *Kami akan siap dalam sepuluh menit.* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.

---

### ✅ Answer Key & Explanations (Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan)

1. **c) will lend** ➡️ Spontaneous offer made at the moment of speaking (`will` + Verb 1 `lend`). *(Saya akan meminjamkan milik saya kepadamu.)*
2. **a) is going to** ➡️ Prediction based on visible evidence right now (`is going to` + Verb 1 `fall`). *(Dia akan jatuh.)*
3. **a) Will / be** ➡️ Nominal interrogative uses `Will` + subject + `be` (no action verb). *(Apakah orang tuamu akan hadir di upacara besok?)*
4. **b) will** ➡️ Future statement/prediction uses `will` + Verb 1 `graduate`. *(Sepupu saya akan lulus kuliah tahun depan.)*
5. **will not (won't) stay** / **are not (aren't) going to stay** ➡️ Negative verbal sentence with Verb 1 `stay`. *(Kami tidak akan menginap di hotel malam ini karena kami mengubah rencana kami.)*
6. **Will / be** (or **Are / going to be**) ➡️ Nominal interrogative with adjective `free`. *(Apakah kamu akan senggang Minggu sore ini?)*
7. **will never tell** ➡️ Promise/future statement with `will` + `never` + Verb 1 `tell`. *(Saya berjanji tidak akan pernah memberi tahu rahasiamu kepada siapa pun.)*
8. **Nominal** ➡️ **ID:** *Mereka tidak akan terlambat untuk penerbangan pagi.* *(Uses `be` + adjective `late`, no action verb.)*
9. **Verbal** ➡️ **EN:** *Will he buy a new book tomorrow?* *(Uses action verb `buy`.)*
10. **Nominal** ➡️ **EN:** *We will be ready in ten minutes.* *(or "We are going to be ready in ten minutes." Uses `be` + adjective `ready`.)*
