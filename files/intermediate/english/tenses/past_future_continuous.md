# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Past Future Continuous Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Past Future Continuous Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to talk about an activity that **would be in progress at a specific future moment from a past point of view**—like *"Yesterday he said he **would be traveling** to Surabaya at this time today"* or *"By 9:00 PM last night, I **would have been...**"*? Wait—no! For an action **in progress** at a past-projected future time, we use **`would be + V-ing`** or **`was/were going to be + V-ing`**! That is the **Past Future Continuous Tense** (also called **Future Continuous in the Past**) ⏳!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin membicarakan suatu aktivitas yang **akan sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa depan dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu**—seperti "Yesterday he said he **would be traveling** to Surabaya at this time today"? Itulah **Past Future Continuous Tense**—juga disebut **Future Continuous in the Past** ⏳!)*

Think of it like this: **in the past**, someone looked ahead to a later moment and pictured an action **still underway (in the middle of happening)** at that moment ➡️!

*(Anggap saja begini: **di masa lalu**, seseorang melihat ke depan menuju suatu momen berikutnya dan membayangkan suatu aktivitas yang **masih berjalan / sedang di tengah proses** pada saat itu ➡️!)*

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Past Future Continuous

**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Past Future Continuous)**

Use the **Past Future Continuous Tense** in **three main situations**:

*(Gunakan **Past Future Continuous Tense** dalam **tiga situasi utama**:)*

1. **Action in Progress at a Future Point Viewed from the Past (Aksi yang Akan Sedang Berlangsung pada Waktu Tertentu Dilihat dari Masa Lalu):**

   * *Example:* She told me she **would be working** at 10:00 AM this morning.

   * *(Dia memberi tahu saya bahwa dia **akan sedang bekerja** pada pukul 10.00 pagi ini.)*

2. **Ongoing Future Action Interrupted / Met by Another Event from a Past Viewpoint (Aksi Berkelanjutan yang Dibayangkan Sedang Berjalan Saat Kejadian Lain Terjadi):**

   * *Example:* I knew he **would be sleeping** when we arrived at his house.

   * *(Saya sudah tahu dia **akan sedang tidur** ketika kami tiba di rumahnya.)*

3. **Past Plans or Expectations About a Continuous Process (Rencana atau Ekspektasi Masa Lalu tentang Proses yang Berlangsung):**

   * *Example:* We **were going to be practicing** all afternoon, so we moved our meeting to the morning.

   * *(Kami **tadinya akan sedang berlatih** sepanjang sore, jadi kami memindahkan rapat ke pagi hari.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Is this a **past viewpoint** looking forward to an action **in progress (`V-ing`)** rather than a single completed action?"* If yes, use **`would be + V-ing`** or **`was/were going to be + V-ing`**! ✅
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah ini **sudut pandang masa lalu** yang melihat ke depan pada suatu aktivitas **yang sedang berlangsung (`V-ing`)**, bukan tindakan tunggal yang selesai?" Jika ya, gunakan **`would be + V-ing`** atau **`was/were going to be + V-ing`**! ✅)*

## 2. Two Main Forms: `Would Be + V-ing` vs. `Was/Were Going To Be + V-ing`

**(Dua Bentuk Utama: `Would Be + V-ing` vs. `Was/Were Going To Be + V-ing`)**

* **`Would be + V-ing`:** The most common form in storytelling, indirect/reported speech (*said, told, thought, knew*), and beliefs about ongoing future actions from a past perspective. Same for **all subjects** (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`).
  *(Bentuk yang paling umum dalam cerita, kalimat tidak langsung seperti said, told, thought, atau knew, dan keyakinan tentang aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung dari sudut pandang masa lalu. Sama untuk **semua subjek**.)*

* **`Was / Were going to be + V-ing`:** Used for past intentions or arrangements involving an ongoing activity (especially when plans shifted or were explained in context).
  *(Dipakai untuk niat atau pengaturan di masa lalu yang melibatkan aktivitas berkelanjutan—terutama saat rencana bergeser atau dijelaskan dalam konteks.)*

  * Use **`was`** for: `I, He, She, It`, singular nouns (*Budi*).

  * Use **`were`** for: `You, We, They`, plural nouns (*the students*).

### Sentence Formula Table (Tabel Rumus Kalimat)

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with `Would Be` | Formula with `Was/Were Going To Be` | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Positive (+)** | `S + would be + V-ing` | `S + was/were going to be + V-ing` | *He said he **would be waiting** at the lobby.* <br> *They **were going to be traveling** all week.* | 
| **Negative (-)** | `S + would not (wouldn't) be + V-ing` | `S + was/were not going to be + V-ing` | *She told me she **wouldn't be working** on Sunday.* <br> *I **wasn't going to be staying** long.* | 
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Would + S + be + V-ing?` | `Was/Were + S + going to be + V-ing?` | ***Would** you **be studying** at 8:00 PM?* <br> ***Were** they **going to be playing** then?* | 
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + would + S + be + V-ing?` | `Wh-word + was/were + S + going to be + V-ing?` | *What **would** she **be doing** at noon?* <br> *Where **was** he **going to be staying**?* | 

## 3. Signal Words & Context Clues

**(Penanda Waktu & Petunjuk Konteks yang Sering Muncul)**

Past Future Continuous usually combines a **past anchor** with a **specific time or ongoing timeframe**:

*(Past Future Continuous biasanya menggabungkan **jangkar waktu lampau** dengan **waktu spesifik atau rentang waktu yang sedang berjalan**:)*

* **Past Reporting / Thought Verbs:** *said, told, thought, knew, believed, promised, explained, assumed* *(berkata, memberi tahu, berpikir, tahu, percaya, berjanji, menjelaskan, mengira)*

* **Specific Moment / Ongoing Markers:** *at that time, at 9:00 AM, all afternoon, all evening, when + Simple Past* *(pada saat itu, pukul 09.00, sepanjang sore, sepanjang malam, ketika...)*

* **Context Shift Clues:** *yesterday, earlier today, last week, before the trip* *(kemarin, tadi pagi/siang, minggu lalu, sebelum perjalanan)*

## 4. Past Future Continuous vs. Past Future Simple

**(Perbandingan: Past Future Continuous vs. Past Future Simple)**

| Feature (Aspek) | Past Future Continuous (`would be + V-ing`) | Past Future Simple (`would + V1`) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Focus (Fokus)** | **Activity in progress** at that moment *(Aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung)* | **Action/decision/event** as a whole *(Tindakan, keputusan, atau kejadian secara umum)* | 
| **Example 1 (Study)** | *She said she **would be studying** at 8:00 PM.* *(In the middle of studying at 8:00)* | *She said she **would study** at 8:00 PM.* *(Plan/promise to study at 8:00)* | 
| **Example 2 (Arrival)** | *I knew they **would be arriving** around noon.* *(Process of arriving around noon)* | *I knew they **would arrive** before noon.* *(Event of arriving)* | 

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context

**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Reported Ongoing Action:**
   ➡️ Rina told me she **would be practicing** the piano at 4:00 PM.
   *(Rina memberi tahu saya bahwa dia **akan sedang berlatih** piano pada pukul 16.00.)*

2. **Past Belief About a Current/Later Process:**
   ➡️ I figured you **would be cooking** dinner when I got home, so I didn't buy food.
   *(Saya mengira kamu **akan sedang memasak** makan malam ketika saya sampai di rumah, jadi saya tidak membeli makanan.)*

3. **Negative Form (`wouldn't be + V-ing`):**
   ➡️ The manager said the team **wouldn't be holding** meetings on Friday afternoon.
   *(Manajer mengatakan bahwa tim **tidak akan sedang mengadakan** rapat pada Jumat sore.)*

4. **Using `Was / Were Going To Be + V-ing`:**
   ➡️ We **were going to be camping** by the lake this weekend, but the weather forecast looked stormy.
   *(Kami **tadinya akan sedang berkemah** di tepi danau akhir pekan ini, tetapi prakiraan cuaca tampak badai.)*

5. **Question Form (`Would + S + be + V-ing`):**
   ➡️ Did you ask if he **would be driving** to Bandung on Saturday?
   *(Apakah kamu bertanya apakah dia **akan sedang menyetir** ke Bandung pada hari Sabtu?)*

## 6. Crucial Traps & Spelling Rules to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting & Aturan Ejaan yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Forgetting `be` (`would + V-ing` ❌).**
  *(Jebakan 1: Lupa menyertakan kata `be` setelah `would`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He said he would **working** at 9:00 AM.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He said he would **be working** at 9:00 AM.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using Stative Verbs in `-ing` Form.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai kata kerja keadaan/perasaan dalam bentuk `-ing`.)*
  Verbs like **`know`, `like`, `love`, `want`, `need`, `understand`, `believe`, `own`** normally use **`would + V1`** (Past Future Simple), not `would be + V-ing`.

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She thought he would be **knowing** the answer.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She thought he would **know** the answer.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `would be + V-ing` with `would have + V3`.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Tertukar antara `would be + V-ing` dan `would have + V3`.)*
  Use **`would be + V-ing`** for an ongoing action at a future point from a past view; use **`would have + V3`** for an unreal past result (*I would have called if I had known*).

* 📝 **Quick `V-ing` Spelling Reminders:**

  * Drop silent **`-e`**: *write* ➡️ **writing**, *make* ➡️ **making**

  * Double final consonant (CVC short vowel): *sit* ➡️ **sitting**, *plan* ➡️ **planning**, *swim* ➡️ **swimming**

  * Change **`-ie`** to **`-y`**: *lie* ➡️ **lying**

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below! ✍️

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah! ✍️)*

1. Yesterday, Budi said he \_____________\_ (would be waiting / will be waiting / would waiting) for us at the café at 3:00 PM.
   *(Kemarin, Budi bilang dia akan sedang menunggu kita di kafe pada pukul 15.00.)*

2. I knew it \_____________\_ (would be raining / would rain / was rain) heavily when we left the stadium, so I brought an umbrella.
   *(Saya sudah tahu hari akan sedang hujan deras ketika kami meninggalkan stadion, jadi saya membawa payung.)*

3. She told me she \_____________\_ (wouldn't be using / won't be using / wouldn't use) her car this morning, so I could borrow it.
   *(Dia memberi tahu saya bahwa dia tidak akan sedang memakai mobilnya pagi ini, jadi saya bisa meminjamnya.)*

4. They \_____________\_ (were going to be traveling / was going to be traveling / were going to travel) all night, but they decided to take a morning flight instead.
   *(Mereka tadinya akan sedang bepergian sepanjang malam, tetapi mereka memutuskan naik penerbangan pagi saja.)*

5. Did she mention whether she \_____________\_ (would be attending / would attend / is attending) the workshop at 2:00 PM?
   *(Apakah dia menyebutkan apakah dia akan sedang menghadiri lokakarya pada pukul 14.00?)*

6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*

   * A) He said he would be needing more time.

   * B) He said he would need more time.

   * C) He said he would be need more time.

7. We thought the technicians \_____________\_ (would be testing / will test / would test) the equipment all afternoon.
   *(Kami mengira para teknisi akan sedang menguji peralatan itu sepanjang sore.)*

8. What \_____________\_ (would you be doing / you would be doing / did you be doing) at 7:00 PM if you hadn't gone out?
   *(Apa yang akan sedang kamu lakukan pukul 19.00 jika kamu tidak pergi keluar?)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **would be waiting** ✅
   ➡️ Sudut pandang masa lalu (`Yesterday, Budi said...`) menuju aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada jam tertentu (`at 3:00 PM`) menggunakan **`would be + V-ing`**.

2. **would be raining** ✅
   ➡️ Menunjukkan kondisi cuaca yang sedang berlangsung saat kejadian lain terjadi (`when we left the stadium`) dari sudut pandang masa lalu (`I knew...`).

3. **wouldn't be using** ✅
   ➡️ Bentuk negatif Past Future Continuous (`would not be + V-ing`) setelah kata kerja pelapor lampau `told me`.

4. **were going to be traveling** ✅
   ➡️ Subjek jamak `They` dengan bentuk rencana masa lalu yang berubah (`were going to be + V-ing`).

5. **would be attending** ✅
   ➡️ Menyatakan aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung pada pukul 14.00 dilihat dari titik acuan masa lalu (`Did she mention...`).

6. **B) He said he would need more time.** ✅
   ➡️ `Need` adalah *stative verb*, sehingga lebih tepat menggunakan Past Future Simple (`would need`) daripada bentuk continuous.

7. **would be testing** ✅
   ➡️ Menunjukkan proses pengujian yang berlangsung terus sepanjang sore (`all afternoon`) dari sudut pandang masa lalu (`We thought...`).

8. **would you be doing** ✅
   ➡️ Kalimat tanya `Wh-` Past Future Continuous menggunakan urutan `Wh-word + would + subjek + be + V-ing`.
