# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Simple Past Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Simple Past Tense)**

Whenever you want to talk about something that **started and finished completely in the past**—like *"I **watched** a great movie last night"* or *"We **went** to Bali two years ago"*—you use the **Simple Past Tense**!

*(Kapan pun kamu ingin membicarakan sesuatu yang **dimulai dan selesai sepenuhnya di masa lalu**—seperti "I **watched** a great movie last night" atau "We **went** to Bali two years ago"—kamu menggunakan **Simple Past Tense**!)*

The great news for self-learners is that in the Simple Past Tense, **all subjects (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`) use the exact same verb form (`V2` or `did`)**—except for `was` vs. `were`!

*(Kabar baik bagi pembelajar mandiri adalah pada Simple Past Tense, **semua subjek (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`) menggunakan bentuk kata kerja yang sama persis (`V2` atau `did`)**—kecuali untuk perbedaan `was` dan `were`!)*

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Simple Past

**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Simple Past)**

Use the Simple Past Tense in **four main situations**:

*(Gunakan Simple Past Tense dalam **empat situasi utama**:)*

1. **Completed Past Actions at a Specific Time (Tindakan Selesai di Waktu Lampau yang Jelas):**

   * *Example:* She **finished** her project **yesterday evening**.

   * *(Dia **menyelesaikan** proyeknya **kemarin sore**.)*

2. **A Sequence of Finished Events in Storytelling (Urutan Kejadian di Masa Lalu / Cerita):**

   * *Example:* I **woke** up, **brushed** my teeth, and **walked** to the bus stop.

   * *(Saya **bangun**, **menggosok** gigi, dan **berjalan** ke halte bus.)*

3. **Past Habits or Routines That Are No Longer True (Kebiasaan Masa Lalu yang Sudah Berhenti):**

   * *Example:* When I was a child, I **played** outside every afternoon.

   * *(Ketika saya masih kecil, saya **bermain** di luar setiap sore.)*

4. **Past Facts or States (Fakta atau Keadaan di Masa Lalu):**

   * *Example:* They **lived** in Surabaya from 2018 to 2021.

   * *(Mereka **tinggal** di Surabaya dari tahun 2018 hingga 2021.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Is this action **100% finished** and disconnected from right now?"* If yes, use the **Simple Past Tense**!
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah tindakan ini **100% sudah selesai** dan terputus dari waktu sekarang?" Jika ya, gunakan **Simple Past Tense**!)*

## 2. Two Types of Sentences: Verbal vs. Nominal

**(Dua Jenis Kalimat: Verbal vs. Nominal)**

Just like the Simple Present, Simple Past sentences are split into **Verbal Sentences** (with action verbs like *ate, studied, went*) and **Nominal Sentences** (without action verbs—describing past identity, job, feeling, or location using **`was / were`**).

*(Seperti halnya Simple Present, kalimat Simple Past dibagi menjadi **Kalimat Verbal**—dengan kata kerja aksi seperti ate, studied, went—dan **Kalimat Nominal**—tanpa kata kerja aksi yang menjelaskan identitas, pekerjaan, perasaan, atau lokasi di masa lalu menggunakan **`was / were`**.)*

### Part A: Verbal Sentences (Action Verbs — `V2` & `did`)

**(Kalimat Verbal — Menggunakan Kata Kerja Aksi)**

* **Positive (+) Rule:** Use **`V2` (Past Simple form)** for **all subjects**. ➡️ *She **bought** a new laptop.* / *They **bought** a new laptop.*

* **Negative (-) & Question (?) Rule:** Use **`did` / `didn't`**, and return the main verb **back to `V1` (Base Form)**!

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Positive (+)** | `Subject + V2` | *I **visited** my grandparents last weekend.* *(Saya mengunjungi kakek-nenek saya akhir pekan lalu.)* | 
| **Negative (-)** | `Subject + did not (didn't) + V1` | *He **didn't attend** the meeting yesterday.* *(Dia tidak menghadiri rapat kemarin.)* | 
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Did + Subject + V1?` | ***Did** you **sleep** well last night?* *(Apakah kamu tidur nyenyak tadi malam?)* | 
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + did + Subject + V1?` | *Where **did** they **go** on vacation?* *(Ke mana mereka pergi berlibur?)* | 

### Part B: Nominal Sentences (`To Be`: `was` / `were` — No Action Verb!)

**(Kalimat Nominal — Tanpa Kata Kerja Aksi, Menggunakan `was / were`)**

Use **`was`** or **`were`** when the sentence is followed by a **Noun**, **Adjective**, or **Adverb of Place/Time**. **Never use `did` or `didn't` in nominal sentences!**

*(Gunakan **`was`** atau **`were`** ketika kalimat diikuti **Kata Benda**, **Kata Sifat**, atau **Keterangan Tempat/Waktu**. **Jangan pernah memakai `did` atau `didn't` dalam kalimat nominal!**)*

| Subject (Subjek) | Positive (+) | Negative (-) | Question (?) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **`I, He, She, It`**, singular nouns | `was` | `was not` *(wasn't)* | `Was I/he/she/it...?` | *The exam **was** very easy.* *(Ujiannya sangat mudah.)* | 
| **`You, We, They`**, plural nouns | `were` | `were not` *(weren't)* | `Were you/we/they...?` | *We **were** at the library yesterday.* *(Kami ada di perpustakaan kemarin.)* | 

## 3. Regular Verbs (`-ed`) vs. Irregular Verbs (`V2`)

**(Kata Kerja Beraturan vs. Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan)**

### A. Regular Verbs: Spelling Rules for `-ed` (Aturan Ejaan `-ed`)

| Rule (Aturan) | Base Verb (`V1`) | Past Form (`V2`) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **1. General Rule:** Add **`-ed`** *(Aturan umum: tambah `-ed`)* | *work, walk, clean, listen* | **worked, walked, cleaned, listened** | *She **cleaned** her bedroom this morning.* | 
| **2. Ends in `-e`:** Just add **`-d`** *(Akhiran `-e`: cukup tambah `-d`)* | *live, like, arrive, dance* | **lived, liked, arrived, danced** | *The train **arrived** ten minutes late.* | 
| **3. Consonant + `-y`:** Change `-y` to **`-ied`** *(Konsonan + `-y`: ubah jadi `-ied`)* | *study, try, cry, carry* | **studied, tried, cried, carried** | *He **studied** hard for the test.* | 
| **4. Short Vowel + 1 Consonant (CVC):** Double final consonant + **`-ed`** *(Gandakan huruf akhir)* | *stop, plan, drop, chat* | **stopped, planned, dropped, chatted** | *The bus **stopped** right in front of the school.* | 

### B. 20 Common Irregular Verbs You Must Know (`V1` ➡️ `V2`)

*(20 Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan yang Wajib Diketahui)*

Irregular verbs do **not** end in `-ed`—their `V2` form changes uniquely:

*(Kata kerja tidak beraturan **tidak** berakhiran `-ed`—bentuk `V2`-nya berubah secara khusus:)*

| `V1` (Base) | `V2` (Past) | Meaning (Arti) | `V1` (Base) | `V2` (Past) | Meaning (Arti) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **go** | **went** | pergi | **take** | **took** | mengambil | 
| **come** | **came** | datang | **give** | **gave** | memberi | 
| **see** | **saw** | melihat | **find** | **found** | menemukan | 
| **eat** | **ate** | makan | **think** | **thought** | berpikir | 
| **drink** | **drank** | minum | **know** | **knew** | tahu / mengenal | 
| **buy** | **bought** | membeli | **tell** | **told** | memberi tahu | 
| **bring** | **brought** | membawa | **leave** | **left** | meninggalkan / berangkat | 
| **write** | **wrote** | menulis | **feel** | **felt** | merasa | 
| **read** | **read** *(dibaca "red")* | membaca | **meet** | **met** | bertemu | 
| **make** | **made** | membuat | **begin** | **began** | memulai | 

## 4. Signal Words (Kata Penanda Waktu Lampau)

**(Penanda Waktu yang Menunjukkan Simple Past)**

When you see these time expressions, you know the action happened in the past:

*(Ketika kamu melihat ungkapan waktu ini, kamu tahu tindakannya terjadi di masa lalu:)*

* **`Yesterday`** *(kemarin)* ➡️ *yesterday morning, yesterday afternoon, yesterday evening*

* **`Last ...`** *(... yang lalu)* ➡️ *last night, last week, last month, last year, last Sunday*

* **`... ago`** *(... yang lalu)* ➡️ *five minutes ago, two days ago, three years ago*

* **`In + Past Year/Month`** *(pada tahun/bulan lampau)* ➡️ *in 2020, in July 1999*

* **`When I was ...`** *(ketika saya masih ...)* ➡️ *when I was a child, when I was in high school*

## 5. Crucial Traps to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using `V2` after `did` or `didn't`.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai `V2` setelah `did` atau `didn't`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I **didn't went** to school yesterday. / **Did** she **bought** the book?

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I **didn't go** to school yesterday. / **Did** she **buy** the book?
    *(Begitu ada `did` atau `didn't`, kata kerja utama kembali ke **bentuk dasar `V1`**.)*

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `didn't` in Nominal Sentences (with adjectives, nouns, or locations).**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai `didn't` pada kalimat nominal yang tidak memiliki kata kerja aksi.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* They **didn't** at home last night. / She **didn't** happy.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* They **weren't** at home last night. / She **wasn't** happy.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Mixing `was / were` with a base action verb (`V1`).**
  *(Jebakan 3: Mencampur `was / were` dengan kata kerja aksi dasar `V1`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He **was go** to Jakarta last week.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He **went** to Jakarta last week.

## 6. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. We \_____________\_ (watch / watched / watching) a documentary about space **last night**.
   *(Kami menonton film dokumenter tentang luar angkasa tadi malam.)*

2. She \_____________\_ (didn't go / didn't went / wasn't go) to the office yesterday because she had a fever.
   *(Dia tidak pergi ke kantor kemarin karena dia demam.)*

3. \_____________\_ (Did / Was / Were) you \_____________\_ (see / saw / seen) Rina at the bookstore two hours ago?
   *(Apakah kamu melihat Rina di toko buku dua jam yang lalu?)*

4. My parents \_____________\_ (was / were / did) very proud when I graduated from college.
   *(Orang tua saya sangat bangga ketika saya lulus kuliah.)*

5. Budi \_____________\_ (buy / buyed / bought) a secondhand bicycle last Sunday.
   *(Budi membeli sepeda bekas hari Minggu lalu.)*

6. The traffic \_____________\_ (wasn't / didn't / weren't) heavy this morning, so we arrived early.
   *(Lalu lintas tidak padat pagi ini, jadi kami tiba lebih awal.)*

7. They \_____________\_ (study / studied / studyed) together at the café until 9:00 PM.
   *(Mereka belajar bersama di kafe hingga pukul 9 malam.)*

8. What time \_____________\_ (did the concert start / the concert started / did the concert started)?
   *(Pukul berapa konsernya dimulai?)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **watched**
   ➡️ Kata kerja beraturan (*regular verb* `watch` ➡️ `watched`) untuk kejadian selesai tadi malam (`last night`).

2. **didn't go**
   ➡️ Kalimat negatif verbal masa lalu memakai **`didn't` + `V1`** (`go`, bukan `went`).

3. **Did ... see**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan verbal masa lalu menggunakan **`Did` + subjek + `V1`** (`see`, bukan `saw`).

4. **were**
   ➡️ Kalimat nominal (diikuti kata sifat `proud`) dengan subjek jamak (`My parents` ➡️ `were`).

5. **bought**
   ➡️ Bentuk `V2` tidak beraturan (*irregular verb*) dari `buy` adalah **`bought`** (bukan `buyed`).

6. **wasn't**
   ➡️ Kalimat nominal (diikuti kata sifat `heavy`) dengan subjek tunggal/uncountable (`The traffic` ➡️ `wasn't`).

7. **studied**
   ➡️ Kata kerja beraturan berakhiran konsonan + `-y` (`study` ➡️ **`studied`**).

8. **did the concert start**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan `Wh-` masa lalu mengikuti pola `Wh-word + did + subjek + V1` (`start`).
