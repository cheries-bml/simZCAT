# 📘 Self-Study Guide: Past Perfect Tense
**Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Past Perfect Tense**

Welcome to your self-study guide on the **Past Perfect Tense**! 🌟 If you imagine two events happening in the past, the **Past Perfect Tense** helps you show which event happened **first (earlier)** before another past event happened. Take your time reading each section, study the examples in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and test yourself with the quiz at the end.

> 💡 **Core Idea (Ide Utama):** Past Perfect Tense is used to describe an action, event, or state that was **completed before another specific time or event in the past**.
> *(Past Perfect Tense digunakan untuk menjelaskan aksi, kejadian, atau keadaan yang sudah **selesai sebelum waktu atau kejadian tertentu lainnya di masa lalu**.)*

---

## 🎯 1. Usage Situations: When to Use Past Perfect Tense (Situasi Penggunaan)

Use the Past Perfect Tense in these **five main situations**:

1. **An Action Completed Before Another Past Action ➡️** *(Aksi yang sudah selesai sebelum aksi masa lalu lainnya terjadi)*
   * **EN:** *The train **had already left** when we arrived at the station.* (First: the train left ➡️ Second: we arrived.)
   * **ID:** *Kereta itu **sudah berangkat** ketika kami tiba di stasiun.*

2. **An Action Completed Before a Specific Time in the Past ⏰** *(Aksi yang sudah selesai sebelum waktu tertentu di masa lalu)*
   * **EN:** *By 8:00 PM last night, she **had finished** her project.* (Before 8:00 PM ➡️ the project was finished.)
   * **ID:** *Pada pukul 20.00 tadi malam, dia **sudah menyelesaikan** proyeknya.*

3. **Cause and Effect in the Past 🔍** *(Hubungan sebab-akibat di masa lalu)*
   * **EN:** *He was exhausted because he **had worked** for fourteen hours.* (Cause: worked 14 hours ➡️ Effect: was exhausted.)
   * **ID:** *Dia kelelahan karena dia **telah bekerja** selama empat belas jam.*

4. **Reported Speech / Indirect Speech 💬** *(Kalimat tidak langsung / pelaporan ucapan di masa lalu)*
   * **EN:** *Rina told me that she **had visited** Manado the previous month.* (Original direct speech: *"I visited Manado last month."*)
   * **ID:** *Rina memberi tahu saya bahwa dia **telah mengunjungi** Manado bulan sebelumnya.*

5. **Unfulfilled Wishes, Hopes, or Conditionals in the Past 💭** *(Harapan, keinginan, atau pengandaian yang tidak terwujud di masa lalu)*
   * **EN:** *I **had hoped** to meet you at the seminar, but I got stuck in traffic.*
   * **ID:** *Saya **tadinya berharap** bisa bertemu kamu di seminar, tetapi saya terjebak macet.*

> ⚠️ **Quick Contrast (Perbandingan Cepat):**
> * **Simple Past:** *When I arrived at the cinema, the movie **started**.* ➡️ Both happened around the same time or the movie started right after I arrived. *(Ketika saya tiba di bioskop, filmnya **dimulai**.)*
> * **Past Perfect:** *When I arrived at the cinema, the movie **had started**.* ➡️ The movie started **first** (before I arrived), so I missed the beginning. *(Ketika saya tiba di bioskop, filmnya **sudah dimulai**.)*

## 🧩 2. Formulas: Positive, Negative, Interrogative (Verbal & Nominal)

Great news for learners: unlike Present Perfect (`have` vs. `has`), Past Perfect uses **had** for **all subjects** (*I, you, we, they, he, she, it*, singular, and plural)! ➡️ `had + not = hadn't`.

### A. Verbal Type (Kalimat Verbal — Uses Action Verbs / Kata Kerja Aksi)
Verbal sentences use **had + Verb 3 (Past Participle)** ➡️ *e.g., gone, eaten, left, written*.

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | **Subject + had + Verb 3 + Object/Complement** | **EN:** *They **had eaten** dinner before I came home.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka **sudah makan** malam sebelum saya pulang ke rumah.* |
| **Negative (-)** | **Subject + had + not + Verb 3 + Object/Complement** | **EN:** *He **had not (hadn't) studied** French before moving to Paris.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **belum belajar** bahasa Prancis sebelum pindah ke Paris.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | **Had + Subject + Verb 3 + Object/Complement?** | **EN:** ***Had** she **saved** enough money before buying the laptop?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** dia **sudah menabung** cukup uang sebelum membeli laptop itu?* |

---

### B. Nominal Type (Kalimat Nominal — No Action Verb / Tanpa Kata Kerja Aksi)
When there is **no action verb** (using adjectives, nouns, or adverbial phrases of place), use **had + been** as the participle of *to be* (`am/is/are/was/were` ➡️ `been`).

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example & Translation (Contoh & Terjemahan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | **Subject + had + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place** | **EN:** *Before the repair, the road **had been** very bumpy.*<br>**ID:** *Sebelum diperbaiki, jalan itu **sudah** sangat bergelombang.* |
| **Negative (-)** | **Subject + had + not + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place** | **EN:** *We **had not (hadn't) been** there before last summer.*<br>**ID:** *Kami **belum pernah ke** sana sebelum musim panas lalu.* |
| **Interrogative (?)** | **Had + Subject + been + Adjective/Noun/Adverb of Place?** | **EN:** ***Had** you **been** a manager long before you resigned?*<br>**ID:** ***Apakah** kamu **sudah lama menjadi** manajer sebelum kamu mengundurkan diri?* |

> 📌 **Helpful Tip on Timeline Order:**
> Look for the **earlier past action** ➡️ that action usually takes **Past Perfect (`had + Verb 3` / `had + been`)**.
> Look for the **later past action** ➡️ that action usually takes **Simple Past (`Verb 2` / `was/were`)**.

## ⏰ 3. Important Time Signals (Penanda Waktu Penting)

Time signals help you see the sequence of events in the past:

| Time Signal | Meaning in Indonesian (Arti) | Typical Usage & Position | Example (EN & ID) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Before** | sebelum | Often introduces the Simple Past clause (or appears at the end) | **EN:** *I **had locked** the door **before** I went to sleep.*<br>**ID:** *Saya **sudah mengunci** pintu **sebelum** saya tidur.* |
| **After** | setelah | Often introduces the Past Perfect clause showing the first action | **EN:** *We went home **after** the meeting **had ended**.*<br>**ID:** *Kami pulang **setelah** rapat itu **selesai**.* |
| **By the time** | pada saat / menjelang | Often followed by Simple Past; main clause uses Past Perfect | **EN:** ***By the time** the police arrived, the thief **had escaped**.*<br>**ID:** ***Pada saat** polisi tiba, pencuri itu **sudah melarikan diri**.* |
| **When** | ketika / saat | Connects two past events; check which happened first | **EN:** *The power went out **when** we **had just saved** the file.*<br>**ID:** *Listrik padam **ketika** kami **baru saja menyimpan** file tersebut.* |
| **Already** | sudah | Placed between `had` and `Verb 3` (or `been`) | **EN:** *The show **had already** begun when we sat down.*<br>**ID:** *Pertunjukan itu **sudah** dimulai ketika kami duduk.* |
| **Just** | baru saja | Placed between `had` and `Verb 3` | **EN:** *She **had just** fallen asleep when the phone rang.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **baru saja** tertidur ketika telepon berdering.* |
| **Until / Till** | sampai | Shows how long something continued before another past point | **EN:** *He **hadn't realized** the mistake **until** his boss pointed it out.*<br>**ID:** *Dia **belum menyadari** kesalahan itu **sampai** bosnya menunjukkannya.* |
| **By + past time** | menjelang / pada + waktu lalu | E.g., *by 2022, by yesterday afternoon, by midnight* | **EN:** *They **had built** the bridge **by** the end of 2024.*<br>**ID:** *Mereka **sudah membangun** jembatan itu **pada** akhir tahun 2024.* |

## 📝 4. Self-Study Quiz (Latihan Mandiri)

Try answering all **10 questions** before checking the answer key below! ✍️

### Part A: Multiple Choice (Pilihan Ganda)
1. When Dika called me at 9:00 AM, I _______ breakfast yet.
   a) didn't have ➡️ b) hadn't had ➡️ c) don't have ➡️ d) haven't had
2. After Tari _______ her presentation, everyone clapped.
   a) finishes ➡️ b) has finished ➡️ c) had finished ➡️ d) is finishing
3. By the time we reached the harbor, the boat _______.
   a) had sailed ➡️ b) sails ➡️ c) has sailed ➡️ d) sailing
4. Before moving to Bitung, my family _______ in a small coastal town. *(Nominal)*
   a) has been ➡️ b) had been ➡️ c) are ➡️ d) will be

### Part B: Fill in the Blanks (Isilah Titik-Titik)
5. The store _______ (already / close) when we got there. *(Verbal, Positive)*
6. He _______ (not / be) on an airplane before that trip. *(Nominal, Negative)*
7. _______ they _______ (meet) each other before the conference started? *(Verbal, Interrogative)*

### Part C: Translate & Identify Type (Terjemahkan & Tentukan Jenis Kalimat)
8. **EN:** *She had not finished her homework before the teacher collected it.* ➡️ Translate to Indonesian & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
9. **ID:** *Dia (laki-laki) sudah menjadi kepala sekolah selama tiga tahun sebelum dia pensiun.* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.
10. **ID:** *Apakah mereka sudah pergi ketika kamu menelepon?* ➡️ Translate to English & state **Verbal/Nominal**.

---

### ✅ Answer Key & Explanations (Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan)

1. **b) hadn't had** ➡️ Action completed before 9:00 AM (*called* is Simple Past). First `had` is the auxiliary; second `had` is Verb 3 of *have*. *(Ketika Dika menelepon saya pukul 09.00 pagi, saya belum sarapan.)*
2. **c) had finished** ➡️ Finishing the presentation happened first (`had finished`), followed by clapping (`clapped`). *(Setelah Tari menyelesaikan presentasinya, semua orang bertepuk tangan.)*
3. **a) had sailed** ➡️ The boat sailed before we reached the harbor (`reached`). *(Pada saat kami tiba di pelabuhan, kapal itu sudah berlayar.)*
4. **b) had been** ➡️ Nominal sentence before a past event (*moving*); uses `had` + `been` + adverb of place (*in a small coastal town*). *(Sebelum pindah ke Bitung, keluarga saya pernah tinggal/berada di sebuah kota pesisir kecil.)*
5. **had already closed** ➡️ Positive verbal sentence with `had` + `already` + `closed` (Verb 3). *(Toko itu sudah tutup ketika kami sampai di sana.)*
6. **had not (hadn't) been** ➡️ Negative nominal sentence with `had not` + `been` + adverb of place (*on an airplane*). *(Dia belum pernah naik pesawat sebelum perjalanan itu.)*
7. **Had / met** ➡️ Interrogative verbal sentence starting with `Had` + subject (*they*) + `met` (Verb 3 of *meet*). *(Apakah mereka sudah saling bertemu sebelum konferensi dimulai?)*
8. **Verbal** ➡️ **ID:** *Dia belum menyelesaikan PR-nya sebelum guru mengumpulkannya.*
9. **Nominal** ➡️ **EN:** *He had been the principal for three years before he retired.* *(No action verb; uses "had been" + noun phrase "the principal".)*
10. **Verbal** ➡️ **EN:** *Had they already left when you called?* *(or "gone"; shows leaving happened before calling.)*
