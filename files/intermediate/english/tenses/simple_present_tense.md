# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Simple Present Tense
**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Simple Present Tense)**

The **Simple Present Tense** is the foundation of everyday English. Whenever you talk about **daily routines**, **habits**, **general facts**, or **fixed schedules**, you use this tense!

*(**Simple Present Tense** adalah fondasi bahasa Inggris sehari-hari. Kapan pun kamu membicarakan **rutinitas harian**, **kebiasaan**, **fakta umum**, atau **jadwal tetap**, kamu menggunakan tense ini!)*

Unlike Bahasa Indonesia—where verbs never change form (*saya makan*, *dia makan*)—English verbs in the Simple Present change slightly when the subject is **singular (`He`, `She`, `It`)**. Once you master that one rule, everything else falls into place!

*(Berbeda dengan bahasa Indonesia—di mana kata kerja tidak pernah berubah bentuk—kata kerja bahasa Inggris pada Simple Present sedikit berubah ketika subjeknya **tunggal (`He`, `She`, `It`)**. Begitu kamu menguasai satu aturan itu, sisanya akan terasa sangat mudah!)*

---

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Simple Present
**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Simple Present)**

Use the Simple Present Tense in **four main situations**:

*(Gunakan Simple Present Tense dalam **empat situasi utama**:)*

1. **Daily Habits & Routines (Kebiasaan & Rutinitas Sehari-hari):**
   * *Example:* I **drink** a glass of warm water every morning.
   * *(Saya **minum** segelas air hangat setiap pagi.)*
2. **General Truths & Scientific Facts (Kebenaran Umum & Fakta Ilmiah):**
   * *Example:* The sun **rises** in the east, and water **boils** at 100°C.
   * *(Matahari **terbit** di sebelah timur, dan air **mendidih** pada suhu 100°C.)*
3. **Permanent Situations & States (Situasi Permanen & Keadaan):**
   * *Example:* My sister **lives** in Bandung and **works** as a graphic designer.
   * *(Kakak perempuan saya **tinggal** di Bandung dan **bekerja** sebagai desainer grafis.)*
4. **Fixed Timetables & Schedules (Jadwal Tetap Transportasi / Acara):**
   * *Example:* The train to Yogyakarta **leaves** at 7:30 AM tomorrow.
   * *(Kereta menuju Yogyakarta **berangkat** pukul 07.30 besok pagi.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Is this something that happens **regularly, repeatedly, or is always true**?"* If yes, use the **Simple Present Tense**!
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah ini sesuatu yang terjadi **secara rutin, berulang, atau selalu benar**?" Jika ya, gunakan **Simple Present Tense**!)*

## 2. Two Types of Sentences: Verbal vs. Nominal
**(Dua Jenis Kalimat: Verbal vs. Nominal)**

In English, Simple Present sentences are divided into **Verbal Sentences** (sentences with an action verb like *eat, study, work*) and **Nominal Sentences** (sentences without an action verb—describing identity, job, feeling, or location using **`am / is / are`**).

*(Dalam bahasa Inggris, kalimat Simple Present dibagi menjadi **Kalimat Verbal**—kalimat dengan kata kerja aksi seperti eat, study, work—dan **Kalimat Nominal**—kalimat tanpa kata kerja aksi yang menjelaskan identitas, pekerjaan, sifat, atau lokasi menggunakan **`am / is / are`**.)*

### Part A: Verbal Sentences (Action Verbs — `V1` / `V1 + s/es`)
**(Kalimat Verbal — Menggunakan Kata Kerja Aksi)**

* **Positive (+) Rule:**
  * **`I, You, We, They`** + **Base Verb (`V1`)** ➡️ *They **play** badminton every Sunday.*
  * **`He, She, It`** + **Verb + `-s / -es / -ies`** ➡️ *He **plays** badminton every Sunday.*
* **Negative (-) & Question (?) Rule:**
  * Use **`do`** for `I, You, We, They`, and **`does`** for `He, She, It`.
  * **Crucial:** Whenever **`does`** or **`doesn't`** appears, the main verb goes **back to `V1`** (drop the `-s/-es`!).

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula (Rumus) | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `S (I/You/We/They) + V1` <br> `S (He/She/It) + V1 + s/es` | *I **wake** up at 5:30 AM.* <br> *She **wakes** up at 5:30 AM.* |
| **Negative (-)** | `S + do / does + not + V1` <br> *(don't / doesn't + V1)* | *We **don't eat** fast food.* <br> *He **doesn't eat** fast food.* |
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Do / Does + S + V1?` | ***Do** you **speak** English?* <br> ***Does** she **speak** English?* |
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + do / does + S + V1?` | *Where **do** they **live**?* <br> *What time **does** the class **start**?* |

### Part B: Nominal Sentences (`To Be`: `am` / `is` / `are` — No Action Verb!)
**(Kalimat Nominal — Tanpa Kata Kerja Aksi, Menggunakan `am / is / are`)**

Use **`am`**, **`is`**, or **`are`** when the sentence is followed by a **Noun** (kata benda), **Adjective** (kata sifat), or **Adverb of Place/Time** (keterangan tempat/waktu). **Never use `do` or `does` in nominal sentences!**

*(Gunakan **`am`**, **`is`**, atau **`are`** ketika kalimat diikuti **Kata Benda**, **Kata Sifat**, atau **Keterangan Tempat/Waktu**. **Jangan pernah memakai `do` atau `does` dalam kalimat nominal!**)*

| Subject (Subjek) | Positive (+) | Negative (-) | Question (?) | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`I`** | `am` | `am not` | `Am I...?` | *I **am** a university student.* *(Saya seorang mahasiswa.)* |
| **`He, She, It`** | `is` | `is not` *(isn't)* | `Is he/she/it...?` | *The coffee **is** very hot.* *(Kopinya sangat panas.)* |
| **`You, We, They`** | `are` | `are not` *(aren't)* | `Are you/we/they...?` | *They **are** in the library now.* *(Mereka ada di perpustakaan sekarang.)* |

## 3. Spelling Rules for `He / She / It` (`-s`, `-es`, `-ies`)
**(Aturan Ejaan Kata Kerja untuk Subjek Tunggal `He / She / It`)**

| Rule (Aturan) | Base Verb (`V1`) | Third-Person Singular | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. General Rule:** Add **`-s`** *(Aturan umum: tambah `-s`)* | *work, read, drink, sleep* | **works, reads, drinks, sleeps** | *My father **reads** the news every morning.* |
| **2. Ends in `-o, -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -z`:** Add **`-es`** *(Akhiran tersebut: tambah `-es`)* | *go, watch, wash, pass, fix* | **goes, watches, washes, passes, fixes** | *She **watches** English movies on weekends.* |
| **3. Consonant + `-y`:** Change `-y` to **`-ies`** *(Konsonan + `-y`: ubah jadi `-ies`)* | *study, try, cry, fly* | **studies, tries, cries, flies** | *Rina **studies** diligently every night.* |
| **4. Vowel + `-y`:** Just add **`-s`** *(Vokal + `-y`: cukup tambah `-s`)* | *play, stay, buy, enjoy* | **plays, stays, buys, enjoys** | *He **plays** guitar in a local band.* |
| **5. Irregular (`have`):** Changes to **`has`** *(Bentuk tidak beraturan)* | *have* | **has** | *Our school **has** a large library.* |

## 4. Signal Words & Adverbs of Frequency
**(Kata Penanda Waktu & Kata Keterangan Frekuensi)**

### A. Adverbs of Frequency (Seberapa Sering Sesuatu Terjadi)
Place these words **before the main verb**, OR **after `to be` (`am / is / are`)**:

*(Letakkan kata-kata ini **sebelum kata kerja utama**, ATAU **setelah `to be` (`am / is / are`)**:)*

* **100%** ➡️ **Always** *(selalu)* — *She **always arrives** early.* / *She **is always** punctual.*
* **80–90%** ➡️ **Usually / Normally** *(biasanya)* — *I **usually take** the bus to work.*
* **70%** ➡️ **Often / Frequently** *(sering)* — *We **often visit** our grandparents.*
* **50%** ➡️ **Sometimes** *(kadang-kadang)* — *He **sometimes cooks** dinner.*
* **10–20%** ➡️ **Rarely / Seldom** *(jarang)* — *They **rarely eat** out.*
* **0%** ➡️ **Never** *(tidak pernah)* — *I **never drink** soda.*

### B. Other Common Time Expressions (Penanda Waktu Lainnya)
* *every day / every morning / every week / every year* *(setiap hari / pagi / minggu / tahun)*
* *once a week / twice a month / three times a year* *(seminggu sekali / sebulan dua kali / setahun tiga kali)*
* *on Mondays / on weekends* *(setiap hari Senin / setiap akhir pekan)*

## 5. Crucial Traps to Avoid
**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Keeping `-s/-es` after `does` or `doesn't`.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Tetap memakai akhiran `-s/-es` setelah `does` atau `doesn't`.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She **doesn't likes** spicy food. / **Does** he **works** here?
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She **doesn't like** spicy food. / **Does** he **work** here?
* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Mixing `am / is / are` with an action verb (`V1`).**
  *(Jebakan 2: Mencampur `am / is / are` dengan kata kerja aksi `V1`.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* I **am go** to campus by motorcycle. / They **are study** together.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* I **go** to campus by motorcycle. / They **study** together.
* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Using `don't / doesn't` in Nominal Sentences (with adjectives or nouns).**
  *(Jebakan 3: Memakai `don't / doesn't` pada kalimat nominal yang tidak memiliki kata kerja aksi.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He **doesn't** lazy. / You **don't** late.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He **isn't** lazy. / You **aren't** late.

## 6. Self-Study Practice Quiz
**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. My mother ______________ (drink / drinks / is drink) two cups of tea every morning.
   *(Ibu saya minum dua cangkir teh setiap pagi.)*
2. ______________ (Do / Does / Is) your brother ______________ (play / plays / playing) basketball on weekends?
   *(Apakah saudara laki-lakimu bermain basket setiap akhir pekan?)*
3. The museum ______________ (don't open / doesn't open / doesn't opens) on Mondays.
   *(Museum itu tidak buka pada hari Senin.)*
4. Water ______________ (freeze / freezes / freezing) at 0 degrees Celsius.
   *(Air membeku pada suhu 0 derajat Celcius.)*
5. They ______________ (aren't / don't / isn't) hungry right now because they just ate lunch.
   *(Mereka tidak lapar sekarang karena mereka baru saja makan siang.)*
6. Budi ______________ (study / studys / studies) computer science at the university.
   *(Budi belajar ilmu komputer di universitas.)*
7. Which sentence has the **correct word order**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang memiliki **urutan kata yang benar**?)*
   * A) She is late never for class.
   * B) She never is late for class.
   * C) She is never late for class.
8. How often ______________ (do you visit / you visit / does you visit) your hometown?
   *(Seberapa sering kamu mengunjungi kampung halamanmu?)*

### Answer Key & Explanations
**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **drinks**
   ➡️ Subjek tunggal (`My mother` = `She`) pada kebiasaan rutin (`every morning`) membutuhkan akhiran `-s`.
2. **Does ... play**
   ➡️ Subjek tunggal (`your brother` = `He`) menggunakan kata bantu tanya **`Does`**, dan kata kerja utamanya kembali ke bentuk dasar **`play`** (tanpa `-s`).
3. **doesn't open**
   ➡️ Subjek tunggal (`The museum` = `It`) menggunakan **`doesn't`** + kata kerja dasar **`open`** (tanpa `-s`).
4. **freezes**
   ➡️ Fakta ilmiah umum dengan subjek uncountable/tunggal (`Water` = `It`) membutuhkan akhiran `-s`.
5. **aren't**
   ➡️ Kalimat nominal (diikuti kata sifat `hungry`, bukan kata kerja aksi) dengan subjek jamak `They` menggunakan **`aren't`** (bukan `don't`).
6. **studies**
   ➡️ Kata kerja berakhiran konsonan + `-y` (`study`) berubah menjadi **`-ies`** untuk subjek tunggal `Budi`.
7. **C) She is never late for class.**
   ➡️ *Adverb of frequency* (`never`) diletakkan **setelah `to be` (`is`)**, bukan sebelumnya.
8. **do you visit**
   ➡️ Pertanyaan `Wh-/How often` untuk subjek `you` mengikuti pola `How often + do + you + V1`.
