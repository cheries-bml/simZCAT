# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Past Future Perfect Tense

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Past Future Perfect Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to talk about an action that **would already have been completed before a certain future point from a past viewpoint**—like *"By 8:00 PM yesterday, she said she **would have finished** her presentation"* or *"By last Friday, we thought they **would have arrived** in Jakarta"*? That is the **Past Future Perfect Tense** (also called **Future Perfect in the Past**) ⏳!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin membicarakan suatu tindakan yang **sudah akan selesai sebelum titik waktu tertentu dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu**—seperti "By 8:00 PM yesterday, she said she **would have finished** her presentation" atau "By last Friday, we thought they **would have arrived** in Jakarta"? Itulah **Past Future Perfect Tense**—juga disebut **Future Perfect in the Past** ⏳!)*

Think of it like this: **at a past moment**, someone looked forward to a later deadline and expected or predicted that an action **would already be done** by that deadline ➡️!

*(Anggap saja begini: **pada suatu momen di masa lalu**, seseorang melihat ke depan menuju suatu batas waktu berikutnya dan memperkirakan atau memprediksi bahwa suatu tindakan **sudah akan selesai** pada batas waktu tersebut ➡️!)*

---

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Past Future Perfect
**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Past Future Perfect)**

Use the **Past Future Perfect Tense** in **three main situations**:

*(Gunakan **Past Future Perfect Tense** dalam **tiga situasi utama**:)*

1. **Reported Expectations About a Completed Future Action from a Past Viewpoint (Melaporkan Ekspektasi dari Masa Lalu tentang Tindakan yang Sudah Selesai):**
   * *Direct thought:* "By 5:00 PM, I **will have sent** the invoice."
   * ➡️ *Past Future Perfect:* He told me he **would have sent** the invoice by 5:00 PM.
   * *(Dia memberi tahu saya bahwa dia **sudah akan mengirimkan** faktur tersebut menjelang pukul 17.00.)*
2. **Past Predictions That Turned Out Differently / Were Never Realized (Prediksi Masa Lalu yang Ternyata Berbeda atau Tidak Terjadi):**
   * *Example:* We thought the train **would have arrived** by 9:00 AM, but it was delayed until noon.
   * *(Kami mengira keretanya **sudah akan tiba** pukul 09.00 pagi, tetapi ternyata tertunda hingga siang hari.)*
3. **Past Plans or Expectations About Duration Up to Another Point (Rencana atau Ekspektasi Masa Lalu tentang Durasi Hingga Titik Tertentu):**
   * *Example:* By last June, she expected she **would have lived** in London for three years.
   * *(Menjelang Juni tahun lalu, dia memperkirakan bahwa dia **sudah akan tinggal** di London selama tiga tahun.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Is this looking forward from a **past point of view** (`said`, `thought`, `knew`, `expected`, `yesterday`) to a deadline where the action **would already be completed (`have + V3`)**?"* If yes, use **`would have + V3`** (or **`was/were going to have + V3`**)! ✅
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah ini melihat ke depan dari **sudut pandang masa lalu** menuju batas waktu di mana tindakannya **sudah akan selesai (`have + V3`)**?" Jika ya, gunakan **`would have + V3`** atau **`was/were going to have + V3`**! ✅)*

## 2. Two Main Forms: `Would Have + V3` vs. `Was/Were Going To Have + V3`
**(Dua Bentuk Utama: `Would Have + V3` vs. `Was/Were Going To Have + V3`)**

* **`Would have + V3`:** The most common and natural form in reported speech (*said, told, thought, knew, hoped, expected*) and storytelling. Same form for **all subjects** (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`).
  *(Bentuk yang paling umum dan alami dalam kalimat tidak langsung seperti said, told, thought, knew, hoped, atau expected serta cerita. Bentuknya sama untuk **semua subjek**.)*
* **`Was / Were going to have + V3`:** Used for past expectations or plans involving a completed result by a certain time (often in narrative or analytical contexts).
  *(Dipakai untuk ekspektasi atau rencana masa lalu yang melibatkan hasil selesai pada waktu tertentu—sering kali dalam konteks naratif atau analitis.)*
  * Use **`was`** for: `I, He, She, It`, singular nouns (*Dina*).
  * Use **`were`** for: `You, We, They`, plural nouns (*the students*).

### Sentence Formula Table (Tabel Rumus Kalimat)

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with `Would Have` | Formula with `Was/Were Going To Have` | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `S + would have + V3` | `S + was/were going to have + V3` | *She said she **would have finished** by 6:00 PM.* <br> *He **was going to have completed** it before Friday.* |
| **Negative (-)** | `S + would not (wouldn't) have + V3` | `S + was/were not going to have + V3` | *We knew they **wouldn't have arrived** yet at 7:00 AM.* <br> *I **wasn't going to have finished** by noon anyway.* |
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Would + S + have + V3?` | `Was/Were + S + going to have + V3?` | ***Would** they **have left** by the time we got there?* <br> ***Was** she **going to have submitted** it by then?* |
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + would + S + have + V3?` | `Wh-word + was/were + S + going to have + V3?` | *How much **would** he **have saved** by last December?* <br> *What **was** going to have changed by then?* |

## 3. Signal Words & Time Contexts
**(Penanda Waktu & Konteks Kalimat yang Sering Muncul)**

Past Future Perfect usually combines a **past viewpoint** with a **deadline/endpoint marker**:

*(Past Future Perfect biasanya menggabungkan **sudut pandang masa lalu** dengan **penanda batas waktu atau titik akhir**:)*

* **Past Reporting / Mental Verbs:** *said, told, thought, knew, believed, expected, hoped, promised, assumed* *(berkata, memberi tahu, berpikir, tahu, percaya, mengharapkan, berharap, berjanji, mengira)*
* **Deadline / Endpoint Markers:**
  * **`by` + time** ➡️ *by 5:00 PM yesterday, by last Friday, by the end of last year* *(menjelang / paling lambat pada...)*
  * **`by the time` + Simple Past** ➡️ *by the time we arrived, by the time the movie started* *(pada saat / menjelang...)*
  * **`before` + past time/event** ➡️ *before noon, before the meeting began* *(sebelum...)*

## 4. Past Future Simple vs. Past Future Continuous vs. Past Future Perfect
**(Perbandingan: Past Future Simple vs. Past Future Continuous vs. Past Future Perfect)**

| Tense (Jenis) | Formula (Rumus) | Focus from a Past Viewpoint (Fokus dari Sudut Pandang Masa Lalu) | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Future Simple** | `would + V1` | A future plan, promise, or decision viewed from the past *(Rencana, janji, atau keputusan dari masa lalu)* | *Yesterday she said she **would call** you later.* *(Kemarin dia bilang akan meneleponmu nanti.)* |
| **Past Future Continuous** | `would be + V-ing` | An action **in progress** at a later moment viewed from the past *(Aktivitas yang **sedang berlangsung** pada momen berikutnya)* | *Yesterday she said she **would be working** at 9:00 PM.* *(Kemarin dia bilang akan sedang bekerja pukul 21.00.)* |
| **Past Future Perfect** | `would have + V3` | An action **already completed before** a later point viewed from the past *(Tindakan yang **sudah selesai sebelum** titik waktu berikutnya)* | *Yesterday she said she **would have finished** by 9:00 PM.* *(Kemarin dia bilang sudah akan selesai menjelang pukul 21.00.)* |

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context
**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Reported Completion Before a Deadline:**
   ➡️ Budi told me he **would have packed** all his bags before 6:00 AM.
   *(Budi memberi tahu saya bahwa dia **sudah akan mengemas** semua tasnya sebelum pukul 06.00 pagi.)*
2. **Past Prediction That Didn't Match Reality:**
   ➡️ We thought the guests **would have eaten** by the time we arrived, but dinner hadn't started yet.
   *(Kami mengira para tamu **sudah akan makan** pada saat kami tiba, tetapi makan malamnya belum dimulai.)*
3. **Duration Up to a Past-Projected Point:**
   ➡️ By last month, he expected he **would have worked** at the clinic for five years.
   *(Menjelang bulan lalu, dia memperkirakan bahwa dia **sudah akan bekerja** di klinik itu selama lima tahun.)*
4. **Negative Form (`wouldn't have + V3`):**
   ➡️ She knew the shop **wouldn't have opened** yet at 7:00 AM.
   *(Dia tahu tokonya **belum akan buka** pada pukul 07.00 pagi.)*
5. **Question Form (`Would + S + have + V3`):**
   ➡️ **Would** they **have finished** the repairs before the store opened?
   *(**Apakah** mereka **sudah akan menyelesaikan** perbaikannya sebelum toko dibuka?)*

## 6. Crucial Traps to Avoid
**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using `V2` instead of `V3` after `would have` (`would have went` ❌).**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai `V2` alih-alih `V3` setelah `would have`.)*
  Always use the **Past Participle (`V3`)** (*gone, written, eaten, taken, finished*)!
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He said he would have **went** home by then.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He said he would have **gone** home by then.
* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `will have + V3` instead of `would have + V3` after a past reporting verb.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai `will have + V3` alih-alih `would have + V3` setelah kata kerja pelapor masa lalu.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She thought she **will have finished** by 5:00 PM.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She thought she **would have finished** by 5:00 PM.
* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `would have + V3` (Past Future Perfect) with `would have + V3` in a Third Conditional.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Membedakan konteks Past Future Perfect vs. Third Conditional.)*
  Both use `would have + V3`, so check the context: in Past Future Perfect, it reports or projects a completion by a past-referenced deadline (*He said he **would have completed** it by noon*), whereas Third Conditional expresses an unreal past result from an `if`-condition (*I **would have helped** you if you had asked*).

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz
**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below! ✍️

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah! ✍️)*

1. Yesterday afternoon, Rina said she ______________ (would have finished / will have finished / would have finish) the slides before 8:00 PM.
   *(Kemarin sore, Rina berkata bahwa dia sudah akan menyelesaikan salindia/slide tersebut sebelum pukul 20.00.)*
2. We thought the bus ______________ (would have left / would have leave / will have left) by 6:30 AM, so we hurried.
   *(Kami mengira busnya sudah akan berangkat menjelang pukul 06.30 pagi, jadi kami bergegas.)*
3. He knew they ______________ (wouldn't have arrived / won't have arrived / wouldn't have arrive) yet at that early hour.
   *(Dia tahu mereka belum akan tiba pada jam sepagi itu.)*
4. ______________ (Would you have read / Will you have read / Did you have read) the entire chapter by the time the class started?
   *(Apakah kamu sudah akan membaca seluruh bab itu pada saat kelas dimulai?)*
5. By last October, she expected she ______________ (would have graduated / would have graduate / had graduated) from college.
   *(Menjelang Oktober tahun lalu, dia memperkirakan bahwa dia sudah akan lulus kuliah.)*
6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*
   * A) He told me he would have wrote the email by noon.
   * B) He told me he would have written the email by noon.
   * C) He told me he will have written the email by noon.
7. They assumed the project ______________ (would have been completed / would have been complete / will have been completed) before the holiday.
   *(Mereka mengira proyek tersebut sudah akan diselesaikan sebelum liburan.)*
8. How many pages ______________ (would he have translated / he would have translated / did he have translated) by the time the meeting began?
   *(Berapa banyak halaman yang sudah akan dia terjemahkan pada saat rapat dimulai?)*

### Answer Key & Explanations
**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **would have finished** ✅
   ➡️ Sudut pandang masa lalu (`Yesterday afternoon, Rina said...`) dengan batas waktu `before 8:00 PM` menggunakan **`would have + V3`** (`finished`).
2. **would have left** ✅
   ➡️ Prediksi dari masa lalu (`We thought...`) tentang keberangkatan yang sudah terjadi menjelang pukul 06.30 menggunakan **`would have + V3`** (`left`).
3. **wouldn't have arrived** ✅
   ➡️ Bentuk negatif Past Future Perfect setelah `He knew...` menggunakan **`wouldn't have + V3`** (`arrived`).
4. **Would you have read** ✅
   ➡️ Kalimat tanya Past Future Perfect mengikuti urutan `Would + subjek + have + V3` (`read`).
5. **would have graduated** ✅
   ➡️ Ekspektasi dari masa lalu (`she expected...`) hingga titik waktu tertentu (`By last October`) memakai **`would have + V3`** (`graduated`).
6. **B) He told me he would have written the email by noon.** ✅
   ➡️ Setelah `would have`, gunakan bentuk `V3` yang tepat (`written`, bukan `wrote`), dan gunakan `would` setelah kata kerja pelapor lampau `told`.
7. **would have been completed** ✅
   ➡️ Bentuk pasif Past Future Perfect (`would have been + V3`) menyatakan pekerjaan yang diperkirakan sudah akan diselesaikan sebelum liburan.
8. **would he have translated** ✅
   ➡️ Pertanyaan `Wh-` Past Future Perfect menggunakan urutan `How many pages + would + subjek + have + V3` (`translated`).
