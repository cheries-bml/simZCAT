# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Past Future Simple Tense
**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Past Future Simple Tense)**

Have you ever wanted to talk about a plan, promise, or prediction that was **in the future from a past point of view**—like *"Yesterday she said she **would call** you later"* or *"I thought it **was going to rain** this morning"*? That is the **Past Future Simple Tense** (also called **Future in the Past**) 📋!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin membicarakan sebuah rencana, janji, atau prediksi yang **berada di masa depan dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu**—seperti "Yesterday she said she **would call** you later" atau "I thought it **was going to rain** this morning"? Itulah **Past Future Simple Tense**—juga disebut **Future in the Past** 📋!)*

Think of it like this: **in the past**, someone looked forward to something that had not happened yet at that moment—whether it eventually happened or not ➡️!

*(Anggap saja begini: **di masa lalu**, seseorang menantikan atau memprediksi sesuatu yang belum terjadi pada saat itu—entah pada akhirnya kejadian itu benar-benar terjadi atau tidak ➡️!)*

---

## 1. The Core Idea: When to Use Past Future Simple
**(Konsep Utama: Kapan Menggunakan Past Future Simple)**

Use the **Past Future Simple Tense** in **four main situations**:

*(Gunakan **Past Future Simple Tense** dalam **empat situasi utama**:)*

1. **Reported Plans, Promises & Predictions from the Past (Melaporkan Rencana, Janji, atau Prediksi di Masa Lalu):**
   * *Direct thought/speech:* "I **will** help you tomorrow."
   * ➡️ *Past Future Simple:* He promised he **would** help me the next day.
   * *(Dia berjanji bahwa dia **akan** membantu saya keesokan harinya.)*
2. **Past Intentions or Plans That Changed or Didn't Happen (Niat atau Rencana Masa Lalu yang Berubah atau Batal):**
   * *Example:* We **were going to visit** the museum on Sunday, but it was closed.
   * *(Kami **tadinya akan mengunjungi** museum pada hari Minggu, tetapi museumnya tutup.)*
3. **Past Predictions Based on Signs or Opinions (Prediksi Masa Lalu Berdasarkan Tanda atau Pendapat):**
   * *Example:* Look at those dark clouds—I knew it **was going to rain** soon.
   * *(Lihat awan gelap itu—saya sudah tahu hari **akan segera hujan**.)*
4. **Second Conditional Results (Imaginary / Unreal Situations) (Hasil Pengandaian Tidak Nyata):**
   * *Example:* If I had more free time, I **would travel** more often.
   * *(Seandainya saya punya lebih banyak waktu luang, saya **akan** lebih sering bepergian.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Ask yourself: *"Am I talking about a **future idea viewed from a past time** (`said`, `thought`, `knew`, `planned`, `yesterday`, `last week`)?"* If yes, **`would + V1`** or **`was/were going to + V1`** is usually the right choice! ✅
> *(Tanyakan pada diri sendiri: "Apakah saya sedang membicarakan **gagasan masa depan yang dilihat dari waktu lampau**?" Jika ya, **`would + V1`** atau **`was/were going to + V1`** biasanya menjadi pilihan yang tepat! ✅)*

## 2. Two Main Forms: `Would + V1` vs. `Was/Were Going To + V1`
**(Dua Bentuk Utama: `Would + V1` vs. `Was/Were Going To + V1`)**

Just like Simple Future has `will` and `be going to`, **Past Future Simple** has two main forms:

*(Sama seperti Simple Future yang memiliki `will` dan `be going to`, **Past Future Simple** memiliki dua bentuk utama:)*

* **`Would + V1`:** Very common for promises, offers, decisions, beliefs, and reported speech (`said/thought/knew`). Same form for **all subjects** (`I, You, We, They, He, She, It`).
  *(Sangat umum untuk janji, tawaran, keputusan, keyakinan, dan kalimat tidak langsung. Bentuknya sama untuk **semua subjek**.)*
* **`Was / Were going to + V1`:** Often used for past intentions/plans (especially plans that changed or didn't happen) and past predictions.
  *(Sering dipakai untuk niat atau rencana masa lalu—terutama rencana yang berubah atau tidak jadi—serta prediksi masa lalu.)*
  * Use **`was`** for: `I, He, She, It`, singular nouns (*Dina*).
  * Use **`were`** for: `You, We, They`, plural nouns (*the students*).

### Sentence Formula Table (Tabel Rumus Kalimat)

| Sentence Type (Jenis Kalimat) | Formula with `Would` | Formula with `Was/Were Going To` | Example (Contoh) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positive (+)** | `S + would + V1` | `S + was/were going to + V1` | *She said she **would arrive** at 8:00 AM.* <br> *We **were going to leave** earlier.* |
| **Negative (-)** | `S + would not (wouldn't) + V1` | `S + was/were not going to + V1` | *He promised he **wouldn't be** late.* <br> *I **wasn't going to buy** it, but it was on sale.* |
| **Yes/No Question (?)** | `Would + S + V1?` | `Was/Were + S + going to + V1?` | ***Would** you **help** me if I asked?* <br> ***Were** they **going to join** the trip?* |
| **Wh- Question (?)** | `Wh-word + would + S + V1?` | `Wh-word + was/were + S + going to + V1?` | *What **would** you **do** in that situation?* <br> *Where **was** she **going to stay**?* |

## 3. Signal Words & Time Contexts
**(Penanda Waktu & Konteks Kalimat yang Sering Muncul)**

Past Future Simple usually appears alongside a **past reporting verb** or a **past time marker**:

*(Past Future Simple biasanya muncul bersama **kata kerja pelapor masa lalu** atau **penanda waktu lampau**:)*

* **Past Reporting / Mental Verbs:** *said, told, thought, knew, believed, hoped, promised, decided, planned, expected* *(berkata, memberi tahu, berpikir, tahu, percaya, berharap, berjanji, memutuskan, merencanakan, mengharapkan)*
* **Past Time Markers:** *yesterday, last night, last week, two days ago, earlier, before* *(kemarin, tadi malam, minggu lalu, dua hari lalu, sebelumnya)*
* **Time Shift Clues in Reported Speech:**
  * *today* ➡️ **that day** *(hari itu)*
  * *tomorrow* ➡️ **the next day / the following day** *(keesokan harinya)*
  * *tonight* ➡️ **that night** *(malam itu)*

## 4. Past Future Simple vs. Simple Future (`Will + V1`)
**(Perbandingan: Past Future Simple vs. Simple Future)**

| Feature (Aspek) | Past Future Simple (`would + V1` / `was/were going to + V1`) | Simple Future (`will + V1` / `am/is/are going to + V1`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reference Point (Titik Acuan)** | **Looking forward from the past** *(Melihat ke depan dari masa lalu)* | **Looking forward from right now** *(Melihat ke depan dari saat ini)* |
| **Example 1 (Decision)** | *Yesterday, I decided I **would study** tonight.* *(Kemarin saya putuskan akan belajar malam ini.)* | *Right now, I decide I **will study** tonight.* *(Sekarang saya putuskan akan belajar malam ini.)* |
| **Example 2 (Prediction)** | *She thought it **would rain** yesterday afternoon.* *(Dia mengira kemarin sore akan hujan.)* | *Look at the sky—I think it **will rain** soon.* *(Lihat langitnya—menurut saya sebentar lagi akan hujan.)* |

## 5. Everyday Examples in Context
**(Contoh Kalimat Sehari-hari dalam Konteks)**

1. **Reported Promise:**
   ➡️ Budi promised he **would text** me as soon as he reached home.
   *(Budi berjanji dia **akan mengirim pesan** kepada saya begitu sampai di rumah.)*

2. **Plan That Changed (`was/were going to`):**
   ➡️ I **was going to call** you last night, but my battery died.
   *(Saya **tadinya akan meneleponmu** tadi malam, tetapi baterai ponsel saya habis.)*

3. **Past Belief / Expectation:**
   ➡️ We didn't think the exam **would be** so challenging.
   *(Kami tidak mengira ujiannya **akan** setantang ini.)*

4. **Negative Intention (`wouldn't` / `wasn't going to`):**
   ➡️ She said she **wouldn't join** the meeting because she had another appointment.
   *(Dia bilang dia **tidak akan ikut** rapat karena ada janji lain.)*

5. **Question About a Past Plan:**
   ➡️ **Were** you **going to tell** me about the surprise party?
   *(**Apakah** kamu **tadinya akan memberi tahu** saya tentang pesta kejutan itu?)*

## 6. Crucial Traps to Avoid
**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using `V2` or `V3` after `would` (`would went` ❌ / `would gone` ❌).**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai `V2` atau `V3` setelah `would`.)*
  After **`would`**, always use the **base verb (`V1`)**!
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He said he would **went** to the bank.
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He said he would **go** to the bank.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `will` instead of `would` after a past reporting verb.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai `will` alih-alih `would` setelah kata kerja pelapor bentuk lampau.)*
  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* She told me she **will** come at 5:00 PM. *(when reporting a past statement)*
  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* She told me she **would** come at 5:00 PM.

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `would + V1` (Past Future) with `would have + V3` (Past Unreal / Regret).**
  *(Jebakan 3: Tertukar antara `would + V1` dan `would have + V3`.)*
  Use **`would + V1`** for a future plan/idea from a past viewpoint; use **`would have + V3`** for an unreal past outcome (*I would have helped you if I had known*).

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz
**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or fill in the blank with the correct verb form. Try answering on your own before checking the key below! ✍️

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau isi bagian yang kosong dengan bentuk kata kerja yang benar. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah! ✍️)*

1. Yesterday morning, Rina told me she ______________ (would finish / will finish / would finished) the report before noon.
   *(Kemarin pagi, Rina memberi tahu saya bahwa dia akan menyelesaikan laporannya sebelum siang.)*
2. We ______________ (were going to / are going to / will going to) watch that film last weekend, but tickets were sold out.
   *(Kami tadinya akan menonton film itu akhir pekan lalu, tetapi tiketnya habis terjual.)*
3. I knew the road ______________ (would be / will be / was being) crowded during the holiday rush.
   *(Saya sudah tahu jalannya akan padat saat arus liburan.)*
4. He promised he ______________ (wouldn't forget / won't forget / didn't forget) to bring my notebook back.
   *(Dia berjanji dia tidak akan lupa mengembalikan buku catatan saya.)*
5. ______________ (Were you going to / Will you going to / Did you going to) tell me the good news today?
   *(Apakah kamu tadinya akan memberi tahu saya kabar baik itu hari ini?)*
6. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*
   * A) She said she would calls me later.
   * B) She said she would call me later.
   * C) She said she would called me later.
7. They hoped the weather ______________ (would clear up / will clear up / clears up) before the hike started.
   *(Mereka berharap cuacanya akan cerah sebelum pendakian dimulai.)*
8. I ______________ (wasn't going to join / wasn't join / didn't going to join) the game at first, but my friends convinced me.
   *(Awalnya saya tidak berniat ikut permainan itu, tetapi teman-teman saya meyakinkan saya.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations
**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **would finish** ✅
   ➡️ Kalimat pelapor berbentuk lampau (`told me`) sehingga rencana masa depannya menggunakan **`would + V1`** (`finish`).
2. **were going to** ✅
   ➡️ Menunjukkan rencana masa lalu (`last weekend`) yang batal terjadi; subjek jamak `We` memakai **`were going to`**.
3. **would be** ✅
   ➡️ Prediksi dari sudut pandang masa lalu (`I knew...`) menggunakan **`would + V1`** (`be`).
4. **wouldn't forget** ✅
   ➡️ Janji di masa lalu dalam bentuk negatif menggunakan **`wouldn't + V1`** (`forget`).
5. **Were you going to** ✅
   ➡️ Pertanyaan tentang rencana masa lalu untuk subjek `you` menggunakan **`Were + you + going to + V1`**.
6. **B) She said she would call me later.** ✅
   ➡️ Setelah `would`, kata kerja utama harus berbentuk **dasar (`V1`)**—`call` (bukan `calls` atau `called`).
7. **would clear up** ✅
   ➡️ Harapan dari titik acuan masa lalu (`They hoped...`) menggunakan **`would + V1`** (`clear up`).
8. **wasn't going to join** ✅
   ➡️ Menunjukkan niat awal di masa lalu yang berubah; gunakan **`wasn't going to + V1`** (`join`).
