# Self-Study Guide: Mastering Cleft Sentences in English Grammar

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Cleft Sentences dalam Tata Bahasa Inggris)**

Have you ever wanted to **spotlight one specific piece of information** in a sentence—like saying *"It was **Sarah** who called you, not Tom"* instead of just *"Sarah called you"*? That is what a **cleft sentence** does!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin **menyoroti satu bagian informasi tertentu** dalam sebuah kalimat—misalnya mengatakan "It was **Sarah** who called you, not Tom" alih-alih sekadar "Sarah called you"? Itulah fungsi **cleft sentence**!)*

The word **"cleft"** means *split* or *divided*. A cleft sentence splits a single simple idea into **two clauses** so you can put extra focus or contrast on the part that matters most.

*(Kata **"cleft"** berarti *terbelah* atau *terbagi*. Cleft sentence membelah satu ide sederhana menjadi **dua klausa** agar kamu bisa memberi fokus atau kontras ekstra pada bagian yang paling penting.)*

* *Normal:* **Budi** broke the glass by accident. *(Budi memecahkan gelas itu secara tidak sengaja.)*

* ➡️ **Cleft:** It was **Budi** who broke the glass by accident. *(**Budi**-lah yang memecahkan gelas itu secara tidak sengaja.)*

## 1. The Core Idea: Why & How Cleft Sentences Work

**(Konsep Utama: Mengapa & Bagaimana Cleft Sentences Bekerja)**

Think of a cleft sentence like turning on a spotlight on a stage: the rest of the sentence stays in the background, while the focused part stands out clearly.

*(Anggap saja cleft sentence seperti menyalakan lampu sorot di atas panggung: bagian kalimat lainnya tetap menjadi latar belakang, sementara bagian yang difokuskan terlihat jelas menonjol.)*

We use cleft sentences to:

*(Kita menggunakan cleft sentence untuk:)*

1. **Emphasize new or surprising information** ➡️ *Menekankan informasi baru atau mengejutkan.*

2. **Correct a misunderstanding or contrast two things** ➡️ *Meluruskan kesalahpahaman atau mempertentangkan dua hal.*

3. **Connect ideas smoothly in writing or speaking** ➡️ *Menghubungkan ide secara mulus saat menulis atau berbicara.*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Every cleft sentence starts with a **focus frame** (like `It is/was...` or `What...`) and can almost always be reduced back into a **normal, single-clause sentence** without changing the basic facts!
>
> * *Cleft:* It is **patience** that you need right now. ➡️ *Normal:* You need **patience** right now.
>   *(Setiap cleft sentence diawali **bingkai fokus** seperti `It is/was...` atau `What...` dan hampir selalu bisa dikembalikan menjadi **kalimat tunggal biasa** tanpa mengubah fakta dasarnya.)*

## 2. Main Types of Cleft Sentences with Examples

**(Jenis-Jenis Utama Cleft Sentences beserta Contohnya)**

### Type 1: `It`-Cleft Sentences (Kalimat Cleft dengan `It`)

This is the most common cleft pattern. You put the focused word or phrase right after **`It + be`**, followed by a relative clause (`who`, `which`, `that`, `when`, `where`).

*(Ini adalah pola cleft yang paling umum. Kamu meletakkan kata atau frasa yang difokuskan tepat setelah **`It + be`**, lalu diikuti klausa relatif seperti who, which, that, when, atau where.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `It + is/was/will be + [Focused Element] + who/which/that/when/where + rest of clause`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **Focusing on a Person (`who` / `that`):**

   * *Normal:* **Maya** organized the charity event. *(Maya mengorganisasi acara amal itu.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** It was **Maya** who organized the charity event.
     *(**Maya**-lah yang mengorganisasi acara amal itu.)*

2. **Focusing on a Thing / Object (`which` / `that`):**

   * *Normal:* I lost **my passport**, not my wallet. *(Saya kehilangan paspor saya, bukan dompet saya.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** It was **my passport** that I lost, not my wallet.
     *(**Paspor sayalah** yang hilang, bukan dompet saya.)*

3. **Focusing on a Time (`when` / `that`):**

   * *Normal:* The train finally arrived **at midnight**. *(Kereta itu akhirnya tiba pada tengah malam.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** It was **at midnight** when the train finally arrived.
     *(**Pada tengah malamlah** kereta itu akhirnya tiba.)*

4. **Focusing on a Place (`where` / `that`):**

   * *Normal:* We first met **in Yogyakarta**. *(Kami pertama kali bertemu di Yogyakarta.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** It was **in Yogyakarta** where we first met.
     *(**Di Yogyakartalah** kami pertama kali bertemu.)*

5. **Present or Future Focus (`is` / `will be`):**

   * ➡️ It is **consistency** that leads to long-term success.
     *(**Konsistensilah** yang membawa pada kesuksesan jangka panjang.)*

### Type 2: `Wh-`-Cleft Sentences / Pseudo-Clefts (Kalimat Cleft dengan `Wh-`)

Instead of starting with `It`, you start with a **`wh-` clause** (most commonly **`What...`**) to describe what happened or what someone wants, then place **`is` / `was`** + the **focused element** at the end.

*(Alih-alih memulai dengan `It`, kamu memulai dengan **klausa `wh-`**—paling sering **`What...`**—untuk menjelaskan apa yang terjadi atau apa yang diinginkan seseorang, lalu meletakkan **`is` / `was`** + **bagian yang difokuskan** di akhir.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `What + [Subject + Verb] + is/was + [Focused Element]`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **Focusing on a Thing or Action (`What... is/was + noun/phrase`):**

   * *Normal:* I really need **a good night's sleep**. *(Saya benar-benar butuh tidur malam yang nyenyak.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** **What** I really need **is** a good night's sleep.
     *(Yang benar-benar saya butuhkan adalah tidur malam yang nyenyak.)*

2. **Focusing on a Whole Action (`What... is/was + (to) + V1 / V-ing`):**

   * *Normal:* He **forgot to lock the front door**. *(Dia lupa mengunci pintu depan.)*

   * ➡️ **Cleft:** **What** he did **was** forget to lock the front door. *(or: **was to forget**...)*
     *(Yang dia lakukan adalah lupa mengunci pintu depan.)*

3. **Focusing on a Reason (`Why... is/was + because...`):**

   * ➡️ **Why** she resigned **was** because she wanted to start her own business.
     *(Alasan mengapa dia mengundurkan diri adalah karena dia ingin memulai bisnisnya sendiri.)*

4. **Focusing on a Place (`Where... is/was + place`):**

   * ➡️ **Where** we plan to stay **is** a small cabin near the lake.
     *(Tempat yang kami rencanakan untuk menginap adalah sebuah kabin kecil di dekat danau.)*

### Type 3: Reversed `Wh-`-Cleft Sentences (Bentuk `Wh-`-Cleft Terbalik)

Sometimes the focused element goes **first**, followed by **`is` / `was`** and the **`What-` clause** at the end. This sounds slightly more emphatic or conclusive.

*(Terkadang bagian yang difokuskan diletakkan **di depan**, diikuti **`is` / `was`** dan **klausa `What-`** di bagian akhir. Bentuk ini terdengar sedikit lebih tegas atau menyimpulkan.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `[Focused Element] + is/was + what + [Subject + Verb]`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ **Honesty** is what I value most in a friendship.
   *(Kejujuran adalah hal yang paling saya hargai dalam persahabatan.)*

2. ➡️ **A cup of hot tea** was what she needed after walking in the rain.
   *(Secangkir teh hangat adalah hal yang dia butuhkan setelah berjalan di tengah hujan.)*

### Type 4: `All`-, `The thing`-, and `The reason`-Clefts (Cleft dengan `All`, `The thing`, & `The reason`)

Great alternatives when you want to say *"the only thing..."* or *"the main reason..."*.

*(Alternatif bagus ketika kamu ingin mengatakan "satu-satunya hal..." atau "alasan utamanya...".)*

1. ➡️ **All** I want **is** a quiet weekend with my family.
   *(Yang saya inginkan hanyalah akhir pekan yang tenang bersama keluarga.)*

2. ➡️ **The thing** that surprised me most **was** how calm he stayed.
   *(Hal yang paling mengejutkan saya adalah betapa tenangnya dia.)*

3. ➡️ **The reason** why they moved **was** to be closer to the office.
   *(Alasan mengapa mereka pindah adalah agar lebih dekat dengan kantor.)*

## 3. Summary Cheat Sheet

**(Tabel Rangkuman Cepat)**

| Cleft Type (Jenis Cleft) | Starting Pattern (Pola Awal) | What Gets Focused (Yang Difokuskan) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **`It`-Cleft** | `It + be + [Focus] + who/which/that...` | Specific noun, time, or place *(Orang/benda/waktu/tempat)* | *It was **Leo** who fixed the laptop.* | 
| **`What`-Cleft** | `What + [Clause] + be + [Focus]` | Information at the end *(Bagian akhir kalimat)* | ***What** I love **is** rainy mornings.* | 
| **Reversed `What`-Cleft** | `[Focus] + be + what + [Clause]` | Element at the very front *(Bagian paling depan)* | ***Patience** is what we need now.* | 
| **`All` / `The thing` Cleft** | `All / The thing + ... + be + [Focus]` | *"Only / main thing"* *(Satu-satunya / hal utama)* | ***All** he did **was** smile and nod.* | 

## 4. Crucial Traps & Verb Agreement Rules

**(Jebakan Penting & Aturan Kesesuaian Kata Kerja)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Subject-Verb Agreement in `What`-Clefts.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Kesesuaian bentuk `is` vs. `are` pada `What`-Clefts.)*

  * When the focused item at the end is **plural**, use **`are`** or **`were`** (though *is/was* is also heard in casual speech, *are/were* is safest in careful writing).
    *(Jika bagian fokus di akhir berbentuk **jamak**, gunakan **`are`** atau **`were`** agar lebih tepat dalam tulisan yang cermat.)*

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* **What** we need **are** better tools and clearer instructions.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* **What** she bought **were** two vintage jackets.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `who` for people and `which`/`that` for things in `It`-Clefts.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Menggunakan `who` untuk orang dan `which`/`that` untuk benda pada `It`-Clefts.)*

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* It was **my sister who** baked that cake. *(Not "which")*

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Verb form after `What ... do/did + was + ...`**
  *(Jebakan 3: Bentuk kata kerja setelah pola `What ... do/did + was + ...`.)*

  * You can use either **base verb (`V1`)**, **`to + V1`**, or **`V-ing`** (matching the structure).
    *(Kamu bisa memakai **kata kerja dasar / V1**, **`to + V1`**, atau **`V-ing`** sesuai susunan kalimat.)*

  * ✅ *Example:* **What** they did **was** *(to)* **send** an email right away.

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option or rewrite the sentence using the requested cleft type. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi terbaik atau tulis ulang kalimat menggunakan jenis cleft yang diminta. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. Rewrite as an **`It`-cleft** focusing on **Rina**: *Rina solved the difficult math problem.*
   *(Tulis ulang sebagai `It`-cleft dengan fokus pada **Rina**: Rina solved the difficult math problem.)*

2. Choose the best completion for a **`What`-cleft**:
   \______________\_ (What I forgot / It was what I forgot / What did I forget) **was** to bring my umbrella.
   *(Yang saya lupa adalah membawa payung saya.)*

3. Choose the correct verb form for a **plural focus**:
   **What** the team lacks \______________\_ (is / are / was) enough time to test the app.
   *(Yang kurang dari tim tersebut adalah waktu yang cukup untuk menguji aplikasi itu.)*

4. Rewrite as an **`It`-cleft** focusing on **last Tuesday**: *We submitted the proposal last Tuesday.*
   *(Tulis ulang sebagai `It`-cleft dengan fokus pada **last Tuesday**: We submitted the proposal last Tuesday.)*
   ➡️ \__________________________________________________________________\_

5. Complete the **`All`-cleft** sentence:
   **All** they asked for \______________\_ (is / was / were) a fair chance to explain.
   *(Yang mereka minta hanyalah kesempatan yang adil untuk menjelaskan.)*

6. Which sentence is a **reversed `what`-cleft**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang merupakan **reversed `what`-cleft**?)*

   * A) It was coffee that kept me awake all night.

   * B) **Good communication** is what makes a relationship strong.

   * C) What I want to do this weekend is visit my grandparents.

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **It was Rina who solved the difficult math problem.** *(or `that solved`)*
   ➡️ *`It`-cleft:* Menempatkan fokus pada `Rina` (orang) setelah `It was`, diikuti `who solved...`.

2. **What I forgot**
   ➡️ *`What`-cleft:* Klausa `What I forgot` berfungsi sebagai subjek yang diikuti `was` dan bagian fokus `to bring my umbrella`.

3. **is**
   ➡️ *Subject-Verb Agreement:* Bagian fokus di akhir adalah `enough time` (uncountable / singular), sehingga menggunakan `is`.

4. **It was last Tuesday when we submitted the proposal.** *(or `that we submitted`)*
   ➡️ *`It`-cleft (Time):* Menyoroti waktu `last Tuesday` setelah `It was`, diikuti `when` atau `that`.

5. **was**
   ➡️ *`All`-cleft:* `All they asked for` merujuk pada satu hal/permintaan di masa lalu, sehingga menggunakan `was`.

6. **B) Good communication is what makes a relationship strong.**
   ➡️ *Reversed `What`-cleft:* Bagian fokus (`Good communication`) diletakkan di depan, diikuti `is what makes...`.
