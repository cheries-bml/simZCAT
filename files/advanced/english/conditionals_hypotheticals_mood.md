## 1. Mixed Conditionals (Kalimat Pengandaian Campuran)

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?
* **English:** Normally, conditional sentences stay in one time frame (past with past, or present with present). **Mixed conditionals** mix two different time frames—usually **Past + Present** or **Present + Past**—to show how an unreal past event affects today, or how an unreal present situation affected the past.
* **Bahasa Indonesia:** Biasanya, kalimat pengandaian berada dalam satu kerangka waktu yang sama. **Mixed conditionals** menggabungkan dua waktu yang berbeda—biasanya **Masa Lalu + Masa Kini** atau **Masa Kini + Masa Lalu**—untuk menunjukkan bagaimana kejadian tidak nyata di masa lalu memengaruhi keadaan hari ini, atau sebaliknya.

### Pattern A: Past Action ➡️ Present Result (Masa Lalu ➡️ Hasil Sekarang)
* **Formula:** `If + Subject + had + V3, Subject + would/could/might + V1`
* **Think of it as:** *"Because I didn't do X in the past, Y is happening right now."*

**Examples:**
1. **If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.**  
   *(Jika saya dulu belajar kedokteran, saya pasti sudah menjadi dokter sekarang.)*
2. **If she hadn't missed the flight, she would be in Bali right now.**  
   *(Jika dia tidak ketinggalan pesawat, dia pasti sudah berada di Bali sekarang.)*
3. **If we had saved more money last year, we could afford this car today.**  
   *(Jika kita menabung lebih banyak tahun lalu, kita bisa membeli mobil ini hari ini.)*
4. **If he had taken the medicine, he might feel better now.**  
   *(Jika dia meminum obatnya, dia mungkin merasa lebih baik sekarang.)*
5. **If they hadn't spent all their savings, they wouldn't be in debt today.**  
   *(Jika mereka tidak menghabiskan semua tabungan mereka, mereka tidak akan berutang hari ini.)*

### Pattern B: Present Reality ➡️ Past Result (Keadaan Sekarang ➡️ Hasil Masa Lalu)
* **Formula:** `If + Subject + V2 / were, Subject + would/could/might + have + V3`
* **Think of it as:** *"Because I am (not) like X in general, Y happened in the past."*

**Examples:**
1. **If I were more organized, I wouldn't have lost my keys yesterday.**  
   *(Jika saya orangnya lebih teratur, saya tidak akan kehilangan kunci saya kemarin.)*
2. **If she spoke Japanese, she would have gotten that job last month.**  
   *(Jika dia bisa berbahasa Jepang, dia pasti sudah mendapatkan pekerjaan itu bulan lalu.)*
3. **If he weren't afraid of heights, he would have climbed the mountain with us.**  
   *(Jika dia tidak takut ketinggian, dia pasti sudah mendaki gunung bersama kita.)*
4. **If they knew the city well, they wouldn't have gotten lost last night.**  
   *(Jika mereka mengenal kota itu dengan baik, mereka tidak akan tersesat tadi malam.)*
5. **If you were a faster runner, you could have won the race last weekend.**  
   *(Jika kamu pelari yang lebih cepat, kamu bisa saja memenangkan lomba akhir pekan lalu.)*

## 2. Inverted Conditionals (Pengandaian Tanpa "If")

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?
* **English:** In formal writing or speeches, you can drop **"if"** and flip the subject and helping verb (**Should, Were, Had**). The meaning stays the exact same, but the tone becomes sharper and more formal.
* **Bahasa Indonesia:** Dalam tulisan formal atau pidato, kamu bisa menghilangkan kata **"if"** dan membalik posisi subjek serta kata kerja bantu (**Should, Were, Had**). Maknanya tetap sama, tetapi nadanya menjadi lebih formal dan tegas.

### Quick Reference Table

| Type | Standard ("If") | Inverted (No "If") | Meaning / Makna |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type 1 (Future)** | *If you should need help...* | **Should** you need help... | Jika sekiranya kamu butuh bantuan... |
| **Type 2 (Present)** | *If I were rich...* | **Were** I rich... | Seandainya saya kaya... |
| **Type 2 (Action Verb)** | *If she knew...* | **Were** she **to know**... | Seandainya dia tahu... |
| **Type 3 (Past)** | *If they had arrived...* | **Had** they arrived... | Seandainya mereka tiba... |

> **Self-Check Tip (Aturan Negatif):**  
> Never use contractions (*Shouldn't, Weren't, Hadn't*) at the start! Always put **"not"** after the subject:  
> - **Correct:** *Had I **not** seen it...* / *Were she **not** here...*  
> - **Incorrect:** *Hadn't I seen it...*

**Examples:**
1. **Should** you **have** any questions, please email me.  
   *(Jika sekiranya Anda memiliki pertanyaan, silakan kirim email kepada saya.)*
2. **Should** it **rain** tomorrow, the match will be canceled.  
   *(Jika sekiranya besok hujan, pertandingan akan dibatalkan.)*
3. **Were** I in your shoes, I would accept the offer.  
   *(Seandainya saya berada di posisimu, saya akan menerima tawaran itu.)*
4. **Were** the company **to lower** prices, sales would rise.  
   *(Seandainya perusahaan menurunkan harga, penjualan akan meningkat.)*
5. **Had** we **known** about the traffic, we would have left earlier.  
   *(Seandainya kami tahu tentang kemacetan itu, kami pasti sudah berangkat lebih awal.)*

## 3. The Subjunctive Mood (Modus Subjungtif)

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?
* **English:** The subjunctive is used to talk about **demands, urgent recommendations, wishes, or unreal situations**—not everyday facts.
* **Bahasa Indonesia:** *Subjunctive* digunakan untuk menyatakan **tuntutan, saran mendesak, harapan, atau situasi tidak nyata**—bukan fakta sehari-hari.

### 3.1 Mandative Subjunctive (Perintah / Saran Mendesak)
After words of urgency or recommendation, the verb in the *that*-clause **always stays in its base form (V1)**—no *-s*, no *-ed*, and always use **be** (never *is/am/are*).

* **Common Triggers:** *advise, ask, demand, insist, propose, recommend, request, suggest, urge* | *crucial, essential, imperative, important, necessary, urgent, vital*
* **Formula:** `Trigger + that + Subject + (not) + V1 / be`

**Examples:**
1. The doctor recommended that he **take** the medicine twice a day. *(Not "takes")*  
   *(Dokter menyarankan agar dia meminum obat itu dua kali sehari.)*
2. It is essential that every student **be** on time. *(Not "is")*  
   *(Sangat penting bahwa setiap siswa tepat waktu.)*
3. The manager insisted that the report **not be** delayed.  
   *(Manajer bersikeras agar laporan tersebut tidak ditunda.)*
4. They demanded that she **apologize** immediately.  
   *(Mereka menuntut agar dia segera meminta maaf.)*

### 3.2 Past Subjunctive & Fixed Expressions
* **Past Subjunctive:** Always use **were** (even with *I, he, she, it*) after *if, wish, as if, as though*.
* **Fixed Idioms:** Traditional phrases that never change form.

**Examples:**
1. He talks as if he **were** the boss.  
   *(Dia berbicara seolah-olah dia adalah bosnya.)*
2. If I **were** you, I would double-check the contract.  
   *(Jika saya jadi kamu, saya akan memeriksa ulang kontraknya.)*
3. **Be that as it may**, we must follow the rules.  
   *(Meskipun demikian, kita harus mengikuti aturan.)*
4. **Come what may**, I will support you.  
   *(Apa pun yang terjadi, saya akan mendukungmu.)*

## 4. Wish / If Only (Harapan & Penyesalan)

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?
* **English:** Both *Wish* and *If only* express a desire for reality to be different. **If only** follows the exact same grammar rules as *wish*, but sounds stronger and more emotional.
* **Bahasa Indonesia:** *Wish* dan *If only* mengungkapkan keinginan agar kenyataan berbeda. **If only** memiliki aturan yang sama persis dengan *wish*, tetapi terdengar lebih kuat dan emosional.

### Three Patterns to Master

| What You're Expressing | Formula | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Present Desire** *(Keinginan saat ini)* | `Wish / If only + S + V2 / were` | I **wish** I **had** more free time. *(Fact: I don't.)* |
| **2. Past Regret** *(Penyesalan masa lalu)* | `Wish / If only + S + had + V3` | **If only** I **had listened** to you! *(Fact: I didn't.)* |
| **3. Annoyance / Complaint** *(Keluhan)* | `Wish / If only + S + would + V1` | I **wish** you **would stop** shouting. |

> **Self-Check Tip:** Don't use *would* for a wish about yourself (e.g., *I wish I would be taller* ➡️ **Incorrect**). Say *I wish I **were** taller*. Only use *would* when you want **someone else or something external** to change.

**Examples:**
1. **Present:** I **wish** I **lived** closer to my office. *(Saya berharap saya tinggal lebih dekat dengan kantor.)*
2. **Present (*were*):** **If only** she **were** here right now! *(Seandainya saja dia ada di sini sekarang!)*
3. **Past Regret:** They **wish** they **had bought** the house earlier. *(Mereka menyesal tidak membeli rumah itu lebih awal.)*
4. **Past Regret:** **If only** I **hadn't said** that yesterday! *(Seandainya saja saya tidak mengatakan itu kemarin!)*
5. **Complaint:** I **wish** it **would stop** raining. *(Saya berharap hujannya segera berhenti.)*

## 5. Self-Test & Answer Key (Latihan Mandiri)

### Practice Questions
Complete each sentence with the correct form:
1. If she had accepted the scholarship last year, she ________ (study) in London right now.
2. ________ (Had / Were / Should) you need further help, feel free to call me.
3. The committee insisted that the new rule ________ (is / be / was) applied immediately.
4. I wish I ________ (didn't eat / hadn't eaten) so much spicy food last night.
5. ________ (Were / Had / Should) I known about your arrival, I would have picked you up.
6. He acts as though he ________ (knows / knew) everything about the project.

### Answer Key & Explanations
1. **would be studying** *(or **would study**)* — Mixed Conditional (Past action ➡️ Present result, signaled by *right now*).
2. **Should** — Inverted Conditional Type 1 (*If you should need...* ➡️ *Should you need...*).
3. **be** — Mandative Subjunctive triggered by *insisted that* (always base form).
4. **hadn't eaten** — Past regret (*wish + Past Perfect*) because it happened *last night*.
5. **Had** — Inverted Conditional Type 3 (*If I had known...* ➡️ *Had I known...*).
6. **knew** — Past Subjunctive after *as though* for an unreal present situation.
