# Self-Study Guide: Mastering the Subjunctive Mood

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Modus Subjungtif dalam Bahasa Inggris)**

Most of the time in English, verbs describe **real facts** (for example: *She **works** here* or *He **was** tired*). However, when you want to talk about **urgent demands, strong recommendations, wishes, or imaginary situations**, English switches to a special verb mode called the **Subjunctive Mood**.

*(Sebagian besar waktu dalam bahasa Inggris, kata kerja menggambarkan **fakta nyata**—misalnya: She **works** here atau He **was** tired. Namun, ketika kamu ingin membicarakan **tuntutan mendesak, saran kuat, harapan, atau situasi khayalan**, bahasa Inggris beralih ke mode kata kerja khusus yang disebut **Subjunctive Mood**.)*

The secret to mastering the subjunctive is simple: **verbs stop following normal tense and singular/plural rules** to signal that the idea is a desire, command, or imagination rather than a fact.

*(Rahasia menguasai subjunctive itu sederhana: **kata kerja berhenti mengikuti aturan tenses dan tunggal/jamak yang biasa** untuk menandakan bahwa ide tersebut adalah keinginan, perintah, atau khayalan—bukan fakta.)*

## 1. Present Subjunctive (Mandative Subjunctive)

**(Subjungtif Masa Kini — Perintah, Tuntutan & Saran Mendesak)**

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?

We use the **Present Subjunctive** after verbs or adjectives that express **urgency, necessity, advice, or demand**. In this pattern, the verb in the `that`-clause **always stays in its pure base form (`V1`)**—no `-s` for *he/she/it*, no `-ed`, and always use **`be`** instead of *am, is, are*.

*(Kita menggunakan **Present Subjunctive** setelah kata kerja atau kata sifat yang mengungkapkan **urgensi, keharusan, saran, atau tuntutan**. Dalam pola ini, kata kerja di dalam klausa `that` **selalu tetap dalam bentuk dasar murni (`V1`)**—tanpa akhiran `-s` untuk he/she/it, tanpa `-ed`, dan selalu gunakan **`be`** alih-alih am, is, are.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `Trigger Verb/Adjective + that + Subject + (not) + Base Verb (V1 / be)`

### Common Trigger Words (Kata Pemicu Umum)

* **Verbs (Kata Kerja):** *advise, ask, command, demand, insist, order, propose, recommend, request, require, suggest, urge*

* **Adjectives (Kata Sifat):** *crucial, essential, imperative, important, mandatory, necessary, urgent, vital*

### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **With a Verb (Dengan Kata Kerja):**
   * *Normal Fact:* He **takes** the medicine every day. *(Dia minum obat setiap hari.)*
   * ➡️ **Subjunctive:** The doctor recommended that he **take** the medicine every day. *(Not "takes")*
   *(Dokter menyarankan agar dia meminum obat itu setiap hari.)*

2. **With `be` (Menggunakan `be`):**
   * *Normal Fact:* Every student **is** on time. *(Setiap siswa tepat waktu.)*
   * ➡️ **Subjunctive:** It is essential that every student **be** on time for the exam. *(Not "is")*
   *(Sangat penting bahwa setiap siswa tepat waktu untuk ujian.)*

3. **Negative Form — Put `not` directly before `V1` (Bentuk Negatif — Letakkan `not` tepat sebelum `V1`):**
   * ➡️ The manager insisted that the report **not be** delayed any longer. *(Not "doesn't be" or "isn't")*
   *(Manajer bersikeras agar laporan tersebut tidak ditunda lagi.)*

4. **Passive Form — Use `be + V3` (Bentuk Pasif — Gunakan `be + V3`):**
   * ➡️ They demanded that the broken machine **be replaced** immediately. *(Not "is replaced")*
   *(Mereka menuntut agar mesin yang rusak itu segera diganti.)*

5. **With an Adjective Trigger (Dengan Kata Sifat Pemicu):**
   * ➡️ It is vital that she **attend** the meeting tomorrow morning. *(Not "attends")*
   *(Sangat penting bahwa dia menghadiri rapat besok pagi.)*

> **Quick Self-Check Trick (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Imagine an invisible **"should"** before the verb!
> *The doctor recommended that he **(should) take** the medicine.* ➡️ Remove *should*, and you get the exact subjunctive verb: **take**!
> *(Bayangkan ada kata **"should"** yang tidak terlihat sebelum kata kerja! Hilangkan "should", dan kamu langsung mendapatkan bentuk subjunctive yang tepat.)*

## 2. Past Subjunctive (Unreal Present / Imaginary Situations)

**(Subjungtif Masa Lalu — Khayalan atau Situasi Tidak Nyata Saat Ini)**

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?

We use the **Past Subjunctive** to talk about situations that are **imaginary, impossible, or contrary to reality right now**. Even though it is called "Past" Subjunctive, it refers to **present** time.

*(Kita menggunakan **Past Subjunctive** untuk membicarakan situasi yang **bersifat khayalan, mustahil, atau bertentangan dengan kenyataan saat ini**. Meskipun disebut "Past" Subjunctive, bentuk ini merujuk pada waktu **sekarang**.)*

The golden rule here is simple: **Always use `were` instead of `was`** for all subjects (*I, he, she, it*), and use **Past Simple (`V2`)** for other verbs.

*(Aturan emasnya sederhana: **Selalu gunakan `were` alih-alih `was`** untuk semua subjek termasuk I, he, she, it, dan gunakan **Past Simple (`V2`)** untuk kata kerja lainnya.)*

### Where We Use It & Examples (Penggunaan & Contoh)

#### A. Hypothetical `If`-Clauses (Kalimat Pengandaian Khayalan)

* ➡️ If I **were** you, I would accept that scholarship immediately.
  *(Seandainya saya menjadi kamu, saya akan langsung menerima beasiswa itu.)*

* ➡️ If she **were** here right now, she would know what to do.
  *(Seandainya dia ada di sini sekarang, dia pasti tahu apa yang harus dilakukan.)*

#### B. `Wish` & `If only` for Present Desires (Harapan untuk Keadaan Sekarang)

* ➡️ I wish I **were** taller so I could reach the top shelf. *(Reality: I am not tall.)*
  *(Saya berharap saya lebih tinggi agar bisa menjangkau rak paling atas.)*

* ➡️ If only we **had** more free time this weekend! *(Reality: We are busy.)*
  *(Seandainya saja kita punya lebih banyak waktu luang akhir pekan ini!)*

#### C. `As if` / `As though` (Seolah-olah)

* ➡️ He talks as if he **were** the owner of the company. *(Reality: He is just an intern.)*
  *(Dia berbicara seolah-olah dia adalah pemilik perusahaan.)*

* ➡️ She acts as though she **knew** everything about the project. *(Reality: She doesn't.)*
  *(Dia bertingkah seolah-olah dia tahu segalanya tentang proyek itu.)*

#### D. `It is (high/about) time + Subject + V2` (Sudah saatnya...)

Used to say something should already be happening right now.

*(Digunakan untuk mengatakan bahwa sesuatu seharusnya sudah dilakukan sekarang.)*

* ➡️ It is high time we **went** home—it's almost midnight! *(Not "go")*
  *(Sudah waktunya kita pulang—ini sudah hampir tengah malam!)*

* ➡️ It is about time the government **took** serious action on pollution.
  *(Sudah saatnya pemerintah mengambil tindakan serius terhadap polusi.)*

#### E. `Would rather + Subject + V2` (Lebih suka jika orang lain...)

Used when you want **someone else** to do something differently right now.

*(Digunakan ketika kamu ingin **orang lain** melakukan sesuatu secara berbeda saat ini.)*

* ➡️ I would rather you **didn't tell** anyone about this secret.
  *(Saya lebih suka jika kamu tidak memberi tahu siapa pun tentang rahasia ini.)*

* ➡️ She would rather he **came** earlier tomorrow.
  *(Dia lebih suka jika pria itu datang lebih awal besok.)*

## 3. Past Perfect Subjunctive (Unreal Past / Regrets)

**(Subjungtif Past Perfect — Penyesalan atau Khayalan Masa Lalu)**

### What Is It? / Apa Itu?

When your wish, imagination, or regret is about something that **already happened (or didn't happen) in the past**, shift one step further back to **`had + V3`**.

*(Ketika harapan, khayalan, atau penyesalanmu berkaitan dengan sesuatu yang **sudah terjadi—atau tidak terjadi—di masa lalu**, mundurkan satu langkah lagi ke bentuk **`had + V3`**.)*

### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **Past Regret with `Wish` (Penyesalan Masa Lalu dengan `Wish`):**
   * ➡️ I wish I **had studied** harder for last week's test. *(Reality: I didn't study hard.)*
   *(Saya menyesal tidak belajar lebih giat untuk ujian minggu lalu.)*

2. **Strong Past Regret with `If only` (Penyesalan Kuat dengan `If only`):**
   * ➡️ If only they **had listened** to our warning yesterday!
   *(Seandainya saja mereka mendengarkan peringatan kita kemarin!)*

3. **Unreal Past `If`-Clause (Pengandaian Masa Lalu):**
   * ➡️ If he **had left** five minutes earlier, he wouldn't have missed the train.
   *(Seandainya dia berangkat lima menit lebih awal, dia tidak akan ketinggalan kereta.)*

4. **Past Comparison with `As if` (Perbandingan Masa Lalu dengan `As if`):**
   * ➡️ You look so exhausted, as if you **had run** a marathon!
   *(Kamu terlihat sangat kelelahan, seolah-olah kamu baru saja berlari maraton!)*

## 4. Fixed / Formulaic Subjunctive Expressions

**(Ungkapan Subjungtif Tetap / Idiom)**

English preserves several traditional phrases that keep old subjunctive verb forms. You don't need to build these from scratch—just learn them as complete expressions!

*(Bahasa Inggris mempertahankan beberapa frasa tradisional yang menggunakan bentuk kata kerja subjungtif lama. Kamu tidak perlu merangkainya dari awal—cukup pelajari sebagai ungkapan utuh!)*

| Expression (Ungkapan) | Meaning in English | Arti dalam Bahasa Indonesia | Example in Context (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Be that as it may** | Even if that is true / Nevertheless | Meskipun demikian / Biarpun begitu | *The plan is risky. **Be that as it may**, we must try.* | 
| **Come what may** | No matter what happens | Apa pun yang terjadi | ***Come what may**, I will always stand by you.* | 
| **Suffice it to say** | It is enough to say | Cukuplah dikatakan bahwa | ***Suffice it to say**, the meeting did not go well.* | 
| **God forbid** | I hope that never happens | Semoga saja tidak / Amit-amit | ***God forbid** anything bad happens during the flight.* | 
| **Long live...** | May (someone/something) live long | Hidup... / Jayalah... | ***Long live** the king! / **Long live** freedom!* | 
| **Far be it from me to...** | I certainly don't want to criticize | Jauh dari maksud saya untuk... | ***Far be it from me to** tell you how to do your job.* | 

## 5. Summary Cheat Sheet

**(Tabel Rangkuman Cepat)**

| Subjunctive Type (Jenis) | When to Use (Kapan Digunakan) | Verb Form (Bentuk Kata Kerja) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Present (Mandative)** | Demands, advice, urgency (`suggest, essential`) | **Base Verb (`V1` / `be`)** | *I suggest that she **go** now.* | 
| **Past Subjunctive** | Imaginary present (`if, wish, as if, high time`) | **`were`** (for *be*) or **`V2`** | *If I **were** rich, I'd travel.* | 
| **Past Perfect Subjunctive** | Past regrets & unreal past (`wish, if only, if`) | **`had + V3`** | *I wish I **had known** earlier.* | 
| **Formulaic Idioms** | Fixed traditional expressions | Frozen base forms | ***Come what may**, stay strong.* | 

## 6. Common Traps to Avoid

**(Jebakan Umum yang Harus Dihindari)**

* **Trap 1: Adding `-s` to verbs after `suggest`, `recommend`, or `insist`.**
  * ❌ *Incorrect:* She suggested that he **goes** to the doctor.
  * ✅ *Correct:* She suggested that he **go** to the doctor.
  *(Jangan tambahkan `-s` meskipun subjeknya tunggal he/she/it.)*

* **Trap 2: Using `don't` or `doesn't` in negative mandative subjunctives.**
  * ❌ *Incorrect:* It is important that you **don't** forget your ID. *(Informal)*
  * ✅ *Correct (Formal Subjunctive):* It is important that you **not** forget your ID.
  *(Dalam bentuk formal, letakkan `not` langsung sebelum kata kerja dasar tanpa `do/does`.)*

* **Trap 3: Confusing `suggest + that + Subject + V1` with `suggest + V-ing`.**
  * When there is **no `that` + new subject**, use `-ing`: *I suggest **going** early.*
  * When there **is `that` + subject**, use `V1`: *I suggest **that we go** early.*
  *(Jika tidak ada subjek baru setelah `suggest`, gunakan `V-ing`; jika ada `that + subjek`, gunakan `V1`.)*

## 7. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best word or phrase to complete each sentence. Try answering on your own before checking the answers below!

*(Pilih kata atau frasa yang paling tepat untuk melengkapi setiap kalimat. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum melihat kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. The committee demanded that the manager \_______\_ (resigns / resign / resigned) immediately.
   *(Komite menuntut agar manajer tersebut segera mengundurkan diri.)*

2. It is crucial that every passenger \_______\_ (be / is / are) seated before takeoff.
   *(Sangat penting bahwa setiap penumpang sudah duduk sebelum lepas landas.)*

3. If I \_______\_ (was / were / am) in charge of the project, I would hire two more designers.
   *(Seandainya saya yang bertanggung jawab atas proyek ini, saya akan merekrut dua desainer lagi.)*

4. The lawyer requested that the documents \_______\_ (not be / aren't / don't be) shared with the press.
   *(Pengacara tersebut meminta agar dokumen-dokumen itu tidak dibagikan kepada pers.)*

5. He spends money as though he \_______\_ (is / were / has been) a billionaire.
   *(Dia membelanjakan uang seolah-olah dia adalah seorang miliarder.)*

6. I wish I \_______\_ (didn't buy / hadn't bought / wouldn't buy) that expensive phone last month; now I'm broke.
   *(Saya menyesal membeli ponsel mahal itu bulan lalu; sekarang saya bangkrut.)*

7. It is high time the students \_______\_ (start / started / starting) preparing for their final exams.
   *(Sudah saatnya para siswa mulai mempersiapkan diri untuk ujian akhir mereka.)*

8. \_______\_ (Come / Comes / Came) what may, we are determined to finish this race together.
   *(Apa pun yang terjadi, kami bertekad untuk menyelesaikan perlombaan ini bersama-sama.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **resign**
   ➡️ *Present Subjunctive:* Kata pemicu `demanded that` mengharuskan kata kerja dasar (`V1`) tanpa `-s`.

2. **be**
   ➡️ *Present Subjunctive:* Kata sifat mendesak `crucial that` selalu diikuti bentuk dasar `be` (bukan `is`).

3. **were**
   ➡️ *Past Subjunctive:* Untuk pengandaian khayalan saat ini (`If I...`), selalu gunakan `were`.

4. **not be**
   ➡️ *Negative Passive Subjunctive:* Setelah `requested that`, bentuk negatif pasif menggunakan `not + be + V3` (`not be shared`).

5. **were**
   ➡️ *Past Subjunctive:* Setelah `as though` untuk menggambarkan keadaan yang tidak nyata saat ini, gunakan `were`.

6. **hadn't bought**
   ➡️ *Past Perfect Subjunctive:* Penyesalan tentang kejadian masa lalu (`last month`) membutuhkan `wish + had + V3`.

7. **started**
   ➡️ *Past Subjunctive:* Pola tetap `It is high time + Subject + V2` untuk menyatakan sesuatu yang seharusnya sudah dilakukan sekarang.

8. **Come**
   ➡️ *Formulaic Subjunctive:* Ungkapan idiom tetap **`Come what may`** berarti "apa pun yang terjadi".
