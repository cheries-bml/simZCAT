# Self-Study Guide: Mastering Adverbial Clauses of Concession

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Adverbial Clauses of Concession dalam Tata Bahasa Inggris)**

Have you ever wanted to say *"Even though it was raining, we still went for a walk"*—acknowledging an obstacle or surprising fact first, and then showing that the main action still happened anyway? That is what an **adverbial clause of concession** does!

*(Pernahkah kamu ingin mengatakan "Even though it was raining, we still went for a walk"—mengakui adanya hambatan atau fakta yang mengejutkan terlebih dahulu, lalu menunjukkan bahwa tindakan utama tetap terjadi? Itulah fungsi **adverbial clause of concession**!)*

In English, **concession** means admitting or accepting a fact or obstacle that would normally stop or change something, while still keeping your main point.

*(Dalam bahasa Inggris, **concession** berarti mengakui atau menerima suatu fakta atau hambatan yang biasanya menghalangi atau mengubah sesuatu, namun tetap mempertahankan pernyataan utama.)*

* *Normal Contrast:* It **rained** heavily, **but** we **went** hiking. *(Hujan turun deras, tetapi kami tetap pergi mendaki.)*

* ➡️ **Adverbial Clause of Concession:** **Although** it **rained** heavily, we **went** hiking. *(Meskipun hujan turun deras, kami tetap pergi mendaki.)*

## 1. The Core Idea: How Concession Clauses Work

**(Konsep Utama: Cara Kerja Adverbial Clauses of Concession)**

An **adverbial clause of concession** is a dependent clause (it has its own **Subject + Verb**, so it cannot stand alone as a full sentence) that starts with a subordinating conjunction like **`although`**, **`though`**, **`even though`**, **`while`**, or **`whereas`**.

*(**Adverbial clause of concession** adalah klausa terikat—memiliki **Subjek + Kata Kerja** sendiri sehingga tidak bisa berdiri sendiri sebagai kalimat lengkap—yang diawali kata hubung subordinatif seperti although, though, even though, while, atau whereas.)*

You can put the concession clause **at the beginning** or **at the end** of the sentence:

*(Kamu bisa meletakkan klausa konsesi **di awal** atau **di akhir** kalimat:)*

1. **Concession Clause First (Use a comma / Pakai koma):**
   ➡️ **Even though** she was exhausted, she finished the report.
   *(Meskipun dia sangat kelelahan, dia menyelesaikan laporan tersebut.)*

2. **Main Clause First (Usually no comma / Biasanya tanpa koma):**
   ➡️ She finished the report **even though** she was exhausted.
   *(Dia menyelesaikan laporan tersebut meskipun dia sangat kelelahan.)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Check what comes **right after** the connecting word!
>
> * **`Although / Even though / Though` + Subject + Verb (Clause)** ➡️ *Even though **he practiced** daily...*
>
> * **`Despite / In spite of` + Noun / Pronoun / `V-ing` (Phrase, NOT a full clause!)** ➡️ *Despite **practicing** daily...*
>   *(Periksa apa yang muncul **tepat setelah** kata penghubungnya! `Although/Even though/Though` diikuti **Subjek + Verb**, sedangkan `Despite/In spite of` diikuti **kata benda / pronoun / V-ing**.)*

## 2. Main Words & Patterns for Clauses of Concession

**(Kata Kunci & Pola Utama Adverbial Clauses of Concession)**

### Pattern 1: `Although`, `Even Though`, and `Though` (Full Clauses)

All three introduce a **subject + verb** clause showing an unexpected result or contrast.

*(Ketiganya mengawali klausa **subjek + kata kerja** untuk menunjukkan hasil yang tidak terduga atau kontras.)*

* **`Although` & `Even though`:** Neutral to formal; **`even though`** is extra strong (*"meskipun kenyataannya..."*).
  *(Netral hingga formal; `even though` terasa lebih tegas.)*

* **`Though`:** Very common in everyday speaking and writing. At the **very end** of a sentence, `though` can also mean *"however / anyway"*.
  *(Sangat umum dalam percakapan dan tulisan sehari-hari. Di **akhir kalimat**, `though` juga bisa berarti "namun / tapi".)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `Although / Even though / Though + Subject + Verb, + Main Clause`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **With `Although`:**
   ➡️ **Although** the restaurant was crowded, the service stayed fast.
   *(Meskipun restorannya ramai, pelayanannya tetap cepat.)*

2. **With `Even though` (Stronger Emphasis):**
   ➡️ **Even though** he didn't speak the local language, he made friends easily.
   *(Meskipun dia tidak bisa berbahasa setempat, dia mudah berteman.)*

3. **With `Though` at the Start or Middle:**
   ➡️ **Though** the price was high, they bought the apartment.
   *(Meskipun harganya tinggi, mereka membeli apartemen itu.)*

4. **`Though` at the End of a Sentence ("However"):**
   ➡️ The movie was a bit slow. **It was still worth watching, though.**
   *(Filmnya agak lambat. Namun, tetap layak ditonton.)*

### Pattern 2: `While` and `Whereas` (Direct Contrast Between Two Facts)

Use **`while`** or **`whereas`** when comparing **two contrasting facts, people, or situations** side by side (often like *"sedangkan"* in Bahasa Indonesia).

*(Gunakan **`while`** atau **`whereas`** saat membandingkan **dua fakta, orang, atau situasi yang bertolak belakang** secara berdampingan—sering kali seperti "sedangkan" dalam bahasa Indonesia.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `While / Whereas + Subject A + Verb A, + Subject B + Verb B`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **With `While`:**
   ➡️ **While** I prefer quiet mornings, my brother loves busy nights.
   *(Sementara saya lebih suka pagi yang tenang, saudara laki-laki saya menyukai malam yang ramai.)*

2. **With `Whereas` (Slightly More Formal / Analytical):**
   ➡️ **Whereas** solar energy is clean and renewable, coal power causes pollution.
   *(Sedangkan energi surya bersih dan terbarukan, pembangkit batu bara menimbulkan polusi.)*

3. **In the Middle of a Sentence:**
   ➡️ Rina enjoys traveling abroad, **whereas** Tono likes staying in his hometown.
   *(Rina senang bepergian ke luar negeri, sedangkan Tono suka tinggal di kampung halamannya.)*

### Pattern 3: `No Matter How / What / Who / Where / When` & `-Ever` Words

Use these to mean **"regardless of how/what/who/where/when..."**—showing that the main action still happens under any condition or degree.

*(Gunakan pola ini untuk berarti **"tidak peduli betapa/apa/siapa/di mana/kapan..."**—menunjukkan bahwa tindakan utama tetap terjadi dalam kondisi atau tingkatan apa pun.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `No matter how + Adjective/Adverb + Subject + Verb, + Main Clause`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **`No matter how + Adjective/Adverb`:**
   ➡️ **No matter how** difficult the task is, we will finish it on time.
   *(Tidak peduli seberapa sulit tugasnya, kita akan menyelesaikannya tepat waktu.)*

2. **`No matter what`:**
   ➡️ **No matter what** happens tomorrow, I will support your decision.
   *(Apa pun yang terjadi besok, saya akan mendukung keputusanmu.)*

3. **Alternative with `However` (`However + Adj/Adv + S + V`):**
   ➡️ **However** tired she feels after work, she always exercises for twenty minutes.
   *(Seberapa pun lelahnya dia setelah bekerja, dia selalu berolahraga selama dua puluh menit.)*

### Pattern 4: Reduced Concession Clauses (`Adjective / Participle + though/as`)

In formal or expressive English, you can shorten a concession clause by moving the **adjective or participle** to the front before **`though`** or **`as`**.

*(Dalam bahasa Inggris yang formal atau ekspresif, kamu bisa mempersingkat klausa konsesi dengan memindahkan **kata sifat atau participle** ke depan sebelum **`though`** atau **`as`**.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `Adjective / V-ing / V3 + though / as + Subject + Verb, + Main Clause`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ **Tired as** he was, he drove us safely to the hotel.
   *(Meskipun dia lelah, dia mengantar kami dengan selamat ke hotel.)*

2. ➡️ **Surprised though** they were, they welcomed the unexpected guests warmly.
   *(Meskipun mereka terkejut, mereka menyambut tamu tak terduga itu dengan hangat.)*

## 3. Summary Cheat Sheet

**(Tabel Rangkuman Cepat)**

| Concession Form (Bentuk) | Followed By (Diikuti Oleh) | Main Function (Fungsi Utama) | Example (Contoh) | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **`Although` / `Even though` / `Though`** | `Subject + Verb` *(Clause)* | Unexpected result despite an obstacle *(Hasil tak terduga meski ada hambatan)* | ***Even though** it hurt, he smiled.* | 
| **`While` / `Whereas`** | `Subject + Verb` *(Clause)* | Direct contrast between two sides *(Kontras langsung dua hal)* | ***Whereas** tea is mild, espresso is strong.* | 
| **`No matter how / what` / `However`** | `Adj/Adv + Subject + Verb` | Regardless of degree or situation *(Tidak peduli tingkat atau situasinya)* | ***No matter how** late it gets, call me.* | 
| **`Adj/Participle + though/as`** | `Subject + Verb` | Concise, emphatic contrast *(Kontras yang ringkas & kuat)* | ***Young though** she is, she leads well.* | 

## 4. Crucial Traps to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using `but` in the same sentence as `although` or `even though`.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai `but` dalam kalimat yang sama dengan `although` atau `even though`.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* **Although** it was raining, **but** we went out.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* **Although** it was raining, we went out. *(or: It was raining, **but** we went out.)*
    *(Pilih salah satu saja—jangan memakai `although` dan `but` sekaligus dalam satu kalimat.)*

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `although` or `even though` directly before a noun phrase without a verb.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai `although` atau `even though` langsung di depan frasa kata benda tanpa kata kerja.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* **Although** the heavy traffic, we arrived on time.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* **Despite** the heavy traffic, we arrived on time. *(or: **Although** the traffic was heavy, we arrived on time.)*

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Putting a verb immediately after `No matter how` instead of an adjective/adverb + subject.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Meletakkan kata kerja tepat setelah `No matter how` alih-alih kata sifat/keterangan + subjek.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* **No matter how** is hard the exam...

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* **No matter how** hard the exam **is**...

## 5. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option to complete each sentence. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi yang paling tepat untuk melengkapi setiap kalimat. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. \______________\_ (Although / Despite / Whereas) the road was slippery, the driver stayed calm.
   *(Meskipun jalannya licin, pengemudi itu tetap tenang.)*

2. \______________\_ (Whereas / Even though / No matter what) city life is fast-paced, rural life is much quieter.
   *(Sedangkan kehidupan kota serba cepat, kehidupan desa jauh lebih tenang.)*

3. No matter \______________\_ (how / what / who) tired you feel tonight, please back up your files first.
   *(Tidak peduli seberapa lelah kamu merasa malam ini, harap cadangkan berkasmu terlebih dahulu.)*

4. Which sentence is **grammatically correct**?
   *(Kalimat manakah yang **secara tata bahasa benar**?)*

   * A) Although he was sick, but he came to the meeting.

   * B) Although he was sick, he came to the meeting.

   * C) Although his sickness, he came to the meeting.

5. \______________\_ (Small though / Though small / Small as though) the room was, it felt cozy and bright.
   *(Meskipun ruangannya kecil, ruangan itu terasa nyaman dan terang.)*

6. She agreed to help us \______________\_ (even though / whereas / no matter how) she had a packed schedule.
   *(Dia setuju membantu kami meskipun jadwalnya sangat padat.)*

7. \______________\_ (Whereas / Despite / However) hard they tried, they couldn't solve the riddle.
   *(Seberapa pun keras mereka berusaha, mereka tidak bisa memecahkan teka-teki itu.)*

8. The test was quite challenging. We passed it, \______________\_ (whereas / though / even though).
   *(Ujiannya cukup menantang. Namun, kami lulus.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **Although**
   ➡️ Diikuti klausa lengkap (`the road was slippery` ➡️ subjek + verb), sehingga memakai `Although` (bukan `Despite`).

2. **Whereas**
   ➡️ Membandingkan dua hal yang bertolak belakang (`city life` vs. `rural life`).

3. **how**
   ➡️ Pola `No matter how + adjective (tired) + subject (you) + verb (feel)`.

4. **B) Although he was sick, he came to the meeting.**
   ➡️ Tidak menggabungkan `Although` dengan `but` (opsi A salah) dan memakai klausa subjek-verb setelah `Although` (opsi C salah).

5. **Small though** *(or `Though small`)*
   ➡️ Bentuk ringkas/penekanan dengan kata sifat `Small` di depan `though` (`Small though the room was...`).

6. **even though**
   ➡️ Menunjukkan hasil yang mengejutkan (`agreed to help us`) meskipun ada hambatan (`had a packed schedule`).

7. **However**
   ➡️ Pola `However + adverb (hard) + subject (they) + verb (tried)` berarti "seberapa pun keras...".

8. **though**
   ➡️ Di akhir kalimat, `though` berfungsi sebagai kata keterangan yang berarti "namun / tapi".
