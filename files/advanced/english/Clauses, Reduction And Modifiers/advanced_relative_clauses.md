# Self-Study Guide: Mastering Advanced Relative Clauses

**(Panduan Belajar Mandiri: Menguasai Advanced Relative Clauses dalam Tata Bahasa Inggris)**

You probably already know basic relative clauses like *"The book **that I bought** is interesting."* **Advanced relative clauses** go a step further: they help you add extra comments about an entire idea, use prepositions smoothly in formal writing, reduce long clauses into shorter phrases, and use words like *whose, whom, whichever, whoever,* or *whereby*.

*(Kamu mungkin sudah mengenal relative clauses dasar seperti "The book **that I bought** is interesting." **Advanced relative clauses** melangkah lebih jauh: pola ini membantu kamu menambahkan komentar tentang keseluruhan gagasan, menggunakan preposisi secara luwes dalam tulisan formal, mempersingkat klausa panjang menjadi frasa ringkas, serta memakai kata seperti whose, whom, whichever, whoever, atau whereby.)*

Learning these patterns will make your English sound much more natural, connected, and mature—both when reading articles and when writing essays or emails.

*(Mempelajari pola-pola ini akan membuat bahasa Inggris kamu terdengar jauh lebih alami, terhubung, dan matang—baik saat membaca artikel maupun saat menulis esai atau email.)*

## 1. The Core Idea: How Advanced Relative Clauses Work

**(Konsep Utama: Cara Kerja Advanced Relative Clauses)**

A relative clause gives extra information about a noun or clause without starting a brand-new sentence. In **advanced** relative clauses, pay special attention to three things:

*(Relative clause memberikan informasi tambahan tentang kata benda atau klausa tanpa harus memulai kalimat baru. Dalam **advanced** relative clauses, perhatikan tiga hal penting berikut:)*

1. **Scope (Cakupan):** Does the clause describe **one noun**, or does it comment on **the whole previous clause**?
   *(Apakah klausa tersebut menjelaskan **satu kata benda**, atau mengomentari **seluruh klausa sebelumnya**?)*

2. **Formality & Prepositions (Keformalan & Preposisi):** Does the preposition stay **at the end** (informal/neutral) or move **before `whom` / `which`** (formal)?
   *(Apakah preposisi diletakkan **di akhir** \[informal/netral\] atau dipindahkan **ke depan `whom` / `which`** \[formal\]?)*

3. **Reduction (Peringkasan):** Can the relative clause be shortened into a **participle** or **adjective/prepositional phrase**?
   *(Bisakah relative clause dipersingkat menjadi **participle** atau **frasa kata sifat/preposisi**?)*

> 💡 **Quick Self-Check Tip (Trik Cek Mandiri Cepat):**
> Always check whether there is a **comma** before the relative clause!
>
> * **No comma (Defining):** Essential info that identifies *which one* (*The students **who arrived late** missed the quiz*).
>
> * **With a comma (Non-defining):** Extra info that can be removed without changing the core identity (*My brother, **who lives in Surabaya**, is visiting this week*).
>   *(Selalu periksa apakah ada **koma** sebelum relative clause! Tanpa koma berarti informasi inti yang membatasi; dengan koma berarti informasi tambahan.)*

## 2. Key Patterns of Advanced Relative Clauses with Examples

**(Pola-Pola Utama Advanced Relative Clauses beserta Contohnya)**

### Pattern 1: Sentential Relative Clauses (Mengomentari Seluruh Klausa dengan `, which`)

Use **`, which`** (with a comma!) when you want to comment on **the entire situation or fact** mentioned in the earlier clause—not just the last noun. You **cannot** use `that` or `what` here.

*(Gunakan **`, which`**—dengan koma!—ketika kamu ingin mengomentari **keseluruhan situasi atau fakta** yang disebutkan pada klausa sebelumnya, bukan hanya kata benda terakhir. Kamu **tidak boleh** memakai `that` atau `what` di sini.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `[Full Clause], which + Verb...`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ She finished the marathon in under four hours, **which** amazed everyone.
   *(Dia menyelesaikan maraton dalam waktu kurang dari empat jam, **yang mana hal itu** membuat semua orang takjub.)*
   *Note:* `, which` refers to *finishing the marathon in under four hours*, not just "four hours".

2. ➡️ The train was delayed for two hours, **which** caused us to miss the opening speech.
   *(Kereta terlambat selama dua jam, **yang menyebabkan** kami ketinggalan pidato pembukaan.)*

3. ➡️ He apologized sincerely, **which** helped calm the situation down.
   *(Dia meminta maaf dengan tulus, **yang membantu** meredakan situasi.)*

### Pattern 2: Prepositions + `Whom` / `Which` (Preposisi di Depan `Whom` atau `Which`)

When a relative pronoun is the object of a preposition (like *in, with, for, about, to, by*), you have two choices:

*(Ketika kata ganti relatif menjadi objek dari preposisi seperti in, with, for, about, to, atau by, kamu punya dua pilihan:)*

* **Formal (Preposition before `whom` / `which`):** `Preposition + whom` (for people) / `Preposition + which` (for things). **Never** use `who` or `that` right after a preposition!
  *(Formal: Preposisi diletakkan **sebelum `whom` atau `which`**. Jangan pernah memakai `who` atau `that` tepat setelah preposisi!)*

* **Everyday / Neutral (Preposition at the end):** You can leave the preposition at the end and often drop `who/which/that` in defining clauses.
  *(Sehari-hari / Netral: Preposisi diletakkan di akhir kalimat.)*

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. **For People (`prep + whom`):**

   * *Neutral:* The mentor **whom** I learned **from** gave great advice. *(or: The mentor I learned from...)*

   * ➡️ **Formal:** The mentor **from whom** I learned gave great advice.
     *(Pembimbing tempat saya belajar memberikan saran yang sangat bagus.)*

2. **For Things (`prep + which`):**

   * *Neutral:* The company **which** they invested **in** grew rapidly.

   * ➡️ **Formal:** The company **in which** they invested grew rapidly.
     *(Perusahaan tempat mereka berinvestasi berkembang pesat.)*

3. **With `by which` / `at which`:**

   * ➡️ The method **by which** we solved the problem is simple and fast.
     *(Metode yang kami gunakan untuk menyelesaikan masalah itu sederhana dan cepat.)*

### Pattern 3: Quantifiers + `Of Whom` / `Of Which` / `Of Whose`

Very useful in formal, academic, and business writing when talking about **part of a group** (e.g., *most of, all of, many of, neither of, one of, each of, several of*).

*(Sangat berguna dalam tulisan formal, akademis, dan bisnis saat membicarakan **sebagian dari suatu kelompok**—seperti most of, all of, many of, neither of, one of, each of, atau several of.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `[Quantifier] + of whom / of which / of whose + ...`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ There were fifty applicants, **many of whom** had international experience.
   *(Ada lima puluh pelamar, **yang banyak di antaranya** memiliki pengalaman internasional.)*

2. ➡️ She bought three reference books, **none of which** were expensive.
   *(Dia membeli tiga buku referensi, **yang tidak satu pun di antaranya** mahal.)*

3. ➡️ The committee reviewed four proposals, **each of which** had clear strengths.
   *(Komite meninjau empat proposal, **yang masing-masing di antaranya** memiliki kelebihan yang jelas.)*

4. ➡️ We interviewed several candidates, **one of whom** will be hired next week.
   *(Kami mewawancarai beberapa kandidat, **yang salah satu di antaranya** akan direkrut minggu depan.)*

### Pattern 4: `Whose` for Things & Possession (`Whose` untuk Kepemilikan Orang & Benda)

We often learn `whose` for people (*a writer whose books...*), but in modern English, **`whose`** is also commonly used for **things, places, or organizations** as a smoother alternative to *of which*.

*(Kita sering mempelajari `whose` untuk orang, tetapi dalam bahasa Inggris modern, **`whose`** juga umum dipakai untuk **benda, tempat, atau organisasi** sebagai alternatif yang lebih luwes daripada of which.)*

* **Formula (Rumus):** `Noun + whose + [Owned Noun] + Verb...`

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ I stayed at a small hotel **whose** view overlooked the sea.
   *(Saya menginap di sebuah hotel kecil **yang pemandangannya** menghadap ke laut.)*

2. ➡️ They launched an app **whose** main goal is helping students manage time.
   *(Mereka meluncurkan aplikasi **yang tujuan utamanya** membantu siswa mengatur waktu.)*

3. ➡️ The student **whose** project won first prize gave a short speech.
   *(Siswa **yang proyeknya** meraih juara pertama menyampaikan pidato singkat.)*

### Pattern 5: `-Ever` Relative Pronouns (`Whoever`, `Whatever`, `Whichever`, `Wherever`, `Whenever`)

These mean **"any person/thing/place/time that..."** or **"no matter who/what/which/where/when..."**.

*(Kata-kata ini berarti **"siapa pun / apa pun / mana pun / ke mana pun / kapan pun yang..."** atau **"tidak peduli siapa/apa/mana/di mana/kapan..."**.)*

#### Examples (Contoh Kalimat)

1. ➡️ **Whoever** finishes the task first can leave early.
   *(**Siapa pun yang** menyelesaikan tugas lebih dulu boleh pulang lebih awal.)*

2. ➡️ Take **whichever** seat you prefer.
   *(Ambillah kursi **mana pun yang** kamu sukai.)*

3. ➡️ **Whatever** happens, stay calm and think clearly.
   *(**Apa pun yang** terjadi, tetaplah tenang dan berpikir jernih.)*

4. ➡️ You can reach me **whenever** you need help.
   *(Kamu bisa menghubungi saya **kapan pun** kamu butuh bantuan.)*

### Pattern 6: Reduced Relative Clauses (Mempersingkat Relative Clause)

When a defining relative clause has a **continuous (`be + V-ing`)**, **passive (`be + V3`)**, or **be + adjective/prepositional phrase**, you can often drop the relative pronoun + `be` to make the sentence shorter and cleaner.

*(Ketika defining relative clause memiliki bentuk **continuous (`be + V-ing`)**, **pasif (`be + V3`)**, atau **be + kata sifat/frasa preposisi**, kamu sering bisa menghilangkan kata ganti relatif + `be` agar kalimat lebih singkat dan rapi.)*

* ➡️ **Active (`who/which is/are + V-ing` ➡️ `V-ing`):**
  The people **waiting** outside are here for the interview. *(The people **who are waiting** outside...)*
  *(Orang-orang **yang sedang menunggu** di luar datang untuk wawancara.)*

* ➡️ **Passive (`which/that was/were + V3` ➡️ `V3`):**
  The messages **sent** this morning need a reply today. *(The messages **which were sent**...)*
  *(Pesan-pesan **yang dikirim** pagi ini perlu dibalas hari ini.)*

* ➡️ **Prepositional / Adjective phrase (`who/that is + phrase` ➡️ `phrase`):**
  The students **in the back row** couldn't hear the speaker. *(The students **who were in the back row**...)*
  *(Para siswa **yang berada di baris belakang** tidak bisa mendengar pembicaranya.)*

| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Sentential `, which`** | `[Clause], which + Verb` | Commenting on the whole clause *(Mengomentari seluruh kalimat)* | *He arrived late, **which** annoyed us.* |

## 4. Crucial Traps to Avoid

**(Jebakan Penting yang Harus Dihindari)**

* ⚠️ **Trap 1: Using `, that` instead of `, which` for a whole clause or after a comma.**
  *(Jebakan 1: Memakai `, that` alih-alih `, which` untuk mengomentari seluruh klausa atau setelah koma.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* He passed the exam with top marks, **that** made his parents proud.

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* He passed the exam with top marks, **which** made his parents proud.

* ⚠️ **Trap 2: Using `who` or `that` directly after a preposition.**
  *(Jebakan 2: Memakai `who` atau `that` tepat setelah preposisi.)*

  * ❌ *Incorrect (Salah):* The colleague **with who** I worked... / The house **in that** I grew up...

  * ✅ *Correct (Benar):* The colleague **with whom** I worked... / The house **in which** I grew up...

* ⚠️ **Trap 3: Confusing `who's` and `whose`.**
  *(Jebakan 3: Tertukar antara `who's` dan `whose`.)*

  * **`who's`** = *who is* or *who has* ➡️ *Do you know **who's** coming?* *(Apakah kamu tahu **siapa yang** akan datang?)*

  * **`whose`** = possessive (*milik siapa / yang ...-nya*) ➡️ *That is the artist **whose** paintings are famous.* *(Itu seniman **yang lukisannya** terkenal.)*

## 5. Self-Study Practice Quiz

**(Kuis Latihan Mandiri)**

Choose the best option to complete each sentence. Try answering on your own before checking the key below!

*(Pilih opsi yang paling tepat untuk melengkapi setiap kalimat. Cobalah menjawab sendiri sebelum memeriksa kunci jawaban di bawah!)*

1. She offered to help us move the boxes, \______________\_ (which / that / what) was very kind of her.
   *(Dia menawarkan diri membantu kami memindahkan kotak-kotak itu, yang sangat baik darinya.)*

2. The researcher \______________\_ (from who / from whom / whom from) we received the data has published several papers.
   *(Peneliti yang datanya kami terima telah menerbitkan beberapa makalah.)*

3. They tested ten new batteries, \______________\_ (most of them / most of which / most of whom) lasted over twelve hours.
   *(Mereka menguji sepuluh baterai baru, yang sebagian besar di antaranya bertahan lebih dari dua belas jam.)*

4. We visited a historic library \______________\_ (whose / who's / which) ceiling was painted in the 1800s.
   *(Kami mengunjungi perpustakaan bersejarah yang langit-langitnya dilukis pada tahun 1800-an.)*

5. \______________\_ (Whoever / Whichever / Whatever) arrives at the station first should save two seats.
   *(Siapa pun yang tiba di stasiun lebih dulu sebaiknya mengamankan dua kursi.)*

6. The documents \______________\_ (signing / signed / which signed) by the director are ready for delivery.
   *(Dokumen-dokumen yang ditandatangani oleh direktur sudah siap dikirim.)*

7. The conference hall \______________\_ (in which / in who / which in) the seminar took place can hold 500 people.
   *(Aula konferensi tempat seminar berlangsung dapat menampung 500 orang.)*

8. There are four candidates on the shortlist, \______________\_ (neither of which / none of whom / both of whom) has prior management experience.
   *(Ada empat kandidat dalam daftar pendek, yang tidak satu pun di antara mereka memiliki pengalaman manajemen sebelumnya.)*

### Answer Key & Explanations

**(Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan)**

1. **which**
   ➡️ *Sentential Relative Clause:* Menggunakan `, which` (bukan `that`) untuk mengomentari keseluruhan tindakan pada klausa sebelumnya.

2. **from whom**
   ➡️ *Preposition + `whom`:* Setelah preposisi `from` dan mengacu pada orang (`the researcher`), gunakan `whom` (bukan `who`).

3. **most of which**
   ➡️ *Quantifier + `of which`:* Mengacu pada benda jamak (`ten new batteries`), sehingga memakai `most of which`.

4. **whose**
   ➡️ *`Whose` for Things:* Menunjukkan kepemilikan benda (`the library's ceiling` ➡️ `whose ceiling`).

5. **Whoever**
   ➡️ *`-Ever` Pronoun for People:* Berarti "siapa pun yang" sebagai subjek kalimat.

6. **signed**
   ➡️ *Reduced Passive Relative Clause:* Singkatan dari *The documents **which were signed** by the director...*.

7. **in which**
   ➡️ *Preposition + `which`:* Mengacu pada tempat (`the conference hall`) secara formal dengan preposisi `in` di depan `which`.

8. **none of whom**
   ➡️ *Quantifier + `of whom`:* Mengacu pada orang jamak (`four candidates`), dan `none` digunakan untuk kelompok lebih dari dua.
